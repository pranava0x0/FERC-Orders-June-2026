## 5. Recurring causes

1. Scheduled runs depend on a permission prompt a human must answer; unattended runs die at the first Bash call.
2. Verification that ends in a green exit code but does not enumerate its own coverage (quote sweep skipped track cards; translated transcripts matched exactly; 183 tests with no CI).
3. Deferral of the highest-yield check (applicant search) to the next sweep, three times.
4. Search agents used for facts and framing; they have returned usable leads at most about one in five attempts and the review always re-fetched.
5. Review findings in `refresh.sh` and `sheet-baseline.json` (PR #32, #34) repeat because the registration step is a prose instruction.

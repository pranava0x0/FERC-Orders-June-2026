ER27-508-000 · 2026-11-16 · Southern California Edison Company

SCE OSC - Large Load, TOT Revisions

Southern California Edison files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in ER27-508-000, answering the CAISO show cause order. A new Appendix XV sets large load interconnection procedures, with a study deposit, a 15-year minimum revenue obligation backed by security, exit fees and a curtailable interim service. More than 250 pages of tariff sheets follow the letter.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions as filed, effective February 16, 2027, and rescind the show cause order as to SCE's tariff.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-10 show cause order for SCE's own transmission owner tariff. SCE says it covers the application, cost shifting and flexible service items and expects other owners' tariffs to differ.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Cost-recovery agreement (§ III.B.3, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Service for flexible loads (§ III.D, p. 62)

Key points

  1. SCE takes CAISO's 50 MW definition into its tariff.
    SCE proposes to incorporate the CAISO’s definition of “Large Load” into its TO Tariff PDF p. 7
  2. A new appendix holds the large load interconnection procedures.
    SCE proposes to create a new Appendix titled the Large Load Interconnection Procedures (“LLIP”) to augment existing TO Tariff interconnection and cost responsibility sections PDF p. 5
  3. A request needs a $250,000 study deposit and a readiness deposit of twice that.
    an Interconnection Study Deposit of $250,000, a Commercial Readiness Deposit equal to two times the study deposit PDF p. 10
  4. The same transmission rules apply to SCE's own retail large load customers.
    SCE’s LLIP thus applies the same transmission-related requirements to SCE’s own Large Load retail transmission customers as it does to Eligible Customers seeking to interconnect on behalf of Large Loads PDF p. 9
  5. A 15-year minimum revenue obligation backed by five years of security protects against stranded network upgrade costs.
    a 15-year minimum transmission revenue obligation backed by 5 years of security PDF p. 22
  6. Early termination after service starts costs the customer all remaining required security.
    early termination triggers an Exit Fee equal to all remaining required security, including the MARO security, due within 30 Business Days PDF p. 24
  7. Curtailment terms for the interim service sit in an appendix to the security and reliability agreement.
    the conditions and limitations applicable to FILI service, including curtailments, are specified in Appendix E to the TSRA PDF p. 17
  8. SCE says the California owners should not be expected to file uniform tariffs.
    SCE believes uniformity in the PTOs’ tariffs should not be expected PDF p. 3

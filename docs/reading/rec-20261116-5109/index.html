ER27-500-000 · 2026-11-16 · Citizens Sycamore-Penasquitos Transmission LLC

Tariff Revisions to Address Commission's ER26-71 Order to Show Cause

Citizens Sycamore-Penasquitos Transmission LLC files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in ER27-500-000, answering the CAISO show cause order. It leases part of an SDG&E line, so its revisions say SDG&E's tariff governs study, interconnection and expansion requests. The operative text matches the Citizens Sunrise filing; only the project facts differ.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective January 15, 2027, and find that Citizens Sycamore has satisfied the concerns in the show cause order.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-10 show cause order for CAISO and its transmission owners. It sends large load study and interconnection matters to SDG&E's tariff rather than setting up a process of its own.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25)

Key points

  1. Citizens Sycamore owns none of the line. SDG&E keeps ownership, operation and maintenance.
    Citizens Sycamore does not own any of the actual facilities. SDG&E retains ownership and operation and maintenance responsibilities. PDF p. 2
  2. Its interest is a lease of under 13 percent of one segment of the project.
    Citizens Sycamore has a limited, less than 13% leasehold interest in a single segment of the overall Sycamore Project transmission line PDF p. 3
  3. It is a non-load-serving transmission owner with no end-use customers.
    Non-Load- Serving Participating TO and has no End-Use Customers PDF p. 2
  4. Study, planning, interconnection and expansion requests would be governed by SDG&E's tariff.
    any study, planning, interconnection or expansion requests related to Citizens Sycamore’s interest in the Sycamore Project will be governed by the relevant provisions of the SDG&E TO Tariff PDF p. 3
  5. New language in Sections 8, 9 and 10 repeats the point, one section at a time.
    Added new language to Section 10, Interconnection Process, to confirm that all conduct that falls under Section 10 will be governed by SDG&E’s TO Tariff PDF p. 4
  6. Definitions are revised to say SDG&E's tariff provisions govern.
    each to clarify that SDG&E’s TO Tariff provisions will govern PDF p. 4
  7. It commits to a compliance filing if CAISO or SDG&E changes its tariff in a way that affects these revisions.
    Citizens Sycamore commits to making a compliance filing to ensure its TO Tariff remains consistent PDF p. 5
  8. It asks for an effective date 60 days after filing.
    accept these TO Tariff revisions to become effective January 15, 2027, which is sixty (60) days from the date of this filing PDF p. 1

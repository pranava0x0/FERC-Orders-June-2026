III. COMMENTS IN SUPPORT OF THE PJM IRAS FILING
In general, NRG supports PJM’s IRAS Filing. PJM proposes to require Load Serving Entities (“LSEs”) to “bring new capacity in an amount that equals or exceeds its New Large Loads’ peak load or be subject to load reduction requests during Emergencies prior to Pre- Emergency Load Management Response.”9 PJM notes that it is “currently short of capacity and looking to reestablish healthy capacity reserve margin.”10 PJM’s approach is commonsense: New Large Loads must be supported by Capacity Resources.

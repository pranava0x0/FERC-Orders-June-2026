ER27-529-000 · 2026-11-16 · New York Independent System Operator, Inc., Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation and 5 others

NYTOs 205: Proposed Revisions re: Integration of Large Loads

Six New York transmission owners file under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in ER27-529-000, with NYISO submitting it as tariff administrator. They add four pro forma agreements: cost recovery, security, facilities study and interconnection. The cost recovery agreement recovers 100 percent of allocated upgrade costs over 15 years from the large load customer.

Asks FERC to: Accept the tariff amendments and pro forma agreements as just and reasonable, mostly effective February 1, 2027, with waiver of the 120-day filing limit.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-12 show cause order for NYISO, as the owners' companion to NYISO's own filing in ER27-517. It covers cost recovery, security and operating terms, which NYISO's filing leaves to the owners.

E-12 NYISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-12 NYISO: Cost transparency (P 73, p. 47)

Key points

  1. NYISO's filing sets the study process and services. The owners' filing covers cost recovery and operations.
    While the NYISO’s filing establishes the study process, readiness requirements, and new Firm and Non-Firm Withdrawal Services for Large Loads, the NYTOs’ filing addresses the complementary elements necessary to recover the costs of facilities built to serve Large Loads PDF p. 2
  2. Each owner recovers all allocated upgrade and facility costs from the large load customer.
    recover 100% of the costs of Network Upgrades and Attachment Facilities allocated to a Large Load Customer PDF p. 3
  3. Recovery runs over 15 years, without accelerated depreciation.
    that compresses cost recovery into a 15-year term without accelerated depreciation PDF p. 3
  4. The customer posts security that the owner can draw on if the project is abandoned or the customer defaults.
    requires the Large Load Customer to post security for those allocated costs, which the Transmission Owner may draw upon in the event of abandonment of a Large Load project, default, or other specified events PDF p. 3
  5. Non-firm withdrawal service would be offered only where thermal or resource adequacy limits rule out firm service.
    limit Non-Firm Withdrawal Service to thermal and resource adequacy constraints PDF p. 3
  6. The owners propose no new delivery rates. Section 5 keeps pointing to state-approved rates.
    Section 5 will continue to reference the voltage-differentiated delivery rates and service classifications approved by the New York Public Service Commission PDF p. 3
  7. The owners will contract with the large load customer directly, not with energy service companies or other intermediaries.
    They do not volunteer to enter into Facilities Study, Security, Cost Recovery, and Interconnection Agreements with ESCOs or other intermediaries of Large Load Customers PDF p. 13
  8. Most revisions would take effect February 1, 2027, matching NYISO's filing.
    request an effective date for the revisions to Attachment LL and Attachment B of Section 8 of the NYISO OATT of February 1, 2027 PDF p. 34
  9. The owners' agreement for facilities studies sets fixed deposits and a schedule inside NYISO's study timeline.
    a pro forma Facilities Study Agreement provides a uniform framework, fixed study deposits, and a defined schedule for the Facilities Studies the Transmission Owners perform under Attachment LL PDF p. 3
  10. The owners add operating requirements for large loads paired with co-located or partner generation.
    operating requirements that apply to Large Loads interconnected under the LLIP, including Large Loads that take Non-Firm Transmission Withdrawal Service at a level below their maximum potential withdrawal and Large Loads paired with Co-Located or Partner Resources PDF p. 27

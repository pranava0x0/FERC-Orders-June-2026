E. The collateral requirement is unjust and unreasonable because it is duplicative, excessive, and the initial credit rate is insupportably high. The collateral requirement should be revised to allow other forms of collateral.

a. The collateral requirement is subjective, unduly discriminatory, and unjust and unreasonable because it is excessive.

PJM’s proposed collateral requirement is not just and reasonable. PJM asserts that its methodology strikes an appropriate balance between protecting PJM’s markets and ensuring market participants have adequate flexibility to manage their collateral requirements.55 PJM notes that the Commission has cautioned against overly conservative credit requirements that are poorly correlated to credit risk that would impose costs.56 PJM’s methodology does not strike that balance.

CAISO files large-load tariff revisions under section 205 in response to the show cause order

CAISO files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in a new docket, ER27-506-000, as its response to the show cause order. It defines a large load as 50 MW or more, makes generators disclose plans to serve co-located load, and adds a Flexible Interim Load Interconnection (FILI) service. Deposits, security and cost recovery sit in the transmission owners' own tariffs, filed separately the same day.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective February 16, 2027, with scheduling coordinator changes on November 1, 2027 and settlement changes on April 30, 2028, and set a 30-day comment period.

Answers the order. Filed under section 205 to answer the E-10 show cause order for CAISO. The California transmission owners filed their own tariff revisions in separate dockets the same day.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Cost-recovery agreement (§ III.B.3, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Service for flexible loads (§ III.D, p. 62) E-10 CAISO: BTMG netting (pp. 60-61, p. 60)

Key points

  1. CAISO says its revisions, with the transmission owners', answer the show cause order.
    Taken together, the CAISO and Participating Transmission Owner (PTO) tariff revisions supply the tariff-level clarity, customer protections, and operational tools the Order seeks. PDF p. 1
  2. A large load is a single site of 50 MW or more.
    and that has a peak load of 50 MW or greater. PDF p. 11
  3. Deposits, security and cost recovery are left to each transmission owner's tariff.
    the PTOs will implement just and reasonable deposit, security, reimbursement, and cost-recovery provisions in their own tariffs PDF p. 3
  4. Generator interconnection customers must disclose plans to serve co-located load.
    requires generator interconnection customers to disclose plans to serve co- located load PDF p. 3
  5. FILI would let flexible loads energize before network upgrades are finished.
    The CAISO’s Flexible Interim Load Interconnection (FILI) can permit earlier energization while network upgrades are pending PDF p. 3
  6. A permanent flexible service is left for later work.
    with additional work planned on permanent flexibility and other emerging configurations PDF p. 3
  7. Loads already operating or with a complete request before February 16, 2027 are not treated as large loads.
    the CAISO will not consider any load a large load subject to the CAISO tariff where the load is operational or has a complete request with the PTO before the CAISO tariff revisions’ effective date of February 16, 2027 PDF p. 13
  8. CAISO asks for a February 16, 2027 effective date and a 30-day comment period.
    the CAISO requests that the Commission accept the tariff revisions contained in this filing effective February 16, 2027 PDF p. 46
  9. Scheduling and settlement software changes come later, in November 2027 and April 2028.
    effective November 1, 2027, and the tariff revisions in Section 11.36 (settlements) effective April 30, 2028 PDF p. 46

I. BACKGROUND
In the December Order, the Commission found “that PJM’s Tariff is unjust and
unreasonable because it does not contain provisions addressing with sufficient clarity or consistency the rates, terms, and conditions of service that apply to generators serving Co- Located Load and Eligible Customers5 taking transmission service on behalf of Co-Located Load.”6  Specifically, the Commission found PJM’s Tariff “unjust and unreasonable because it 3

II. MOTION FOR SHORTENED ANSWER PERIOD AND EXPEDITED ACTION
In light of the circumstances described herein, the PJM Transmission Owners request that the Commission shorten the period for answers to this Motion to three (3) days, making answers due no later than January 20, 2026.  The PJM Transmission Owners also respectfully request that the Commission expedite its consideration of this Motion and promptly issue an order immediately after the due date for answers has passed, but no later than January 21, 2026. The PJM Transmission Owners and other parties will benefit from the Commission’s

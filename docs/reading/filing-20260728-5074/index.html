EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-28 · Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc.

Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc. submits Request for Issuance of an Errata to the 06/18/2026 Order Instituting Proceedings Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act

Wabash Valley Power Association asks FERC to issue an errata to the E-7 show cause order removing it from the caption and listing it with the non-jurisdictional transmission owners, since it ceased to be a public utility on January 1, 2025.

Asks FERC to: Issue an errata removing Wabash Valley from the caption of EL26-67-000 and adding it to the list of non-jurisdictional PJM transmission owners in footnote 13.

Changes the schedule. Seeks correction of the E-7 show cause order's list of respondents; FERC granted the change in its August 13, 2026 errata notice.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7)

Key points

  1. Wabash says it ceased to be a public utility on January 1, 2025 because it is wholly owned by exempt cooperatives.
    January 1, 2025 because it became exempt from the Federal Power Act as a result of being wholly owned by exempt entities. PDF p. 4
  2. Wabash says it is similarly situated to the non-jurisdictional owners FERC listed in footnote 13.
    it is a PTO that is similarly situated to all of the other non-jurisdictional entities listed in footnote 13 of the Order. PDF p. 4
  3. Wabash does not dispute the need to address large-load and co-located load service in organized markets.
    it does not dispute the need for the Commission to address issues surrounding service to large load and co-located load within organized markets and elsewhere. PDF p. 3
  4. The requested errata would remove Wabash from the caption and add it to footnote 13.
    removes WVPA from the caption of Docket No. EL26-67-000, which intended to identify only PJM and jurisdictional PTOs PDF p. 5

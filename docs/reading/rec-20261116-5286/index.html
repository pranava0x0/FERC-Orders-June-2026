LSPGNY 205: Proposed Revisions re: RS10 Large Load-Driven Upgrade Cost Recovery

LS Power Grid New York Corporation I files revisions to Rate Schedule 10 of the NYISO tariff under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in ER27-518-000, answering the New York show cause order. It adds a pro forma cost recovery agreement with a 20-year minimum revenue contribution, security and NYISO billing. No upgrade is yet listed, so current rates do not change.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective January 16, 2027, 61 days after filing, and grant the requested waivers.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-12 show cause order for NYISO. It covers cost recovery for one non-incumbent owner with no retail customers, separate from NYISO's and the other owners' filings.

E-12 NYISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-12 NYISO: Cost transparency (P 73, p. 47)

Key points

  1. LS Power Grid proposes a company-specific way to recover the cost of upgrades driven by large loads.
    a transparent, company-specific mechanism for LS Power Grid to recover the costs of transmission upgrades that are identified as necessary PDF p. 2
  2. No upgrade is listed yet, so the filing does not change current rates.
    No Large Load-Driven Upgrade is listed in proposed Table 1 as of the requested effective date PDF p. 2
  3. An upgrade enters the rate only after a separate section 205 filing adds it to the table.
    a Large Load-Driven Upgrade becomes an Eligible Project only after Commission acceptance of a section 205 filing adding it to Table 1 PDF p. 4
  4. The agreement sets a fixed annual minimum revenue contribution for each covered upgrade.
    the CRA establishes a fixed Annual Minimum Transmission Revenue Contribution (“Annual MTRC”) PDF p. 4
  5. The contribution runs 20 years from the upgrade's in-service date.
    MTRC Term begins on the actual Upgrade In-Service Date and ends on the day immediately preceding its twentieth anniversary PDF p. 5
  6. Operational security equals all remaining revenue responsibility.
    The CRA requires Operational Security equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the nominal Remaining Cumulative Revenue Responsibility PDF p. 6
  7. Retail security can offset it dollar for dollar if the customer certifies it is available and enforceable.
    the direct Operational Security requirement may be reduced dollar-for-dollar by qualifying retail security support only where the Eligible Customer delivers a current Retail Security Certification PDF p. 6
  8. It calls the termination fee an accelerated contractual charge, not a penalty.
    The Termination Fee is an accelerated contractual termination charge, not a penalty PDF p. 5

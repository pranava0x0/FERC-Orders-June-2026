EXPEDITED MOTION FOR A NINE-DAY EXTENSION OF TIME OF
CONSTELLATION ENERGY GENERATION, LLC

Constellation Energy Generation, LLC (“Constellation”) respectfully requests a short nine-day extension of the December 7, 2026 comment deadline in the above-captioned dockets. On November 16, 2026, Pacific Gas and Electric Company (“PG&E”), Southern

California Edison Company (“SCE”), and San Diego Gas & Electric Company (“SDG&E”) submitted filings under section 205 of the Federal Power Act in the above-captioned dockets in response to the Commission’s June 18, 2026 order instituting a section 206 proceeding (“Show Cause Proceeding”) requiring a response from the California Independent System Operator Corp. (“CAISO”) and numerous transmission-owning utilities in the CAISO region.1  CAISO also made a section 205 filing on November 16, 2026 in response to the same Commission order in Docket No. ER27-508-000.  The Show Cause Proceeding addresses RTO/ISO and transmission utility tariff provisions relating to large load integration.

The PG&E, SCE, and SDG&E filings currently have been assigned a comment date of December 7, 2026, while the CAISO filing has a comment date of December 15, 2026.  All of these filings involve important issues that are highly interrelated.  As such, Constellation

respectfully requests a short nine-day extension of time to respond to the PG&E, SCE, and SDG&E filings.  Constellation submits that a nine-day extension to align response deadlines will facilitate more efficient and effective briefing of the issues presented in these interrelated dockets.  Additionally, a short extension would also be beneficial given the impacts of the Thanksgiving holiday falling during the comment period.

Given the timing of the upcoming holiday and short nature of this extension request, Constellation requests that the Commission grant this Motion summarily and issue a notice extending the deadline by November 23, 2026.  Granting the requested relief to extend the comment deadline to December 16, 2026, will not affect or interfere with the proposed effective dates for tariff changes requested in the CAISO filing, nor in the PG&E, SCE, and SDG&E filings.

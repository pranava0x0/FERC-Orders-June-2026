Seller obligations · PDF pp. 33–43

§5.5, §6.6, §6.6A: auction participation

The auction provisions place committed backstop capacity into PJM's existing auction system. The offer obligation runs through the term of the commitment and is subject to the stated exceptions.

all of the RBP UCAP MW that is committed in the Reliability Backstop Procurement through the term of the commitment PDF p. 34

Procurement target · PDF pp. 44–45

§18, §18.1: procurement size

The proposed one-time procurement begins in 2027. Its initial target cannot exceed either the updated 2028/29 auction shortfall or the reported large-load quantity expressed in unforced capacity.

the UCAP equivalent of the aggregate region-wide Large Load MW quantity reported by the Electric Distributor PDF p. 45

Demand flexibility · PDF pp. 46–47

§18.2: qualifying bilateral offsets

A qualifying new capacity resource must meet the tariff's eligibility requirements. The tariff allows several kinds of supporting evidence: a bilateral contract for at least five delivery years, qualifying self-supply, or a signed commitment for demand response or distributed generation.

signed bilateral contracts for Qualifying RBP Offset UCAP MW PDF p. 47

Retail jurisdiction · PDF pp. 48–49

§18.3: cost allocation and credits

Zonal shares reflect load growth and eligible offsets. If a distributor reports an allocation from its retail regulator, PJM uses those MW to bill suppliers. If it does not, capacity obligations supply the default shares.

Electric Distributors may report updated RBO MW to the Office of the Interconnection after the initial allocation is reported PDF p. 49

Registry reporting · PDF p. 50

§18.3A: the distributor's report

At least seven days before the procurement, each distributor outside a Fixed Resource Requirement service area must report the identity and quantity of incremental large load supported by an agreement. This report establishes backstop exposure, not registry membership.

the quantity and identity of incremental Large Load that is supported by an electric service obligation, construction commitment, or similar agreement PDF p. 50

Reliability and timing · PDF p. 51

§18.4: offer and selection windows

The proposed tariff gives sellers four weeks to submit offers and allows six weeks after that for clearing and selection. These intervals run from the actual procurement, not the filing date.

The Reliability Backstop Procurement offer submission window for RBP Sell Offers shall open for four weeks PDF p. 51

Seller eligibility · PDF pp. 52–58

§18.5: eligibility, selection and settlement

PJM sets eligibility and evidence requirements by resource type, then ranks offers by levelized cost. The $555/MW-day ceiling applies to the MW-weighted average levelized cost of the selected offers, not a guaranteed price for each resource.

The MW-weighted average of the levelized procurement cost of all selected RBP Sell Offers shall not exceed $555/MW-day UCAP PDF p. 57

Seller obligations · PDF pp. 59–60

§18.6: delivery and shortfall charges

Committed capacity has an auction offer obligation. Undelivered MW lose daily backstop settlement credits and can incur shortfall charges during the fixed 15-year period.

will not be compensated for the undelivered megawatts via RBP Settlement Credits PDF p. 60

Exit liability · PDF p. 61

§18.7: exiting suppliers

A supplier leaving PJM would still owe its allocated charges, even if another entity's departure caused the exit. A FERC rate filing must specify the terms and the amount.

subject to a rate filing with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission that details the specific terms and amounts owed PDF p. 61

Constellation asks who benefits when an owner leaves

Constellation Energy Generation · Related public statement

Constellation asked FERC to clarify that it had not assigned the departing owner’s share to suppliers by default. In its view an exiting owner, rather than captive customers, should bear exit costs it causes.

PDF pp. 16–17

Credit and default · PDF pp. 62–66

§18.8: collateral on both sides of the auction

Sellers post security before offering; losing sellers get it back within five business days after clearing. Suppliers assigned charges secure their share under a separate formula. A transfer does not release the original supplier's collateral until the successor posts and unpaid invoices are settled.

Any RBP Collateral posted by transferor shall be returned once Transferee has posted the RBP Collateral PDF p. 66

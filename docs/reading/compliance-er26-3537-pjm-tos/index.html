PJM Transmission Owners' partial compliance filing implementing the Interim NITS rate

The PJM Transmission Owners file a partial compliance filing under the June 18 co-location order, adding tariff records that set the Interim NITS rate, and request a July 1, 2028 effective date.

Asks FERC to: Accept the partial compliance filing without hearing, modification or condition, effective July 1, 2028.

Carries out the order. Carries out the E-2 order's new co-location services by filing the Interim NITS rate, ahead of the November 16 date that aligns with PJM's E-7 show cause response.

E-2 PJM: Interim NITS (P 2, p. 3) E-2 PJM: One of three new co-location services (P 2, p. 3)

Key points

  1. The filing implements the rate for Interim Network Integration Transmission Service ordered on June 18.
    the PJM Transmission Owners are submitting new and revised tariff records to the PJM Open Access Transmission Tariff PDF p. 1
  2. The three new services are Interim NITS, firm contract demand and non-firm contract demand transmission service.
    The new transmission services are: Interim NITS, firm contract demand transmission service, and non-firm contract demand transmission service. PDF p. 1
  3. FERC granted a 90-day extension to November 16, 2026, but PJM committed to file Interim NITS provisions on August 17.
    Notwithstanding its request for extension, PJM committed to submit a compliance filing on August 17, 2026, addressing several compliance directives, including those related to Interim NITS. PDF p. 2
  4. New Part XI sections 706 and 706.1 set the Interim NITS rate, with two new definitions.
    New Sections 706 and 706.1 specify the Interim NITS rate. PDF p. 2
  5. The owners request a July 1, 2028 effective date.
    The PJM Transmission Owners request that the revisions submitted with this partial compliance filing become effective on July 1, 2028. PDF p. 2
  6. They note the directives are under challenge and the tariff sheets may face a termination filing.
    the tendered tariff sheet revisions herein may be subject to a subsequent termination filing. PDF p. 1

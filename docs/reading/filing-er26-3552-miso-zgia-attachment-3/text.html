4

5 
A. 
No.  ZG Interconnection Service is fundamentally different from ERIS or NRIS service.

7 
substation where the ZG Resource and ZG Associated Load are interconnected. (except for 
8

14 
ERIS or NRIS can be held by a Resource  at the same time that it has ZG Interconnection 
15 
Service.

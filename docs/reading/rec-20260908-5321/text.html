I. COMMENTS IN SUPPORT
A. ZG Interconnection Service Will Provide the MISO Region With
Another Efficient, Effective, and Tailored Tool to Address the Region’s Large Load Growth

The MISO Transmission Owners support MISO’s proposed ZG Interconnection Service construct as a just and reasonable framework that provides a limited, streamlined interconnection path for a ZG Resource, i.e., a planned generator seeking to connect at the same substation and with the same voltage level as existing or planned load, including large loads and co-located loads.5  The proposed streamlined ZGIA framework, which is specifically designed to complement MISO’s standard Interconnection Queue process, the Definitive Planning Phase (“DPP”),6 establishes a targeted 90-day study process for generation resources seeking a more limited interconnection service than Energy Resource Interconnection Service (i.e., ERIS), which grants “as available” access to the full MISO Transmission System.7  The ZGIA interconnection study process recognizes “the limited distance over which energy would need to be—and is permitted to—flow,” allowing for a more targeted and efficient study process.8  Load customers, including large load customers, also will benefit from ZGIA interconnection studies that recognize on-site generation, potentially reducing network upgrade requirements to interconnect. By proposing a new ZG Interconnection Service process that echoes MISO’s standard interconnection process with slight variations, the ZG Interconnection Service process adds consistency, transparency, and structure around these distinctive interconnection requests to meet specific load needs.  Through its proposed ZGIA framework, MISO will have a uniform process to account for actual impacts of these resources with limited injections; ensure these Interconnection Customers pay for only the necessary upgrades of their limited impacts; confirm the Interconnection Customer’s proposed means for limiting injections; and memorialize and enforce the Interconnection Customer’s commitments, including the control equipment and operational limitations, via a binding ZGIA.24

B. ZG Interconnection Service Will Address the Show Cause Order’s
Concern With Co-Located Load
When discussing MISO’s lack of Tariff provisions regarding interconnection service for generation serving co-located load, the Commission’s Show Cause Order highlighted MISO’s development of a zero-injection generator interconnection process and articulated a concern that such a process and its studies were not part of the Tariff.25 MISO’s proposal here, the culmination of that stakeholder-driven development, addresses that concern.  The proposed ZGIA framework is an adaptation of standard interconnection service that aligns with directives of the Show Cause Order.  Specifically, MISO’s proposal

II. MOTION TO INTERVENE

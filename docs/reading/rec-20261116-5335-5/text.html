ARTICLE 3

SCOPE OF INTERCONNECTION SERVICE
3.1
Provision of Service. For purposes of this Agreement, Transmission Owner shall provide load interconnection service to Customer at the Point of Interconnection for the Term, up to the Maximum Load Facility Peak Demand, subject to this Agreement, the Tariff, and Applicable Laws and Regulations.

3.2
Load Limits. Customer shall ensure that the actual demand of the Load Facility does not exceed the Maximum Load Facility Peak Demand, the Maximum Annual

PDF page 13

Energy, or the amount fully studied in the System Impact Study and Facilities Study, as measured at the Point of Interconnection.

3.3
Identification of Reliability Violation. If NYISO or Transmission Owner identifies a reliability violation associated with the Load Facility, at any time, Transmission Owner shall notify Customer of the amount of load that may be reliably served, and the Parties shall coordinate operational limitations or mitigation measures to minimize interruptions or reductions; Customer shall conform its load to such amount.

3.4
No Transmission Service or Other Services. This Agreement does not constitute a request for, or agreement to provide, transmission service under the Tariff, or the sale or purchase of energy, capacity, or ancillary services under the NYISO Services Tariff. Transmission Owner does not sell electric energy under this Agreement. Customer shall be solely responsible for arranging its energy supply and shall maintain a service agreement with a load serving entity for the Load Facility. All Delivery Service shall be provided pursuant to, and billed in accordance with, the applicable Tariff, and Customer shall select and qualify for the applicable service classification. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Transmission Owner to provide Delivery Service beyond the Tariff or to expand the transmission system beyond the facilities identified in the studies for the Load Facility.

3.5
Load Ramp Projection. Within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date, Customer shall provide monthly projections of energy and demand for the Term in the form of Exhibit A. On the fifth anniversary of the Effective Date and every fifth anniversary thereafter, Customer shall provide updated projections for the remainder of the Term (the most recent version, the “Load Ramp Projection”); provided that no update may change the monthly projections by more than [five percent (5%)] from the prior version (absent prior written authorization by Transmission Owner and NYISO), and in no event may the demand projection exceed the Maximum Load Facility Peak Demand or the annual energy projection exceed the Maximum Annual Energy.

3.6
Reduction of Interconnection Rights. If the Load Facility fails to meet the Minimum Committed Load for [three (3)] consecutive months or [three (3)] non- consecutive months in any [six (6)]-month period after the Commercial Operation Date (a “Shortfall Event”), Transmission Owner may notify Customer that it intends to reduce the Maximum Load Facility Peak Demand if Customer does not consistently meet the Minimum Committed Load during the following sixty (60) Calendar Days. Any reduction shall be proportionate to the average load shortfall over the applicable period, and Exhibit A shall be amended accordingly, subject to any filing with or acceptance by FERC or the NYPSC required under Applicable Laws and Regulations. Any reduction of the Maximum Load Facility Peak Demand under this Article 3.6 shall not reduce Customer’s obligations under the Cost Recovery Agreement or the Security Amount under the Security Agreement. 3.7

EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-09 · Individual No Affiliation

Motion to Intervene and Comments of Evan Pierce re the large-load interconnection tariff provisions

New Era Energy & Digital, a data center developer, moves to intervene in EL26-67 and files limited comments asking PJM to address a Maximum Net Withdrawal study framework and fast-track treatment of uprates and reactivated capacity in its adequacy report.

Asks FERC to: Grant intervention and have PJM address Maximum Net Withdrawal for paired load and generation, and expedited uprate and reactivation pathways, in its forthcoming filings.

Answers the order. Limited comments on E-7 asking PJM to address proximate-generation study treatment in its show cause response and uprate fast-tracking in the 30-day adequacy report.

E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8) E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7)

Key points

  1. New Era says PJM should study a load paired with dependable nearby generation by its enforceable maximum net withdrawal rather than full gross demand.
    PJM should evaluate the project based on the facility’s enforceable maximum net withdrawal from the transmission system, rather than assuming the facility will impose its full gross demand PDF p. 4
  2. A net-withdrawal cap would need metering, controls, operator visibility and tariff consequences for exceedance.
    a binding net-withdrawal cap in the relevant service agreement or other tariff- governed arrangement PDF p. 4
  3. PJM should say what transmission service an Eligible Customer takes for a load operated under such a cap.
    PJM should also explain what transmission service the Eligible Customer must take for a large load operated subject to an enforceable Maximum Net Withdrawal. PDF p. 5
  4. The 30-day adequacy report should cover expedited study of low-risk uprates at existing plants.
    PJM should address whether its Tariff provides an adequate pathway for expedited treatment of uprates at existing generating facilities. PDF p. 5
  5. It also asks about expedited reconnection of retired or mothballed capacity without bypassing reliability review.
    The purpose would not be to bypass reliability review, but to avoid subjecting bounded reactivations of existing capacity to procedures designed for wholly new speculative projects. PDF p. 6

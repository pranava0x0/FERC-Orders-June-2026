EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-07 · Corporate Energy Buyers Association

Answer of Corporate Energy Buyers Association in Support of Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.'s et al. 08/03/2026 Motion for Abeyance

The Corporate Energy Buyers Association answers in support of the MISO abeyance motion, saying 90 days allows stakeholder work and avoids moving the debate to the FERC docket.

Asks FERC to: Grant the MISO and Transmission Owners abeyance motion in an expedited manner.

Answers the order. Supports the 90-day abeyance of the E-8 60-day show cause deadline; FERC granted the motion on August 14, 2026.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. CEBA says abeyance gives time for collaboration instead of a rushed proposal that shifts debate into the docket.
    as opposed to a rushed proposal that would unnecessarily shift stakeholder debate to the Commission docket. PDF p. 1
  2. CEBA says 90 days would not materially slow reform.
    a 90-day extension would not materially impede the Commission’s objectives for advancing reforms quickly PDF p. 1
  3. CEBA asks for expedited grant, tied to MISO's mid-November filings.
    CEBA respectfully requests that the Commission grant the motion in an expedited manner. PDF p. 2

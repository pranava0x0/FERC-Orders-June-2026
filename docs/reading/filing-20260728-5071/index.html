EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-28 · Indicated PJM Transmission Owners

Motion for Abeyance of the Indicated PJM Transmission Owners and Request for Expedited Action and Shortened Comment Period

The Indicated PJM Transmission Owners move to hold their response deadline in the E-7 show cause proceeding in abeyance for 90 days, to November 16, 2026, so they can prepare section 205 filings on cost recovery agreements, new service rates and transparency.

Asks FERC to: Hold the deadline to respond in abeyance until November 16, 2026, shorten the comment period to five days, and rule by August 10, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Seeks to move the E-7 60-day show cause deadline to November 16, 2026; FERC granted it on August 14. Describes the cost recovery, rate and transparency filings the Transmission Owners plan.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: Cost-recovery agreement (P 77, p. 28) E-7 PJM: Cost transparency (P 71, p. 51) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56)

Key points

  1. The Transmission Owners ask for a 90-day abeyance to November 16, 2026 to develop section 205 filings.
    respectfully move to hold the deadline to respond in the above- captioned proceeding in abeyance for 90 days, until November 16, 2026. PDF p. 1
  2. They expect to file a pro forma cost recovery agreement and rates for flexible and electrically proximate large load services, with penalties for exceeding contractual limits.
    will contain a pro forma cost recovery agreement(s), as well as rates for new transmission services for “flexible large loads” and “electrically proximate large loads or co-located loads.” PDF p. 4
  3. They say a cost recovery agreement raises open issues, including overlap with bilateral retail agreements and how to set each customer's minimum contribution.
    the specific mechanism for determining each Eligible Customer’s required minimum contribution. PDF p. 6
  4. They may revise local planning procedures such as Attachment M-3 to meet the order's transparency findings on Network Upgrade costs.
    considering what, if any, PJM Tariff modifications they will need to include in their own FPA section 205 filing(s) relating to transparency enhancements. PDF p. 9
  5. They ask FERC to rule by August 10 with a five-day comment period.
    request that the Commission establish a comment period of five (5) days. PDF p. 10

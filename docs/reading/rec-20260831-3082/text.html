UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION



PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.
Docket No.
ER26-3515-000


NOTICE SHORTENING ANSWER PERIOD

(August 31, 2026)

On August 28, 2026, pursuant to Rules 212 and 2008 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure,[fn:2] the Organization of PJM States, Inc. (OPSI) filed a motion for extension of time to submit comments in response to PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.’s filing in the above-referenced docket.  OPSI also requests expedited action on its motion.
Under Rule 213(d)(1)(i) of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure, answers to motions requesting an extension of time shall be made within five days after the motion is filed, unless otherwise ordered.[fn:3]  Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that the deadline for filing answers to OPSI’s motion is hereby shortened to 5:00 P.M. EDT, on Tuesday, September 1, 2026.








Carlos D. Clay,
Deputy Secretary.

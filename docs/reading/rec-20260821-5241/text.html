11) Should the stakeholder process be advisory, i.e., stakeholder votes only inform the Board?

x

Comments: Yes, the stakeholder process should be advisory.  Stakeholder input is important, but members currently exercise undue influence over PJM. Members would continue to have all of their rights at FERC.

Stakeholder Process

Questions/Comments
Yes
No
16) Should the approval threshold in the Members Committee be lowered from two-thirds sector-weighted voting?

x

Comments: No, the approval threshold in the Members Committee should not be lowered from two-thirds sector-weighted voting.  The current sector weighting appropriate balances sectors; However, reform is necessary associated with members’ use of agency voting, which typically results in just a handful of agents casting votes for more than 50% of the votes cast.

17) Should the voting rules barring affiliate votes, which apply to senior standing committees, also apply to lower-level committees?

x

Comments: No, the current rules balance upper and lower committees and appropriately recognize member size in the market.  A member’s financial commitment to PJM should be reflected.  In past, other reforms that acknowledged these differences between members were explored, but rejected, and thus, the rules should be preserved here.

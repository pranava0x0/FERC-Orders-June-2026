What the November 6 filing covered

The original proposal added CHILLS provisions to SPP’s western pricing zones. The November 10 amendment changes the Upper Missouri rate sheet.

to add provisions for Conditional High Impact Large Load Service (“CHILLS”) for pricing Zones in the SPP West Region PDF p. 2

Cost allocation · PDF pp. 2–3

Which revenue credits were missing?

The proposed correction restores two references: Schedule 14 DC tie revenues and Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues. The latter compensates western tie owners for increased use.

omitted revisions to the rate sheet for the Upper Missouri Zone PDF p. 2

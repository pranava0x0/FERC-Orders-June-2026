I. BACKGROUND

SPP submitted the November 6 Filing to add provisions for Conditional High Impact Large Load Service (“CHILLS”) for pricing Zones in the SPP West Region, and to make other clarifying edits.

II. DESCRIPTION OF AMENDED FILING

In the November 6 Filing, SPP proposed revisions to rate sheets for Point-To- Point Transmission Service in the SPP West Region. SPP inadvertently omitted revisions to the rate sheet for the Upper Missouri Zone to add references to Schedule 14 and Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues. SPP proposes the following revisions:

Point-To-Point Transmission Service and Conditional High Impact Large Load Service Revenue Crediting for Point-To-Point Rates

Rates for Point-To-Point Transmission Service under this Attachment T shall reflect any adjustments for Schedules 7, and 8 revenues, 14, Schedule and 15 revenues, Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues, and revenues distributed and allocated under Attachment AU, implemented in accordance with Section 34.1(2) of this Tariff. The rates, including any applicable adjustments for Schedules 7, and 8, 14 revenues, andSchedule 15 revenues, Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues, and revenues distributed and allocated under Attachment AU, shall be set forth in the RRR File.4

In SPP’s filing to expand its Regional Transmission Organization,5 SPP added Zone 19 West to include the geographic area of the Upper Missouri Zone located in the Western Interconnection. The rate sheet for the Upper Missouri Zone is applicable to entities located in the Upper Missouri Zone East (“Zone 19 East”) and the Upper Missouri Zone West (“Zone 19 West”).

 First, SPP adds a reference to Schedule 14 for the DC Tie Access Charge. The DC Tie Access Charge is defined as “[a] charge on a transmission reservation utilizing a West DC Tie that sinks outside of the Zone where the West DC Tie is located.”6 The Transmission Customer is responsible for the DC Tie Access Charge determined in

Schedule 14 of the Tariff. SPP adds this reference to the Upper Missouri Zone rate sheet because the DC Tie Access Charge is applicable in this Zone.

Second, SPP adds a reference to Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues. The Incremental Market Efficiency Use Charge is “[a] market recovery mechanism to compensate West DC Tie Transmission Owners for the expected loss of life of certain West DC Tie facilities due to increased utilization of the West DC Ties by the Integrated Marketplace.”7 SPP adds this reference to the Upper Missouri Zone rate sheet because Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues are applicable in this Zone.

SPP is not including revisions, as proposed in the November 6 Filing to other rate sheets, to remove the references to Attachment AU revenues from this rate sheet because the Upper Missouri Zone includes utilities in the SPP East Region.

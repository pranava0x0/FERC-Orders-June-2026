I. 
BACKGROUND 
 
SPP submitted the November 6 Filing to add provisions for Conditional High 
Impact Large Load Service (“CHILLS”) for pricing Zones in the SPP West Region, 
and to make other clarifying edits.  
 
II. 
DESCRIPTION OF AMENDED FILING 
 
In the November 6 Filing, SPP proposed revisions to rate sheets for Point-To-
Point Transmission Service in the SPP West Region. SPP inadvertently omitted 
revisions to the rate sheet for the Upper Missouri Zone to add references to Schedule 
14 and Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues. SPP proposes the following 
revisions: 
 
Point-To-Point Transmission Service and Conditional High Impact Large 
Load Service Revenue Crediting for Point-To-Point Rates 
Rates for Point-To-Point Transmission Service under this Attachment T shall 
reflect any adjustments for Schedules 7, and 8 revenues, 14, Schedule and 15 
revenues, Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues, and revenues 
distributed and allocated under Attachment AU, implemented in accordance 
with Section 34.1(2) of this Tariff. The rates, including any applicable 
adjustments for Schedules 7, and 8, 14 revenues, andSchedule 15 revenues, 
Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues, and revenues distributed and 
allocated under Attachment AU, shall be set forth in the RRR File.4 
 
In SPP’s filing to expand its Regional Transmission Organization,5 SPP added 
Zone 19 West to include the geographic area of the Upper Missouri Zone located in the 
Western Interconnection. The rate sheet for the Upper Missouri Zone is applicable to 
entities located in the Upper Missouri Zone East (“Zone 19 East”) and the Upper 
Missouri Zone West (“Zone 19 West”). 
 
 First, SPP adds a reference to Schedule 14 for the DC Tie Access Charge. The 
DC Tie Access Charge is defined as “[a] charge on a transmission reservation utilizing 
a West DC Tie that sinks outside of the Zone where the West DC Tie is located.”6 The 
Transmission Customer is responsible for the DC Tie Access Charge determined in

Schedule 14 of the Tariff. SPP adds this reference to the Upper Missouri Zone rate 
sheet because the DC Tie Access Charge is applicable in this Zone. 
 
Second, SPP adds a reference to Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues. 
The Incremental Market Efficiency Use Charge is “[a] market recovery mechanism to 
compensate West DC Tie Transmission Owners for the expected loss of life of certain 
West DC Tie facilities due to increased utilization of the West DC Ties by the 
Integrated Marketplace.”7 SPP adds this reference to the Upper Missouri Zone rate 
sheet because Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues are applicable in this Zone. 
 
SPP is not including revisions, as proposed in the November 6 Filing to other 
rate sheets, to remove the references to Attachment AU revenues from this rate sheet 
because the Upper Missouri Zone includes utilities in the SPP East Region.

Re: PJM Interconnection, L.L.C., Docket No. ER26-3515-000
Dear Secretary Reese:
Enclosed for filing in the above-captioned proceeding are the Motion for Leave to File Supplemental Comments and Supplemental Comments of Persistence Analytics Group LLC regarding PJM

Interconnection, L.L.C.'s Large Load Registry and Bring Your Own New Capacity implementation materials.

PAG's supplemental comments are limited to implementation issues raised by PJM's October 2026 materials, including the distinction between the proposition actually validated, the currency of supporting evidence, material-change revalidation, and the evidentiary maturity appropriate to later consequential reliance. PAG does not seek to delay the Phase 1 Registry process or the 2029/30 Base Residual Auction and does not propose a new public scoring methodology or separate approval layer.

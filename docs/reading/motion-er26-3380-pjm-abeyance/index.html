Motion for Abeyance of PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (ER26-3380-000 and EL26-108-000), holding the backstop paper hearing pending a new section 205 filing

PJM asks FERC to hold the paper hearing on its Reliability Backstop Procurement rules in abeyance while it prepares a new section 205 filing on cost allocation, Transmission Owner exit and LSE collateral.

Asks FERC to: Hold the paper hearing proceedings and any additional briefing in ER26-3380 and EL26-108 in abeyance pending PJM's new section 205 filing.

Parallel proceeding. Schedule motion in PJM's backstop procurement dockets, which run beside E-7 and concern how PJM secures generation for new large loads, the subject of the E-7 adequacy report.

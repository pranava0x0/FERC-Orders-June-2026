ER27-520-000 · 2026-11-16 · PJM Transmission Owners (filed by PECO Energy Company and PJM)

PJM Transmission Owners file tariff revisions for service to co-located and large loads under section 205

The PJM Transmission Owners file tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in a new docket, ER27-520-000. The filing sets rates for the new co-located and large-load services, a pro forma cost recovery agreement with a minimum revenue commitment and credit support, and a 15-year zonal allocation of upgrade costs from PJM’s Fast Energization Process.

Asks FERC to: Accept the tariff revisions without hearing, modification or condition, with the new service rates effective March 16, 2027 and the agreement and cost allocation on an indeterminate date.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-7 show cause order for PJM. The owners also say it meets their compliance obligation under the E-2 co-location order and will tell the EL25-49 parties so by letter.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: Cost-recovery agreement (P 77, p. 28) E-7 PJM: Cost transparency (P 71, p. 51) E-2 PJM: Paper hearing sets the rates (Disposition, p. 5) E-2 PJM: One of three new co-location services (P 2, p. 3)

Key points

  1. The owners say the revisions answer the E-7 show cause order and comply with the E-2 co-location order.
    These revisions are both responsive to and consistent with the preliminary findings made by the Commission in its June 18, 2026 Order Instituting Proceeding PDF p. 1
  2. Large Load customers would make a minimum contribution to transmission revenue requirements, backed by shortfall payments and financial security.
    to make a minimum contribution towards transmission owners’ revenue requirements, along with a shortfall payment and crediting mechanism and financial security obligations PDF p. 2
  3. The committed contribution is 80% to 100% of a full firm transmission service benchmark.
    which percentage shall be no less than 80% and no greater than 100% PDF p. 77
  4. Initial credit support equals the projected charges on the full initial load commitment over the agreement’s term.
    equals the aggregate projected charges associated with 100 percent of the Initial Load Commitment over the CRA term PDF p. 18
  5. The cost recovery agreement runs at least ten years after the first service date.
    the date that is at least ten (10) years after the first Service Date as specified in Exhibit A PDF p. 80
  6. Owners may keep using existing or new customer protections instead of the pro forma agreement if FERC accepts them.
    as alternatives to the pro forma CRA, so long as those arrangements result in comparable protections and are accepted for filing by the Commission PDF p. 2
  7. Upgrade costs from Fast Energization go to the zones with large loads for 15 years, then to regional allocation.
    this allocation methodology will apply to each portfolio of IRRs identified through PJM’s Fast Energization Process for 15 years PDF p. 3
  8. Rates for the three new transmission services largely repeat those FERC approved in the co-location order.
    The rates for these services are largely identical to those proposed by the PJM Transmission Owners and approved by the Commission in the Co-Located Load Paper Hearing Order PDF p. 3
  9. The owners commit to change their formula rate templates to show state regulators the costs of transmission built for large loads.
    provide transparency to state regulators and other interested parties regarding the costs of transmission facilities being constructed by each Transmission Owner to provide transmission service to Large Loads PDF p. 4

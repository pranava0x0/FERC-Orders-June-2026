LSPGNY AND FORMULA RATE BACKGROUND 
1 
Q. 
PLEASE PROVIDE AN OVERVIEW OF LSPGNY AND ITS FORMULA RATE. 
2 
A. 
LSPGNY is a transmission-only company that owns Segment A, developed with the New 
3 
York Power Authority to meet the AC Transmission Public Policy Transmission Need.  
4 
LSPGNY’s formula rate consists of its Formula Rate Template (NYISO Open Access 
5 
Transmission Tariff (“OATT”) Section 6.10.7.2.1) and its Formula Rate Implementation 
6 
Protocols (Section 6.10.7.2.2) (together, the “Formula Rate”), which were accepted by 
7 
the Commission in Docket No. ER20-716.  LSPGNY’s revenue requirement is recovered 
8 
under Rate Schedule 10 of the NYISO OATT. 
9 
Q. 
HOW DOES THE FORMULA RATE OPERATE EACH YEAR? 
10 
A. 
By September 30, LSPGNY posts an Annual Projection of its revenue requirement for 
11 
the upcoming Rate Year.  By June 30 after each Rate Year, LSPGNY posts an Annual 
12 
Update using actual FERC Form No. 1 or other Company data.  The difference between 
13 
the revenue LSPGNY collected during the Rate Year and its actual revenue requirement, 
14 
with interest, is the True-up Adjustment, which is included in a later Annual Projection. 
15 
Q. 
WHY MUST LSPGNY REVISE ITS FORMULA RATE? 
16 
A. 
In the Show Cause Order, the Commission directed the NYISO and its transmission 
17 
owners to show cause why their tariffs remain just and reasonable or to propose changes 
18 
for integrating large loads.  Among other things, the Commission called for a mechanism 
19 
to ensure that large load payments are “credited toward transmission owners’ 
20 
transmission revenue requirements.”  LSPGNY’s current Formula Rate Template reports 
21 
plant values only in total and has no means of identifying the facilities built to serve large 
22 
loads or of crediting the amounts Eligible Customers pay under their CRAs.  The 
23 
revisions I describe provide that mechanism.

IV. 
THE LARGE LOAD COST RECOVERY FRAMEWORK 
1 
Q. 
WHAT IS A CRA? 
2 
A. 
A CRA is an agreement between LSPGNY and an Eligible Customer taking transmission 
3 
service on behalf of a large load.  Under its CRA, the Eligible Customer commits to an 
4 
annual Minimum Transmission Revenue Contribution (“Annual MTRC”) for each 
5 
Covered Upgrade built to serve the large load, for the term of the CRA.  The Annual 
6 
MTRC is calculated solely under the CRA.  The terms of the pro forma CRA, including 
7 
how the Annual MTRC is established, termination, and credit support requirements, are 
8 
described in the transmittal letter and the testimony of Mr. Hayes.  The Formula Rate 
9 
Template uses the Annual MTRC set by each CRA as an input. 
10 
Q. 
HOW ARE THE COSTS OF COVERED UPGRADES RECOVERED? 
11 
A. 
The cost of each Covered Upgrade is included in LSPGNY’s rate base and revenue 
12 
requirement and recovered under the NYISO OATT together with the cost of Segment A.  
13 
Each Eligible Customer pays transmission charges for the service it takes, like any other 
14 
transmission customer.  The transmission revenues that count toward an Eligible 
15 
Customer’s Annual MTRC, as determined under its CRA, are its “Qualifying 
16 
Transmission Revenues.” 
17 
Q. 
WHAT IS THE SUPPLEMENTAL MTRC? 
18 
A. 
The Supplemental MTRC is the amount, if any, by which an Eligible Customer’s Annual 
19 
MTRC for a Covered Upgrade exceeds its Qualifying Transmission Revenues for that 
20 
Covered Upgrade.  It requires the Eligible Customer to pay at least its committed Annual 
21 
MTRC each year, even if its load develops more slowly than expected.  The 
22 
Supplemental MTRC is measured separately for each Covered Upgrade and is never less 
23

PDF page 7

Exhibit No. GNY-200 
Page 7 of 20 
 
than zero, so Qualifying Transmission Revenues above the Annual MTRC on one 
1 
Covered Upgrade do not offset a shortfall on another. 
2 
Q. 
HOW IS THE SUPPLEMENTAL MTRC BILLED? 
3 
A. 
The Supplemental MTRC follows the same projection and true-up cycle as the rest of the 
4 
Formula Rate.  In each Annual Projection, LSPGNY projects each Eligible Customer’s 
5 
Qualifying Transmission Revenues and resulting Supplemental MTRC for the Rate Year.  
6 
The NYISO bills each active Eligible Customer its projected Supplemental MTRC in 
7 
equal monthly installments during the Rate Year and remits the amounts to LSPGNY.  In 
8 
the Annual Update, the Supplemental MTRC billed is compared to the Supplemental 
9 
MTRC based on actual Qualifying Transmission Revenues.  The difference is included, 
10 
without interest, in that Eligible Customer’s Supplemental MTRC in a later Annual 
11 
Projection. 
12 
Q. 
WHY IS THE SUPPLEMENTAL MTRC PROJECTED AND TRUED UP 
13 
RATHER THAN BILLED AFTER THE RATE YEAR? 
14 
A. 
Billing the projected amount during the Rate Year matches the timing of the 
15 
Supplemental MTRC credit to the Rate Year in which it reduces the revenue requirement, 
16 
spreads the Eligible Customer’s payments evenly over the year, and uses the projection 
17 
and true-up process that stakeholders already know.  Because each Eligible Customer’s 
18 
Supplemental MTRC is trued up to actual Qualifying Transmission Revenues, the 
19 
Eligible Customer is ultimately responsible for its full Annual MTRC.  Any difference 
20 
between the projected and actual amounts is corrected in a later Rate Year. 
21 
Q. 
WHAT HAPPENS IF AN ELIGIBLE CUSTOMER TERMINATES ITS CRA? 
22 
A. 
If an Eligible Customer terminates its CRA after an applicable in-service date, it pays a 
23 
Termination Fee equal to its Remaining Cumulative Revenue Responsibility less any

Supplemental MTRC already billed and remaining unpaid as of the effective termination 
1 
date, as provided in CRA Section 3.3.4. The NYISO collects and holds that fee. The 
2 
excluded unpaid bills remain separately payable as Large Load Charges, and receipt of 
3 
the Termination Fee in full discharges only the remaining responsibility included in the 
4 
fee, not those unpaid bills.  The NYISO remits to LSPGNY the scheduled Annual 
5 
Termination Fee Remittances determined under CRA Section 3.3.6 and the Termination 
6

10 
as the Supplemental MTRC, with projected remittances credited in the Annual Projection 
11 
and actual remittances received credited in the Annual Update.  The Covered Upgrade 
12 
remains in rate base.  The terms governing termination are described in the testimony of 
13 
Mr. Hayes. 
14 
Q. 
HOW DOES THE FORMULA RATE TREAT LARGE LOAD CRA REVENUE? 
15 
A. 
The Supplemental MTRC and Annual Termination Fee Remittances (together, “Large 
16

19 
Load CRA Revenue it projects for the Rate Year (“Projected Large Load CRA 
20 
Revenue”).  In the Annual Update, it credits the Large Load CRA Revenue it receives 
21 
during the Rate Year (“Actual Large Load CRA Revenue”): Supplemental MTRC 
22 
payments the NYISO collects and remits, Applied Security Proceeds applied to 
23 
Supplemental MTRC obligations and remitted by the NYISO, amounts LSPGNY

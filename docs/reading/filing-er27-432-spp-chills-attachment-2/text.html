SCHEDULE 15 
CONDITIONAL HIGH IMPACT LARGE LOAD SERVICE 
For each Point of Delivery of the Conditional High Impact Large Load Service 
(“CHILLS”), the Transmission Customer and Network Customer must compensate the 
Transmission Provider for the CHILLS up to the monthly sum of the applicable charges set forth 
below in addition to other applicable charges specified in the Tariff.  All effective rates under this 
schedule will be posted in the Revenue Requirements and Rates File (“RRR File”) on the SPP 
website. 
 
 
1. 
Zonal Rates per Kilowatt: The Transmission Customer and Network Customer 
must pay the zonal rate (per kilowatt) based upon the Zone where the load is located for the 
CHILLS. The zonal rate will be the hourly rate applicable to Non-Firm Point-To-Point 
Transmission Service for the specific on-peak or off-peak hour of the CHILLS usage. For each 
hour without Curtailment, the hourly charge will be calculated using kilowatts of the CHILLS 
Reserved Capacity.  For each hour with Curtailment, the hourly charge will be calculated using 
the Curtailment kilowatt limit specified by the Transmission Provider. CHILLS load exceeding 
the Curtailment limit specified by the Transmission Provider or the CHILLS Reserved Capacity is 
subject to additional charges and penalties pursuant to Part VII, Section 44.4 of this Tariff. 
 
The Zones are as follows: 
Zone 1: American Electric Power – West 
Zone 2: Kansas City Board of Public Utilities 
Zone 3: City Utilities of Springfield, Missouri 
Zone 4: Empire District Electric Company 
Zone 5: Grand River Dam Authority 
Zone 6: Evergy Metro, Inc. 
Zone 7: Oklahoma Gas & Electric Company 
Zone 8: Midwest Energy, Inc. 
Zone 9: Evergy Missouri West, Inc. 
Zone 10: Southwestern Power Administration 
Zone 11: Southwestern Public Service 
Zone 12: Sunflower Electric Power Corporation 
Zone 13: Western Farmers Electric Cooperative

2.  
Caps: The total demand charge in any month for usage up to the CHILLS Reserved 
Capacity will not exceed the monthly rate times the amount in kilowatts of CHILLS Reserved 
Capacity in such month.  
 
3.  
Redispatch Costs: The redispatch costs will be calculated in accordance with the 
formula and protocols shown in Attachment K.  
 
4.  
Discounts: The Transmission Provider may offer discounts under this 
Schedule.  Three principal requirements apply to discounts for transmission service as 
follows:  (1) any offer of a discount made by the Transmission Provider must be announced to all 
Eligible Customers solely by posting on the OASIS, (2) any customer-initiated requests for 
discounts must occur solely by posting on the OASIS, and (3) once a discount is negotiated, details 
must be immediately posted on the OASIS.  For any discount agreed upon for service to a Point(s) 
of Delivery, the Transmission Provider must offer the same discounted CHILLS rate for the same 
time period to all Eligible Customers on all unconstrained transmission paths that go to the same 
Point(s) of Delivery on the Transmission System.  In offering discounts, the Transmission 
Provider’s goal will be to maximize transmission revenues. 
 
5.  
Real Power Losses: The Transmission Customer and Network Customer will be 
responsible for real power losses determined in accordance with Attachment M and Part VII, 
Section 45.4 of this Tariff.

6. 
Wholesale Distribution Service: Where Wholesale Distribution Service is 
provided to effectuate CHILLS, the Transmission Customer and Network Customer must pay all 
charges levied pursuant to the Wholesale Distribution Service Agreement and Schedule 10. 
 
7.  
Base Plan Zonal Charges, Subregional Charge, and Region-wide Charges: The 
Transmission Customer and Network Customer must pay the applicable Schedule 11 zonal rate 
(per kilowatt) based upon the Zone where the load is located, the applicable Schedule 11 
Subregional Charge (per kilowatt) based upon the Subregion where the load is located, and the 
applicable Schedule 11 region-wide rate for CHILLS. The rates will be the applicable point-to-
point hourly rates for the specific on-peak or off-peak hour of CHILLS usage.  The hourly charges 
will be calculated using hourly kilowatts as stated in Section 1 of Schedule 15. The total Schedule 
11 zonal charge in any month for usage up to the CHILLS Reserved Capacity will not exceed the 
Schedule 11 point-to-point zonal monthly rate times the amount in kilowatts of CHILLS Reserved 
Capacity in such month. The total Schedule 11 region-wide charge in any month for usage up to 
the CHILLS Reserved Capacity will not exceed the Schedule 11 point-to-point region-wide rate 
times the amount of kilowatts of CHILLS Reserved Capacity in such month.  
 
8.  
Resales: Resales are not permissible for CHILLS.

EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-07 · Missouri Public Service Commission

Comments of the Public Service Commission of the State of Missouri in support of Southwest Power Pool, Inc.'s et al. request for abeyance filed 08/03/2026

The Missouri Public Service Commission comments in support of the SPP Joint Movants' 95-day abeyance request in EL26-68, arguing good cause exists because SPP's Board meets November 17, one day outside the 90-day window.

Asks FERC to: Grant the Joint Movants' request for a 95-day abeyance of the section 206 proceeding.

Answers the order. Supports the E-9 SPP abeyance motion and ties the extra five days to state-regulator review of the cost-shift protections the order directs SPP to address.

E-9 SPP: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-9 SPP: Cost-recovery + crediting (Ordering (B)(1)(b), p. 21)

Key points

  1. Missouri PSC says the 95 days fits SPP's November 17 Board meeting and state input to the compliance filing.
    SPP's Board of Directors meeting is scheduled for November 17, 2026—one day outside the 90-day window. PDF p. 1
  2. It cites the 12-month Order No. 1920 extension FERC gave SPP in April 2025 as precedent for good cause.
    the Commission granted SPP a 12-month extension of time to submit filings in compliance with the Commission's Order No. 1920 PDF p. 3
  3. State regulators care about transparency and cost-shift avoidance because they set retail rates that include transmission costs.
    Issues related to transparency and the avoidance of cost shifting are particularly important to the Missouri PSC and other state regulators who bear the responsibility of setting retail rates PDF p. 4
  4. The abeyance gives the CARE Team time to finish cost mitigation recommendations for the Board.
    The requested abeyance will allow the CARE Team time to complete this important work and present its recommendations to the Board. PDF p. 4

Dear Mr. Miller, Ms. Sipple, Mr. White and Mr. Oxman, 
Thank you for your separate correspondences expressing your organization’s thoughts and concerns regarding 
PJM’s approach to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s (FERC’s) Large Load Integration Show Cause 
Order (Order). Your correspondences were sent to the PJM Board for consideration. 
As you are aware, the PJM staff is having discussions on its approach to complying with this Order both in the 
public stakeholder process and through bilateral discussions with several parties. I encourage you to continue to 
work with the PJM team as our proposal is still under development.   
We appreciate and understand the concerns that you have raised and look forward to your continued engagement 
in the stakeholder process and with the PJM team.  
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
David E. Mills 
President & CEO, PJM Interconnection, LLC

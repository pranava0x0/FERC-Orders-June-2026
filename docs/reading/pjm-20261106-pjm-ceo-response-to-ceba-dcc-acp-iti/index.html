PJM’s chief executive replies to CEBA, the Data Center Coalition, ACP and ITI

PJM's chief executive replies on November 5, 2026 to four trade groups that wrote about PJM's approach to the show cause order. The reply says their letters went to the PJM Board and the proposal is still under development.

Asks FERC to: No request. It encourages the groups to keep working with the PJM team.

Background. A reply sent to the signing groups and copied to the PJM Board. It was not filed at FERC and takes no position on the substance of their letters.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7)

Key points

  1. PJM thanks the groups for letters on its approach to the large load show cause order.
    Thank you for your separate correspondences expressing your organization’s thoughts and concerns regarding PJM’s approach to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s (FERC’s) Large Load Integration Show Cause Order (Order). PDF p. 1
  2. PJM says the letters were sent to its Board for consideration.
    Your correspondences were sent to the PJM Board for consideration. PDF p. 1
  3. PJM says its proposal is still under development and invites continued work with the PJM team.
    I encourage you to continue to work with the PJM team as our proposal is still under development. PDF p. 1

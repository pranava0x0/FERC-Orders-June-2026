EL26-71-000 · 2026-11-13 · Valley Electric Association, Inc.

Valley Electric asks FERC to rescind the CAISO show cause order as to it

Valley Electric Association (VEA), an electric cooperative in the CAISO area that says it is not a public utility under section 201(f), asks FERC to rescind the June 18, 2026 show cause order as to VEA. It also conditionally asks for more time to respond.

Asks FERC to: Rescind the show cause order as to VEA. If FERC has not acted by November 16, 2026, extend VEA's response time until thirty days after FERC rules on the motion.

Asks FERC to remove VEA from the E-10 CAISO show cause proceeding rather than answer its directives. FERC's order said it would rescind at a non-public utility's request.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. VEA asks FERC to rescind the show cause order as to VEA, citing the Commission's statement that it would do so at a non-public utility's request.

    VEA is an electric cooperative that is not a public utility under Section 201(f) of the Federal Power Act PDF p. 2
  3. VEA says any large load in its territory would connect to the 230 kV system of GridLiance West, a public utility that remains a respondent.
    Any large load seeking service in VEA’s territory would, either directly or through VEA, interconnect to the 230 kV system of GridLiance West LLC (“GLW”), a public utility that remains a respondent in this proceeding, rather than to VEA’s 138 kV facilities. PDF p. 3
  4. If FERC has not acted by November 16, 2026, VEA conditionally asks for a brief extension of its time to respond until the Commission rules.
    If the Commission has not acted on this motion by November 16, 2026, VEA conditionally requests, pursuant to Rule 2008, a brief extension of its time to respond to the Show Cause Order until the Commission rules. PDF p. 3

    VEA has conferred with the CAISO in advance of this filing and is authorized to state that the CAISO supports this motion. PDF p. 7
  6. VEA concludes a section 205 filing is not warranted for it.

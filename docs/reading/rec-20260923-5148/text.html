Clean Energy contends that the proposal does not adequately ensure that “zero means zero,” emphasizing the possibility of injections during and outside the Transient Tolerance Window and requesting additional penalties for unauthorized injections.27

That argument conflates the scope of the Interconnection Service MISO proposes to provide with the physical response of a Generating Facility following a transient event.  ZGIS provides no general right to inject energy onto the Transmission System and does not convey Transmission Service.28  ZGIS permits the Generating Facility to operate only for the limited purpose of delivering energy to its designated ZG Associated Load.29

The Transient Tolerance Window does not alter those service limitations.

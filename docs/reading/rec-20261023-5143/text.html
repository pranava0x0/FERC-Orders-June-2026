As noted in the LARS Filing, the addition of new large loads to the system has the potential to strain the region’s resource adequacy needs, with new large load additions coming online faster than the generation needed to support them.8  By studying the generation and load in a parallel, coordinated, and streamlined manner, MISO’s LARS proposal can reduce study times for both load and generation, and identify cost-effective solutions for interconnection that are more efficient than if load and generation were studied separately.9

The MISO Transmission Owners support MISO’s LARS proposal.  As explained in the LARS Filing, the Commission should accept the proposed Tariff revisions, effective December 2, 2026, to ensure new large loads paired with generation can be studied in streamlined, efficient, and expedited manner.

II. COMMENTS IN SUPPORT
The MISO Transmission Owners support MISO’s LARS Filing.  The LARS Filing will provide the region with another efficient, effective, and appropriately tailored tool to address the need to expedite the interconnection of new large loads and the necessary generation to serve them.  The proposed LARS process provides a framework to a parallel, streamlined, and coordinated study process for pairing new load with much needed generation projects.  The pairing of new load with generation for large load customers is part of a broader suite of efforts on the part of MISO and MISO stakeholders to address resource adequacy and reliability needs across the MISO footprint while providing a pathway to bring new large loads online as fast as possible. A. The LARS Proposal Is Just and Reasonable Given It Is Narrowly
Tailored in Scope, Avoids Negative Impacts on the Generator
Interconnection Queue, and Facilitates Speed to Power
The LARS proposal is a narrowly tailored but valuable tool that enables the accelerated evaluation and integration of electrically proximate large load and generation pairs and the related identification of more efficient network upgrades.  As a new permanent study process, the LARS proposal will provide for a new Large Load Generator Interconnection Agreement in an estimated 120 days.13  The LARS proposal is a balanced and necessary addition to MISO’s tools for addressing exponential load growth across the region.

1. Urgent Need to Interconnect New Large Loads and Associated
Generation
MISO and MISO stakeholders, including the MISO Transmission Owners, fully agree on the need to meet the moment by facilitating the interconnection of new large loads, such as data centers, without jeopardizing resource adequacy and reliability needs.14  The urgent resource adequacy and reliability needs that led to the LARS proposal are in part due to recent, rapid changes to the MISO footprint.  Specifically, recent assessments by MISO demonstrate that a combination of factors, including, among other things, the rapid

2. LARS Is an Appropriate, Focused, and Needed Solution to Bringing
on New Large Loads and Generation in a Timely Manner

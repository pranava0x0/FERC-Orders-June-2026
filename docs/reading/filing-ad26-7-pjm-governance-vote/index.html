Responses and related views

States object to members controlling access to FERC

OPSI and PJM Governors’ Collaborative governance negotiating team · Related public statement

Before the vote, the states’ negotiating team opposed the members’ term sheet. The team said the proposed supermajority requirement would let private members decide whether a state proposal received equal consideration at FERC. It preferred a direct route for states to be heard.

PDF p. 1, September 29 letter

LaCerte points to gridlock in PJM’s stakeholder process

Commissioner David LaCerte · Related public statement

At WIRES on July 9, LaCerte said PJM’s stakeholder process too often protected individual business interests and prevented agreement. He was discussing governance months before the members voted, not evaluating their final term sheet.

July 9 WIRES keynote, 00:40:00

Rosner links investment signals to state confidence

Commissioner David Rosner · Related public statement

At the July hearing Rosner told Senator McCormick that market rules can send investment signals, but those signals lose force if states do not trust how prices are set. He wanted states involved in regional planning.

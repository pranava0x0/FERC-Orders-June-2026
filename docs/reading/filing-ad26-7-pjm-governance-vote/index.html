PJM informational filing on the September 30, 2026 Members Committee vote on governance term sheets

PJM informs FERC that its Members Committee approved a Members term sheet by a two-thirds sector-weighted vote on September 30, 2026, in the confidential governance talks under AD26-7, and that state representatives find it unsatisfactory.

Background. A PJM governance update in AD26-7. It has no direct tie to a show cause directive, though it concerns the stakeholder process surrounding PJM's large-load filings.

Key points

  1. Confidential talks facilitated by FERC's Dispute Resolution Service began September 1.
    on September 1st, representatives of PJM, the states in the region, Members, and stakeholders commenced confidential and privileged discussions facilitated by FERC's Dispute Resolution Service. PDF p. 1
  2. The Members term sheet won the required two-thirds vote over the PJM and Consumer Advocates term sheets.
    The Members term sheet, sponsored by a group of PJM Members, received the requisite sector-weighted two-thirds majority vote of the PJM Members Committee PDF p. 1
  3. PJM reports that the Organization of PJM States and the Governors' Collaborative do not view the approved term sheet as satisfactory.
    the Organization of PJM States, Inc., the PJM Governors' Collaborative and other state representatives do not view the approved term sheet as satisfactory. PDF p. 1

ER27-533-000 · 2026-11-16 · GridLiance West LLC

GridLiance West - Response to Show Cause Order EL26-71

GridLiance West LLC files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in ER27-533-000, answering the CAISO show cause order. It adds large load procedures, a pro forma cost recovery agreement and an interim service, and asks to be dropped from the proceeding. The letter closely mirrors the Horizon West and Trans Bay Cable filings, apart from company facts and minor wording.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective February 16, 2027, allow 30 days for comments, and terminate the show cause proceeding as to GridLiance West.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-10 show cause order. It follows CAISO's draft tariff language and sets an application process, a cost recovery agreement and an interim service for a non-load-serving owner.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Cost-recovery agreement (§ III.B.3, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Service for flexible loads (§ III.D, p. 62) E-10 CAISO: BTMG netting (pp. 60-61, p. 60)

Key points

  1. GridLiance West runs about 165 miles of 230 kV line in southern Nevada, inside the CAISO grid.
    The HVTS consists of approximately 165 miles of 230-kV transmission lines and related substation infrastructure that runs through rural Southern Nevada PDF p. 2
  2. It says parts of the show cause order do not apply to a non-load-serving owner, and asks to be dropped from the proceeding.
    GridLiance West also explains how certain aspects of the Show Cause Order are not applicable to GridLiance West due to its role as a Non-Load-Serving Entity PTO PDF p. 2
  3. It copies CAISO's 50 MW definition of large load.
    This definition is identical to the definition CAISO proposes to add to Appendix A of the CAISO Tariff, and adopts the 50 MW threshold the Commission identified as reasonable PDF p. 6
  4. Because it serves no retail load, it offers unbundled retail transmission service so large loads can be studied under its tariff.
    a voluntary offer of unbundled retail transmission service, where consistent with state law, so that Large Loads can request Interconnection and be studied under the GridLiance West Tariff PDF p. 6
  5. Study deposits start at $35,000 plus $1,000 per MW, matching CAISO's generator process.
    must provide a refundable study deposit of: (a) $35,000 plus $1,000 per MW PDF p. 8
  6. The interim service depends on the load's ability to reduce at a point of interconnection, under a three-party agreement.
    FILI Service is available if the Large Load can reduce its load at a specified Point of Interconnection according to protocols in a FILI Service Agreement among the Large Load, GridLiance West, and CAISO PDF p. 13
  7. The customer pays all actual network upgrade costs through a revenue commitment.
    The Cost Recovery Agreement requires the Eligible Customer to pay 100 percent of the actual costs of the Large Load Network Upgrades through the Total Required Revenue Commitment PDF p. 14
  8. Retail credit support does not count toward the security, because it holds no retail agreement and could not enforce it.
    GridLiance West has not proposed to allow an Eligible Customer to satisfy the financial security required under the Cost Recovery Agreement with credit support posted under a retail agreement PDF p. 15

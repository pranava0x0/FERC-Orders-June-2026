ER27-330-001 · 2026-11-12 · PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.

PJM moves to withdraw its October backstop filing in favor of ER27-454

PJM Interconnection moves to withdraw its October 29, 2026 filing and October 30 amendment in ER27-330. It says it resubmitted the same tariff revisions in ER27-454 after using the wrong eTariff filing codes.

Asks FERC to: Withdraw the October 29 filing and its amendment in ER27-330, and treat ER27-454 as the proceeding for the same tariff revisions.

Concerns PJM's reliability backstop procurement tariff rules and a filing-code error. The motion never cites the E-7 show cause order.

E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8)

Key points

  1. PJM moves to withdraw its October 29, 2026 filing in ER27-330.
    withdraw its October 29, 2026 filing in this proceeding PDF p. 1
  2. PJM says it used eTariff codes for compliance filings instead of code 10 for a new section 205 proceeding.
    PJM inadvertently used eTariff filing codes applicable to compliance filings and not eTariff filing code 10 applicable to the initiation of a new proceeding under section 205. PDF p. 2
  3. PJM says the refiling only corrects metadata and does not change the substance of the October 29 filing.
    Such refiling to correct the metadata does not in any way affect the substance of the October 29 Filing. PDF p. 2
  4. PJM says the re-submittal was driven only by the need to revise the metadata.
    To be clear, the re-submittal was driven by the sole purpose of making the ministerial revision to the metadata. PDF p. 2

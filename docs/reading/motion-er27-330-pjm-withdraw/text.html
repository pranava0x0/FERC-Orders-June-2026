MOTION TO WITHDRAW FILING AND TERMINATE PROCEEDING  
IN DEFERENCE TO NEW DOCKET NO. ER27-454-000  
 
 
Pursuant to Rule 212 of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s 
(“Commission”) Rules of Practice and Procedures,1 and section 35.17(a) of the 
Commission Rules and Regulations,2 PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (“PJM”) moves to 
withdraw its October 29, 2026 filing in this proceeding,3 and its related October 30, 2026 
amendment,4 in which PJM proposed revisions to the PJM Open Access Transmission 
Tariff (“Tariff”), pursuant to Federal Power Act (“FPA”) section 205,5 to update the 
Reliability Backstop Procurement (“RBP”) rules to address, inter alia, issues identified in 
the Commission’s September 29 Order.6  PJM has resubmitted these same Tariff revisions 
in Docket No. ER27-454-000.7

Good cause exists to grant this motion.  The Commission’s regulations permit a 
public utility to withdraw a pending rate filing that “has not become effective and upon 
which no Commission or delegated order has been issued.”8  That is the case here.  
While the October 29 initial filing and October 30 amendment were submitted as a 
new, independent proposal under FPA section 205, PJM inadvertently used eTariff filing 
codes applicable to compliance filings and not eTariff filing code 10 applicable to the 
initiation of a new proceeding under section 205.  PJM understands that an eTariff filing 
code 10 is necessary to facilitate the Commission’s orderly review and processing of 
proposals for new Tariff revisions under FPA section 205.  
To correct PJM’s clerical error and facilitate the Commission’s review of PJM’s 
proposed updates to the RBP rules, PJM re-submitted in Docket No. ER27-454-000 the 
same eTariff records proposed in the October 29 Filing, effective January 29, 2027, and 
February 28, 2027, as requested.  To be clear, the re-submittal was driven by the sole 
purpose of making the ministerial revision to the metadata.9  Such refiling to correct the 
metadata does not in any way affect the substance of the October 29 Filing.  Accordingly, 
this docket is no longer necessary, as the same Tariff revisions have been properly filed in 
Docket No. ER27-454-000.10

PJM notes that the Commission established the same comment and intervention 
deadline of November 30, 2026, for the submittal in Docket No. ER27-454-000 as the 
Commission had previously established for the revisions in these proceedings.11  As such, 
no party will be harmed by the termination of this docket and addressing the same proposal 
in new Docket No. ER27-454-000. 
 
For the reasons stated above, PJM requests that the Commission grant this motion 
to withdraw and terminate the docket.

Connie Bohl, Manchester, OH.
Hello, I live in Sprigg Township where the Buck Canyon data center is being put in. I’m against these mega data centers for several reasons but electricity is what I want to talk about in these comments. Where is the electricity going to come from for these massive data centers? Are you going to pass the cost for this down to the consumers who live near these monsters? If so, and I do believe that is likely the case, how can you justify multi billionaires paying a portion, at the most from what I’m hearing, for the infrastructure and their bills? These are multi BILLIONAIRES forcing these things on us while our elected officials are silent. To me, there is no justification for these mega monsters to do anything but pay 100% of ALL their costs. Infrastructure and their bills. Why give them a discount? My sincere hope is that our elected officials change this and that these mega data centers never go in. They have already proven what can and does happen to the grid in a few different places and I hope they never get to do that here or anywhere else for that matter. I would ask that they pay what is THEIRS to pay, 100% of it across the board with whatever protections for the citizens that you can provide. If this was your average citizen they would be required to pay every, single, penny and that’s after our rates have went up over the last few years. To summarize, why are these MEGA DATA CENTERS more important than the citizens who pay our bills every month and it is 100% on us to keep the electric on. Please choose the people in this situation and hold them 100% percent responsible for their wish list. 
Thank you, 
Connie Bohl

Combined Notice of Filings #1

FERC's November 10, 2026 Combined Notice of Filings lists many routine electric rate filings. Two PJM entries matter here: the ER26-3515-002 compliance filing on the Large Load definition and registry, and the ER27-454 revised Reliability Backstop Procurement rules.

Changes the schedule. Sets the comment date of November 30, 2026 for PJM's compliance filing on the Large Load definition and registry in ER26-3515, which supplies the Large Load definition the E-7 study-process directive depends on.

E-7 PJM: Application / study process (P 6(a), p. 7)

Key points

  1. FERC notices PJM's compliance filing on the Large Load definition and registry, filed November 9 with comments due November 30, 2026.
    Compliance filing: Compliance Filing re Large Load Definition and Large Load Registry to be effective 10/12/2026. Source text
  2. FERC notices PJM's new Section 205 filing revising the backstop procurement rules from ER26-3380, effective January 29, 2027.
    §205(d) Rate Filing: Revised Reliability Backstop Procurement Rules in ER26-3380-000 to be effective 1/29/2027. Source text
  3. Interventions and protests are due by 5:00 pm Eastern on each comment date.

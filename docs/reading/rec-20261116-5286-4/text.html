1  Annual Supplemental MTRC billing 
Complete one calculation for each Covered Upgrade and provide a customer total. 
The prior-period true-up is the customer-level Supplemental MTRC adjustment based on actual Qualifying 
Transmission Revenues, excluding prior-period adjustments already included in the billed comparison 
amount, and is calculated without interest. It excludes the ordinary formula-rate true-up and its interest, and 
unpaid balances already billed. This Charge Statement instructs billing; it does not establish cash receipts 
for the Annual Update. An unpaid invoice remains outstanding. Recovery of a collection shortfall through the 
RTFC or the ordinary formula-rate true-up does not create a second CRA charge or discharge the Eligible 
Customer’s unpaid invoice. Any later recovery is credited through the formula rate when received, without 
duplicating a previously recognized receipt.

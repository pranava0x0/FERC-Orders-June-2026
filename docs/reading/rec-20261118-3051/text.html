ERRATA NOTICE EXTENDING COMMENT PERIOD

(November 18, 2026)

	On November 16, 2026, the Commission issued a notice in the above-captioned proceeding.  Combined Notice of Filings #1 November 16, 2026 (November 16 Combined Notice).  The comment due date on the November 16 Combined Notice inadvertently reflects 5 pm ET 12/7/26.  This errata notice hereby corrects and extends the comments due date to 5 pm ET 1/15/27.





Carlos D. Clay,

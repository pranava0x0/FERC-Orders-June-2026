ER27-517-000 · 2026-11-16 · New York Independent System Operator, Inc.

NYISO files large-load integration procedures and market rules under section 205

NYISO files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in a new docket, ER27-517-000. They set up Large Load Integration Procedures, two new withdrawal services instead of the three PJM-style services, curtailment of non-firm service when the local price reaches $200/MWh, and a State Exempt Load Project exception for New York's resource adequacy rules. The New York transmission owners filed cost recovery terms separately.

Asks FERC to: Accept without modification, effective from February 1, 2027 to May 1, 2029 by part; keep the show cause case in abeyance, end it for NYISO on acceptance, and allow 28 days for comments.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-12 show cause order for NYISO. The New York transmission owners and the non-incumbent owners filed their own parts separately the same day.

E-12 NYISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-12 NYISO: Define ‘large load’ (P 64, p. 41) E-12 NYISO: Timely study window (P 44, p. 31) E-12 NYISO: Proximate-generation service (P 121, p. 72) E-12 NYISO: Alternative transmission technologies (P 68, p. 79) E-12 NYISO: Cost transparency (P 73, p. 47)

    “Firm Withdrawal Service” and “Non-Firm Withdrawal Service” PDF p. 2
  3. NYISO explains why it will not adopt the three PJM co-location services.
    Part V explains why the NYISO is not proposing to adopt the three new transmission services that the Commission established in the PJM Co-Location Order PDF p. 3
  4. Non-firm service would be curtailed when the local price reaches $200 per megawatt-hour.
    establish a rule under which Non-Firm Withdrawal Service would be curtailed when the applicable nodal Locational- Based Marginal Price (“LBMP”) for a Large Load reaches $200 MW/h PDF p. 3
  5. The New York transmission owners file a separate two-party cost recovery agreement.
    the NYTOs will submit a new two-party pro forma cost recovery agreement to address the cost- shifting issues discussed in the Show Cause Order PDF p. 3
  6. NYISO's study cycle would take about 18 to 24 months, with a 90-day system impact study inside it.
    between one year and six months and two years, including a 90-day period for the performance of the System Impact Study PDF p. 14
  7. The new study process includes a description of how alternative transmission technologies will be considered.
    (x) describe how alternative transmission technologies will be considered PDF p. 2
  8. NYISO ties paired generation to the same capacity region as the load instead of using the order's electrically proximate test.
    use Associated Resource rules tied to NYISO Capacity Regions in lieu of the Show Cause Order’s “electrically proximate resource PDF p. 3
  9. A partner resource serving a large load must be in the same capacity region as that load.
    for a Partner Resource Arrangement, the Resource must be located in the same Capacity Region as the given Large Load. PDF p. 42
  10. NYISO adds a co-located load and generator arrangement that lets the load take firm withdrawal service.

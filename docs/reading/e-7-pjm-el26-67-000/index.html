Responses and related views

Who pays when a data center brings its own generator?

Commissioner David LaCerte · Related public statement

Answering Senator Cotton, LaCerte said beneficiaries should pay for the new infrastructure and states should hold hyperscalers to their commitments in retail rates. He did not specify a PJM cost formula.

PDF pp. 79–80, printed pp. 75–76

Pennsylvania’s consumer advocate challenges regional cost sharing

Pennsylvania Office of Consumer Advocate · Direct response

The office asked FERC to clarify that a customer agreement is only one protection. It wanted parties to be able to show that existing regional allocation methods themselves charge other customers for network upgrades needed by a large load.

PDF pp. 2–5

Four state offices want the full upgrade cost charged to the new load

Maryland Office of People’s Counsel, Ohio Consumers’ Counsel, Delaware Division of the Public Advocate and Illinois Attorney General · Direct response

The four offices backed FERC’s transparency directives but said disclosure alone leaves existing cost shifts intact. They asked that agreements require full payment for the necessary upgrades and that other customers receive the corresponding revenue credits promptly.

PDF pp. 1–4, section III.B challenge

An Indiana representative asks for federal review of large-load projects

Rep. Victoria Spartz (Indiana’s 5th Congressional District) · Direct response

Spartz asked FERC and DOE to scrutinize potentially duplicate load forecasts and to require developers to pay attributable generation and transmission costs. Her November letter represents a member of Congress, not Indiana’s utility commission.

PDF pp. 1–4

Rosner wants commitments that survive a canceled project

Commissioner David Rosner · Direct response

Rosner argued that a cost recovery agreement should prevent households from paying for a network upgrade if the data center never opens. He invited alternative regional mechanisms if they offered comparable protection.

PDF p. 91, Rosner concurrence

ER27-25-000 · 2026-10-23 · Alliant and Consumers Energy

Comments of Alliant Energy Corporate Services, Inc. and Consumers Energy Company in Support of MISO's Proposed Tariff Revisions

Alliant Energy Corporate Services and Consumers Energy, both load-serving entities in MISO, file comments supporting LARS as a way to study large loads and paired generation together, with continued stakeholder work on how projects keep interconnection rights.

Asks FERC to: Find MISO's LARS proposal just and reasonable and accept it.

Answers the order. Supports MISO's LARS filing, which MISO presents as its answer to the E-8 show cause order on operational dynamics of serving electrically proximate large loads.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The utilities support a study process that handles generation paired with large load together.
    support the need for an interconnection process that facilitates the timely study of generation paired with an associated large load(s) to arrive at optimized transmission solutions. PDF p. 1
  2. Joint planning can avoid duplicate or costly upgrades and lower customer costs.
    eliminate duplicative or unnecessarily expensive upgrades, and help deliver lower-cost outcomes for customers. PDF p. 2
  3. They cite MISO's goal of finishing the parallel study in about 120 days.
    with the objective of completing the process within approximately 120 days. PDF p. 2
  4. They back strong readiness requirements to keep speculative projects out.
    Strong readiness requirements are therefore an appropriate safeguard against speculative projects consuming scarce study resources PDF p. 3
  5. They expect MISO to keep working with stakeholders on how projects retain interconnection rights over time.
    providing greater certainty regarding the conditions under which projects may retain interconnection rights over time. PDF p. 3

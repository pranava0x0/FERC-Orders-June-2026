NOTICE OF DENIAL OF REHEARING BY OPERATION OF LAW AND PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION

(August 20, 2026)

	Rehearing has been timely requested of the Commission’s order issued on 
June 18, 2026, in this proceeding.  ISO New Eng. Inc., 195 FERC ¶ 61,215, errata notice, 196 FERC ¶ 61,012 (2026).  In the absence of Commission action on a request for rehearing within 30 days from the date it is filed, the request for rehearing may be deemed to have been denied.  16 U.S.C. § 825l(a); 18 C.F.R. § 385.713 (2025); Allegheny Def. Project v. FERC, 964 F.3d 1 (D.C. Cir. 2020) (en banc).

As provided in 16 U.S.C. § 825l(a), the request for rehearing of the above-cited order filed in this proceeding will be addressed in a future order to be issued consistent with the requirements of such section.  As also provided in 16 U.S.C. § 825l(a), the 






Commission may modify or set aside its above-cited order, in whole or in part, in such manner as it shall deem proper.  



Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Secretary.

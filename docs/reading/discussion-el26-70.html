Each is a plain summary of one filing, checked against its pages. Where a stance is given, it is what the filing asks FERC to do with the proposal.

AMP wanted an enforceable route back to the show cause case

American Municipal Power · EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-14

AMP opposed an unconditional delay and asked FERC to require a compliance filing. The order says FERC would return to the section 206 case for any preliminary finding MISO’s tariff filings did not address.

What MISO’s regional report leaves out

Organization of MISO States · EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-14

OMS said the report does not fully reflect state proceedings on resource planning, retail rates and utility approvals because MISO is not a party to those proceedings. It wanted regional planning to complement, rather than displace, state responsibility for adequacy.

Support for more time came with a reservation

Organization of MISO States · EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-07

OMS supported time for a coordinated sequence of filings but said granting abeyance should neither narrow the inquiry nor prejudge that the coming tariff changes would settle every issue.

Eolian wants nearby generation considered for permanent service

Eolian · EL26-70-001 · 2026-09-21

Eolian sought clarification that generation located electrically close to a large load could qualify for permanent limited service if it could constrain grid withdrawals over the long term. FERC dismissed the request because the preliminary order was not final.

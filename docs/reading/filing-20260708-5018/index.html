EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-08 · Individual No Affiliation

Supplemental Technical Comment of L.M. Marlowe on the integration of large, high-density electric loads and the associated reliability, observability, and cost-allocation methodologies under consideration by the Commission

An individual commenter filing as L.M. Marlowe submits a supplemental technical comment across the six show cause dockets, urging boundary telemetry, cost-causation allocation and a four-gate verification standard at the point of interconnection, and reserves rights in her framework.

Asks FERC to: Consider continuous boundary telemetry and a four-gate verification standard in the large-load interconnection and cost-allocation methods, and note the commenter's reservation of intellectual property rights.

Answers the order. Comment filed in EL26-67 and the other show cause dockets; its cost-causation and observability proposals bear on the cost-shifting and transparency directives and curtailment questions raised by the June orders.

E-7 PJM: Cost-recovery agreement (P 77, p. 28) E-7 PJM: Cost transparency (P 71, p. 51) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56)

Key points

  1. She argues reliability checks should move from periodic review to continuous validation at the interconnection boundary, since large computational loads change state in sub-cycle time.
    reliability verification should migrate from periodic review toward continuously operating validation performed at the interconnection boundary, sampled at sub-cycle resolution PDF p. 3
  2. Reliability burdens from large computational loads should be assigned to the loads that cause them, not to residential and small-commercial ratepayers.
    should be assigned to the loads that cause them, on a cost-causation basis, rather than socialized onto residential and small-commercial ratepayers. PDF p. 4
  3. She asks whether curtailment priority could place federally directed computational load ahead of ordinary large loads, citing DOE's 15-minute backup-generation curtailment of loads of 50 MW or more.
    directed curtailment of data centers and large loads of 50 MW or greater onto backup generation within fifteen minutes of an emergency signal PDF p. 4
  4. She proposes four verification gates at the point of common coupling: timing and stability, dependency map, throughput settlement, and certification.
    Such a standard would operate as a sequence of verification gates applied at interconnection, each addressing a specific consideration raised in this comment PDF p. 7
  5. The commenter reserves rights in a proprietary framework covered by pending patent applications.
    Commenter reserves all rights, including the right to seek appropriate relief in the proper forum. PDF p. 9

EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-13 · FERC

Errata correcting the caption and footnote 11 of 195 FERC ¶ 61,212 (Wabash Valley Power Association)

FERC's Secretary issues an errata notice correcting the caption and footnote 11 of the E-8 MISO show cause order, so Wabash Valley Power Association is listed among entities not required to respond as Transmission Owners.

Changes the schedule. Corrects who counts as a Transmission Owner that must respond to the E-8 show cause order; MISO itself must still respond about non-public utilities' Tariff provisions.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The caption wrongly included Wabash Valley Power Association, and footnote 11 omitted it.
    The caption in the order inadvertently included “Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc.” and footnote 11 inadvertently omitted “Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc.” Source text
  2. The notice deletes Wabash from the caption and adds it to footnote 11.
    This errata notice hereby corrects the caption as shown above, by deleting “Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc.” Source text
  3. FERC keeps authority to review non-public-utility entities' formula rates as part of MISO's Tariff.
    the Commission retains the authority to review each entity’s formula rates as a component of MISO’s Tariff in an order to show cause directed towards MISO Source text

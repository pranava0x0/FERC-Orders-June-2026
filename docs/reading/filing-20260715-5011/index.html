EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-15 · Relentless Energy Systems LLC

Comments of Relentless Energy Systems LLC providing technical evidence and Xyce netlists demonstrating Geometric Phase Isolation, a Grid Enhancing Technology, for passive hardware-level VAR-offloading at Point-of-Load

Relentless Energy Systems LLC submits technical evidence and circuit netlists for Geometric Phase Isolation, a passive resonant device it says offloads reactive power at the point of load, as a grid enhancing technology for large-load interconnection.

Asks FERC to: Have Commission engineering staff review the Geometric Phase Isolation evidence as a grid enhancing technology applicable to large-load interconnection constraints.

Answers the order. Filed across the show cause dockets as a grid enhancing technology; it bears on the E-7 directive to evaluate alternative transmission technologies in transmission service studies.

E-7 PJM: Alternative transmission technologies (P 70, p. 70)

Key points

  1. Relentless submits solver-verified models of Geometric Phase Isolation as a grid enhancing technology tied to the show cause orders.
    technical evidence and Xyce netlists and results to assist regional transmission organizations and the Commission in mitigating the infrastructure impacts of large loads PDF p. 1
  2. The company says the device offloads reactive power at the load and increases interconnection capacity without new rights-of-way.
    materially increases available interconnection capacity without requiring new transmission rights‑of‑way. PDF p. 2
  3. The submission lists three document groups: netlist sweeps, a resilient-resonance netlist, and a non-provisional patent application.
    The following three (3) groups of documents are included in this public eFiling for technical review by Commission engineering staff PDF p. 2

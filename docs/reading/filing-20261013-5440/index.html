ER26-3650-000 · 2026-10-13 · Midcontinent Independent System Operator Inc.

Motion for Leave to Answer and Answer of Midcontinent Independent System Operator Inc. to the Comments and Protests re the proposal to establish Interconnection Reliability Requirements for Large Loads

MISO answers the comments and protests on its large-load reliability proposal, defends its definitions, transition provisions and Tariff/BPM split, and asks FERC to accept the proposal unchanged.

Asks FERC to: Accept the answer and accept the IRR Proposal without modification or condition, effective December 4, 2026.

Answers the order. MISO's reply to protests of its first filing after the E-8 order, defending it as a response to the order's findings on large load definition and ongoing operational requirements.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. MISO says the pleadings broadly support the proposal and only one protest asks for rejection with refiling by end of 2027.
    only one protest requests the revisions to be rejected and MISO directed to file revised requirements by the end of 2027. PDF p. 2
  2. MISO says the comments offer alternatives but do not show the proposal fails section 205.
    but do not establish that the framework proposed by MISO fails to satisfy the requirements of Section 205 of the Federal Power Act. PDF p. 3
  3. MISO asks that the proposal be accepted without modification and without expanding the proceeding to other large-load issues.
    MISO respectfully requests that the IRR Proposal be accepted without modification and grant the requested effective date of December 4, 2026. PDF p. 3
  4. MISO ties the proposal to the E-8 findings on a large load definition and ongoing operational requirements.
    a definition of large load sufficient to capture loads that present the unique challenges described in the Show Cause Order PDF p. 4
  5. On scope, MISO says the proposal does not create a regime for distribution-connected load not taking MISO Transmission Service.
    the IRR Proposal does not create a free-standing MISO reliability regime for distribution- connected load that is not taking MISO Transmission Service. PDF p. 27
  6. MISO's conclusion requests acceptance without modification or condition.
    MISO respectfully requests that the Commission (i) accept this Answer and (ii) accept the IRR Proposal without modification or condition. PDF p. 34

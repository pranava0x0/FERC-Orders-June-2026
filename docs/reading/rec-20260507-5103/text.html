I. MOTION FOR LEAVE TO ANSWER
While Rule 213(a)(3) of the Commission’s rules of practice and procedure provides that an “answer may be made to any pleading, if not prohibited under paragraph (a)(2) of this section,”8 Rule 213(a)(2) precludes, as a matter of right, an answer to a protest or answer.9  The Commission, however, will permit such answers for good cause shown.10  Good cause exists when the response will assist the Commission in its decision-making process, such as by helping to explain the relevant issues or otherwise facilitating the development of the record.11  Good cause exists here to allow ERock to respond to the April 24 Reply Brief in order to address several erroneous arguments made by PJM, and to facilitate the development of the record.

II. ANSWER

At the heart of PJM’s proposed implementation of Interim NITS lies a fundamental disconnect between what PJM is proposing and what the Commission directed PJM to implement in the December 18 Order. III. CONCLUSION
PJM should not be allowed to openly flout the Commission’s unequivocal directives in the December 18 Order.  The Commission required PJM to establish “a new interim, non-firm transmission service,”35 that will allow a load that is co-located with a generator to “take service directly from the generator in the interim until both ‘graduate’ to full, front-of-meter service” so that the load will not “always have to shut down completely during peak periods.”36  Rather than complying with these and related directives, PJM’s Proposed Interim NITS Limitation undercuts them.  Accordingly, for the reasons set forth herein, ERock respectfully requests that the Commission grant its Motion, consider this Answer and prohibit PJM from imposing its Proposed Interim NITS Limitation.

EL26-67-000 · 2026-08-03 · Constellation Energy Generation, LLC

Answer of Constellation Energy Generation, LLC in support of PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.'s Motion for Abeyance

Constellation answers in support of PJM's abeyance motion with one clarification: PJM must still file complete Interim NITS provisions on August 17, 2026 in the co-location docket, and may handle Contract Demand Services on a separate track.

Asks FERC to: Grant PJM's abeyance motion with a clarification that PJM's obligation to file full Interim NITS provisions by August 17, 2026 in the co-location proceeding is unchanged.

Answers the order. Answer on PJM's E-7 abeyance motion that insists the E-2 Interim NITS compliance proceed on schedule while the E-7 flexible load service work is deferred.

E-2 PJM: Interim NITS (P 2, p. 3) E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56)

Key points

  1. Constellation supports abeyance on the condition that PJM still files a complete set of Interim NITS provisions on August 17, 2026.
    PJM remains obligated to file on August 17, 2026, a complete set of provisions to facilitate Interim Network Integration Transmission Service (“NITS”), that will allow for a prompt start of service. PDF p. 1
  2. Constellation says Contract Demand Services may need up to 90 more days while Interim NITS is basically NITS.
    developing the terms and conditions for Contract Demand Services may require an additional 90 days. PDF p. 2
  3. It asks FERC to require PJM to reconsider its proposed June 1, 2029 Interim NITS effective date.
    Reconsider its proposed June 1, 2029 effective date given that the interconnection of large loads is an “urgent” issue PDF p. 2
  4. Constellation says the two proceedings have distinct compliance obligations that PJM must not conflate.
    they are separate proceedings with distinct compliance obligations that PJM must not conflate PDF p. 2

ANSWER OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY GENERATION, LLC  
Constellation1 submits this answer to support PJM’s motion to hold the Large Load Show 
Cause Order2 proceeding in abeyance with one clarification:3 that PJM remains obligated to file 
on August 17, 2026, a complete set of provisions to facilitate Interim Network Integration 
Transmission Service (“NITS”), that will allow for a prompt start of service.4 
Constellation agrees that the implementation of Interim NITS should be prioritized and that 
Contract Demand Services may proceed on a separate track.5 Quickly implementing Interim NITS 
for new and pending large load applicants is the fastest approach to “release the bottleneck of large 
load investments across the PJM footprint”6 and obtain national and economic security. Unlike

If the Commission grants PJM’s motion, it should clarify that PJM remains obligated to 
make all directed changes to Interim NITS as applicable to Eligible Customers taking service on 
behalf of Co-Located Loads, including that PJM: 
 Reconsider its proposed June 1, 2029 effective date given that the interconnection 
of large loads is an “urgent” issue that “merit[s] serious consideration” of an 
accelerated timeframe, and explain whether Interim NITS can be “effective at an 
earlier date;”8 
 Explain “what new and existing interconnection requirements and operational 
practices” are “needed for necessary control technologies and/or protection 
systems” for Eligible Customers taking Interim NITS;9 
 Specify “curtailment priorities” for Interim NITS, and detail how PJM will 
“prioritize” Interim NITS customers for curtailment based on “duration of 
service;”10 and 
 Automatically “convert” an Eligible Customer taking Interim NITS to a Network 
Customer when Network Upgrades are completed.11 
The Large Load Show Cause Order does not excuse PJM from including a full set of rates, 
terms, and conditions to facilitate the prompt implementation of service. Furthermore, while the 
Co-Located Load and Large Load proceedings have overlapping concepts,12 they are separate 
proceedings with distinct compliance obligations that PJM must not conflate,13 especially when

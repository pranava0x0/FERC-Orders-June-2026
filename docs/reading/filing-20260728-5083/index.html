EL26-70-000 · 2026-07-28 · Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc.

Motion of Wabash Valley Power Association, Inc. for Request for Issuance of an Errata of the 06/18/2026 Order Instituting Proceedings Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act

Wabash Valley Power Association asks FERC to correct the E-8 order by removing it from the caption and listing it with the non-jurisdictional transmission owners in footnote 11, because it ceased to be a public utility on January 1, 2025.

Asks FERC to: Issue an errata removing Wabash from the caption and adding it to footnote 11, or alternatively rescind the show cause order as to Wabash.

Changes the schedule. Seeks correction of who is a named respondent to the E-8 show cause order; FERC granted it in the August 13, 2026 errata notice.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. Wabash says it does not dispute the need to address large and co-located load service.
    WVPA desires to clarify that it does not dispute the need for the Commission to address issues surrounding service to large load and co-located load PDF p. 2
  2. Wabash says it left public utility status on January 1, 2025, as an entity owned by exempt cooperatives.
    it ceased to be a public utility on January 1, 2025 because it became exempt from the Federal Power Act PDF p. 4
  3. Wabash says it is similarly situated to the non-jurisdictional owners listed in footnote 11.
    WVPA is a PTO within MISO, however, it is a PTO that is similarly situated to all of the other non-jurisdictional entities listed in footnote 11 of the Order. PDF p. 4
  4. Wabash cites FERC's treatment of a similar ISO-NE request and its CAISO rescission precedent.
    an errata to the Order would be consistent with the Commission’s treatment of a request from a similarly situated non-jurisdictional entity in Docket No. EL26-72-000 PDF p. 5
  5. Wabash asks for the two corrections and, alternatively, rescission as to Wabash.
    removes WVPA from the caption of Docket No. EL26-70-000, which intended to identify only MISO and jurisdictional PTOs; and (2) adds WVPA in the list of non-jurisdictional PTO entities in footnote 11. PDF p. 6

AFFIDAVIT OF DON A. FRERKING
ON BEHALF OF
SOUTHWEST POWER POOL, INC.
1. My name is Don A. Frerking.  I am employed by Southwest Power Pool,
Inc. (“SPP”) as Lead Engineer, Regulatory Policy.  My business address is 201 Worthen Drive, Little Rock, AR 72223. In my current position I provide regulatory support, particularly regarding transmission policy and rates, for SPP’s internal leadership and external stakeholders.  In this role, I conduct research, perform rate analyses, and monitor policies and procedures related to the administration and modification of SPP’s governing documents.

2. I earned a Bachelor of Science Degree in Industrial Engineering and a Master of Business Administration with an emphasis in Finance, both from the University of Missouri-Columbia.  I am a registered Professional Engineer in the State of Missouri. Prior to beginning my employment with SPP in 2019, I was employed by Evergy, Inc. and one of its predecessor companies, Kansas City Power & Light Company, for more than thirty years in various analytical or managerial roles in the areas of valuation engineering, business development, finance and structuring, business planning, and regulatory affairs.

3. I am submitting this affidavit to support the accuracy of the information included in Attachment A to this affidavit. The gross and net transmission plant per megawatt (“MW”) estimated values for the SPP Region that are included in Attachment A were used as support for the Load Safe Harbor Limit proposed in the above referenced

PDF page 3

docket. The difference between the gross and net plant values shown in Attachment A is the amount of accumulated depreciation expense associated with the transmission assets. The transmission plant per MW values were derived from: (1) gross and net plant values and annual transmission revenue requirement (“ATRR”) values in individual Transmission Owner formula rate template updates (effective as of the August 2026); and (2) ATRR and zonal coincident peak (“CP”) load information sourced from the August 2026 Revenue Requirements and Rates (“RRR”) file.1 The loads represent the sum, across all Zones in the SPP Region in the first column and across all Zones in the SPP East Region in the second column, of the highest monthly value for each Zone during calendar year 2025. Values for gross and net plant were sourced primarily from the Transmission Owners’ formula rates. However, approximately 4% of the value was derived through estimation, based on the ratios between the gross and net plant amounts and the ATRR amounts of those Transmission Owners for which formula rate information was used, multiplied by the ATRR amounts of those Transmission Owners for which formula rate information was not used.

4. To the best of my knowledge, the information included in Attachment A is true and correct.

1 The RRR file for August 2026 can be found that the following: https://www.spp.org/Documents/77514/RRR%20For%20Bills%20October%202025%20Revenue %20Requirements%20and%20Rates-Rev%2020260818.xlsx

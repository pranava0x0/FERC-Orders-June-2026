The targeted application of ride-through requirements to Large Loads that meet the Large Computational Load definition is just and reasonable.  First, the proposed definition recognizes that ride-through and other operational requirements being developed are intended to apply only to Eligible Customers serving Large Loads with operating characteristics warranting additional reliability consideration. B. PJM Is Proposing Ride-Through Requirements Applicable to Eligible Customers Taking Transmission Service on Behalf of Large Computational Loads

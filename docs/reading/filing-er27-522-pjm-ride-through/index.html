PJM files ride-through and operating requirements for large computational loads under section 205

PJM files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in a new docket, ER27-522-000. They define Large Computational Load, set a framework for voltage and frequency ride-through and other operating requirements whose details go in PJM's manuals, and add an annual report on the new transmission services.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective January 16, 2027, severing the ride-through requirements from the rest if needed to act quickly.

Answers the order. Filed under section 205 to answer the E-7 finding that PJM's tariff lacks operating requirements for large loads, and on compliance with the E-2 co-location order's annual reporting directive.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56) E-2 PJM: One of three new co-location services (P 2, p. 3)

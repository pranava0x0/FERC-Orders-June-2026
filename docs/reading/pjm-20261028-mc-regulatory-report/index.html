MC Legal Report: Summary of Significant Filings, Legal Activity and Commission and Court Orders (Sep 14 to Oct 16, 2026)

PJM's MC Legal Report lists FERC orders and PJM filings from September 14 to October 16, 2026. It records the dismissal of rehearing of the EL26-67 show cause order, the ER26-3515 Large Load Registry order and the ER26-3380 backstop order.

Background. Summarizes PJM-side developments around the E-7 show cause order: the September 21 rehearing dismissal, the Large Load Registry order, the backstop order and DOE emergency orders touching large loads.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8)

Key points

  1. PJM reports FERC dismissed rehearing and clarification requests on the show cause order as premature, with the proceeding in abeyance through November 16, 2026.
    FERC concluded that the Show Cause Order is not a final agency action, because it made only preliminary findings and initiated further proceedings PDF p. 2
  2. The report notes FERC will give no clarification while the abeyance lets PJM's stakeholder process and section 205 filings proceed.
    FERC also declined to provide clarification while this proceeding remains in abeyance through November 16, 2026 PDF p. 2
  3. On October 9 FERC accepted part of PJM's Large Load definition and registry filing and set a November 8 compliance date for the rest.
    The Commission directed PJM to submit a compliance filing by November 8, 2026, of the tariff sections with the Large Load definition and the Large Load Registry PDF p. 1
  4. On September 29 FERC accepted the Reliability Backstop Procurement revisions effective February 28, 2027, and opened EL26-108 on cost allocation, exit responsibility and collateral.
    the Commission accepted PJM’s proposed Reliability Backstop Procurement (RBP) Tariff revisions, subject to refund and further proceedings and effective February 28, 2027. PDF p. 2
  5. PJM asked on October 12 to hold the EL26-108 paper hearing in abeyance pending its forthcoming section 205 filing.
    requesting to hold the paper hearing proceedings, and any additional briefings, in abeyance pending resolution of PJM’s forthcoming Section 205 filing PDF p. 3
  6. DOE emergency orders under FPA section 202(c) let PJM direct backup generation at large loads to run before or during an EEA 3.
    authorizing PJM to direct backup generation resources at large loads to operate as a last resort before declaring an Energy Emergency Alert (“EEA”) 3 or during an EEA 3 PDF p. 1

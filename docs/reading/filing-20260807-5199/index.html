EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-07 · American Municipal Power, Inc.

Answer of American Municipal Power, Inc. in Response to the Motion to Hold Abeyance filed by Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc. et al

American Municipal Power answers the MISO abeyance motion, saying it falls short of the Show Cause Order's standards, and asks FERC to condition any abeyance on a cost-transparency and cost-shifting proposal within 20 days and a compliance filing by November 16.

Asks FERC to: Direct MISO to present a cost-transparency and cost-shifting proposal within 20 days, vet it with states and customers, and file compliant Tariff revisions by November 16, 2026.

Answers the order. Opposes abeyance of the E-8 deadline unless MISO advances the order's cost-recovery agreement, financial security and cost-transparency directives; FERC granted abeyance but cited AMP's objection.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-8 MISO: Cost-recovery agreement (P 6(b), p. 6) E-8 MISO: Minimum cost recovery / security (P 119, p. 74)

Key points

  1. AMP says the motion fails the Show Cause Order's abeyance standards, so FERC has grounds to deny it.
    The Motion fails to provide the information required by the Commission’s June 18 Show Cause Order in the event abeyance is sought PDF p. 1
  2. AMP names the missing protections: disclosure of upgrades and costs, cost recovery agreements, financial security and stranded-cost protection.
    public disclosure of large-load-driven network upgrades and their costs, cost recovery agreements, financial security requirements, and protection of existing transmission customers from stranded costs PDF p. 2
  3. AMP calls MISO's roadmap a few lines with a vague November filing.
    This falls well short of the detailed roadmap to compliance that the Commission required to justify abeyance. PDF p. 3
  4. AMP says upgrade costs are likely in the billions and MISO has presented no cost proposals in the stakeholder process.
    Costs associated with transmission upgrades to serve large loads are likely to be in the billions of dollars. PDF p. 4
  5. AMP says municipal and cooperative transmission-dependent utilities are not protected by state commissions because zonal cost allocation is wholesale.
    Transmission dependent utilities such as municipalities and cooperatives are not protected by state utility commissions and retail tariffs PDF p. 5
  6. AMP proposes three directives to MISO.
    bring forward a specific proposal to meet its obligations under the Show Cause Order to address cost transparency and cost shifting within the next twenty days PDF p. 5

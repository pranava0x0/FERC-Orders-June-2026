Third Supplemental Notice of Commissioner-Led Technical Conference: 2026 Reliability Technical Conference, October 21, 2026 (agenda; Panel 1 on integrating large loads)

FERC gives the third supplemental notice of its October 21, 2026 Reliability Technical Conference in AD26-8. It lists pending dockets, including all six large-load show cause dockets, whose merits will not be discussed, and attaches an agenda with a large-load panel.

Background. Schedules a reliability conference whose first panel covers large load integration and which lists the EL26-67 to EL26-72 show cause dockets as pending matters not to be argued on the merits.

Key points

  1. The conference is set for October 21, 2026, in person at FERC headquarters, on bulk-power reliability and security.
    will convene its annual Commissioner-led Reliability Technical Conference, in the above-referenced proceeding, on Wednesday, October 21, 2026, from 10:00 am to 2:40 pm Eastern Daylight Time Source text
  2. Discussion may touch pending proceedings, but merits discussion will be curtailed.
    some discussions may involve issues raised in proceedings that are currently pending before the Commission. Any discussion on the merits of such proceedings will be curtailed. Source text
  3. The notice lists the show cause dockets for each region and PJM dockets ER26-3380 and ER26-3515 among the pending matters, starting with PJM's EL26-67.
    PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. Docket Nos. EL26-67-000 EL26-67-001 Source text
  4. Panel 1 asks how entities should model and plan for rapidly emerging large loads such as data centers.
    How should entities model and plan for the rapid emergence of large, energy-intensive loads, such as data centers and AI compute campuses Source text
  5. Panel 1 speakers include a PJM operations planning director.
    Christopher Pilong, Sr. Director, Operations Planning, PJM Interconnection Source text

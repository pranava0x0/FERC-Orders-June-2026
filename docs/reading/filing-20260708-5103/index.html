EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-08 · Global Capacity Alliance

Motion to Intervene and Initial Comments of Global Capacity Alliance in response to FERC's 06/18/2026 Show Cause Order

Global Capacity Alliance moves to intervene and comments on the June 18 show cause orders, proposing Front-of-the-Meter Netting and a Capacity Attribution Contract so flexible large loads can pair with co-located resources while upgrades are built.

Asks FERC to: Grant intervention and direct the RTOs and ISOs, in responding to the show cause orders, to evaluate Front-of-the-Meter Netting frameworks and independent standard-setting pathways.

Answers the order. Comment filed across the show cause dockets; its netting and interim non-firm service concept speaks to the flexible-load service and co-location topics the June orders direct RTOs to address.

E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56) E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7)

Key points

  1. GCA says a separately metered load and battery at one substation can be curtailed and dispatched at once because dispatch has no link between them.
    A 100 MW data center and a 100 MW BESS sit at the same substation on separate meters. PDF p. 1
  2. FTM-Netting rests on a telemetry shield, a post-operating-day compliance ledger and scarcity yielding in system-wide emergencies.
    Telemetry Shield: Each dispatch interval, the Market Participant (MP) dynamically adjusts the load's consumption floor via market telemetry to match the paired resource's availability PDF p. 2
  3. A private Capacity Attribution Contract bridges non-firm energization and completed network upgrades, then sunsets when the load turns firm.
    its commercial engine is the private Capacity Attribution Contract (CAC), which bridges the gap between initial non-firm energization and completed network upgrades. PDF p. 2
  4. Because the assets are separately metered they stay visible in planning models, unlike behind-the-meter setups.
    they are studied as standard, independent interconnections—fully visible to RTO planning models and subject to existing constraint analyses. PDF p. 2
  5. GCA asks FERC to direct RTO and ISO responses to evaluate FTM-Netting.
    urges the Commission to direct the RTOs and ISOs, in responding to the June 18, 2026, Show Cause Orders, to actively evaluate and incorporate Front-of-the-Meter (FTM) Netting frameworks PDF p. 3

Motion for Leave to Answer and Answer of Central Municipal Power Agency Services, et al. to Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.’s 10/13/2026 Motion for Leave to Answer and Answer

The Midwest TDUs answer MISO's October 13 answer, saying it confirms the requirements do not reach distribution-connected loads but does not fix the Tariff language or explain how agreements with Large Loads will show compliance.

Asks FERC to: Issue a deficiency letter directing MISO to address both issues and revise the proposed Tariff language to reflect the clarifications.

Answers the order. Answer in the MISO reliability-requirements docket that relies on the E-8 order's suggested large-load definition (P 58), which refers to interconnection at the transmission system.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The TDUs say MISO's answer confirms intent to exclude distribution-connected loads but does not show the Tariff language does so.
    it fails to show that this critical threshold issue is unambiguously addressed in the proposed Tariff language. PDF p. 2
  2. They say MISO gave no added clarity on how a Transmission Customer's agreement with a Large Load will assign compliance responsibilities.
    MISO’s Answer fails to provide additional clarity as to how it will effectuate a Transmission Customer’s agreement with a Large Load PDF p. 2
  3. They want the Large Load definition to say it interconnects to the transmission system, as the E-8 order's suggested definition does.
    MISO’s Tariff language should explicitly define Large Loads as those that “interconnect[] to the transmission system,” PDF p. 4
  4. They ask for a deficiency letter and Tariff revisions.
    The Commission should therefore issue a deficiency letter directing MISO to address these issues and require revisions to the proposed Tariff language PDF p. 2

Eolian wants nearby generation considered for permanent service

Eolian · Direct response

Eolian sought clarification that generation located electrically close to a large load could qualify for permanent limited service if it could constrain grid withdrawals over the long term. FERC dismissed the request because the preliminary order was not final.

Eolian request and FERC discussion (unpaginated)

Chang asks for evidence before narrowing the states’ role

Commissioner Judy Chang · Direct response

Chang supported developing a regional record. She welcomed evidence from state regulators on possible jurisdictional problems and distinguished the narrower preliminary findings from the broader federal authority contemplated in the national rulemaking.

PDF pp. 109–110, Chang concurrence

Who decides if a regulated generation solution will go forward?

New York Independent System Operator · Direct response

NYISO explained that if the market and transmission proposals cannot cover a reliability need, it may refer a non-transmission solution to the New York Public Service Commission. The state commission then considers the project and cost recovery. This is NYISO’s description of the process, not a state endorsement of a project.

PDF pp. 7–8

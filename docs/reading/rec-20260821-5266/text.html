A. 
Introduction and Summary 
The capacity challenge facing the PJM Region is serious.9  While PJM’s filing includes 
several imperfections, Constellation does not oppose PJM’s use of a one-time RBP to address the 
shortfall in the 2028/2029 Base Residual Auction (“BRA”), reduced as applicable for accepted

C. 
The Risk Is Most Acute When Available RBP UCAP Precedes or Exceeds 
Timely RBP-Related Load 
The price-formation and reliability risks are most acute when Available RBP UCAP 
exceeds the RBP-Related Load included in the Planning Parameters for the applicable RPM 
Auction.  That mismatch does not require long-term load growth to disappear—a delay alone is 
sufficient.  Even if all forecast load ultimately connects, a multi-year delay can cause RPM to 
signal reduced need for resource retention and new entry before the load arrives. 
Dr. Shanker uses a simplified example in which the full Initial RBP Target is available in 
the relevant auction but load is delayed.23  This delayed-load example shows the resulting 
problem.24  Assume, for illustration, that approximately 6.8 GW of RBP capacity becomes 
available and must enter RPM at a zero price, but only 3 GW of RBP-Related Load is reflected in 
the applicable auction parameters due to large load delays.  RPM would then reflect 3.8 GW of 
unmatched zero-offer RBP supply without the corresponding RBP-Related Load.  That clearing 
outcome can signal reduced need for retention or entry in the RPM market.  If the remaining load 
arrives several years later, some existing capacity may already have retired (or otherwise exited 
PJM), and prospective new entry may have stayed on the sidelines.  Because retirement/exit can 
occur faster than replacement capacity can be developed, PJM may again face a shortfall and 
pressure for another out-of-market procurement.25

18.10 Annual RBP Isolation and Matched Demand Adjustment 
(a) Applicability 
For each Delivery Year during the Reliability Backstop Procurement commitment period, and 
for each Base Residual Auction and Incremental Auction conducted after the Office of the 
Interconnection commits RBP UCAP MW through the Reliability Backstop Procurement, the 
Office of the Interconnection shall apply the RBP isolation and matched demand adjustment set 
forth in this section. 
(b) Determination of Available RBP UCAP MW 
Before posting the Planning Parameters for each applicable RPM Auction, the Office of the 
Interconnection shall determine Available RBP UCAP MW for the applicable Delivery Year on 
a resource-specific and aggregate basis. The determination shall account for the RBP Resource’s 
committed RBP UCAP MW, applicable Accredited UCAP, expected commercial-operation or 
registration status, deliverability, any notice submitted pursuant to Tariff, Attachment DD, 
section 5.5, approved replacement capacity, and any rescission pursuant to Tariff, Attachment 
DD, section 18.6(e). 
The Office of the Interconnection shall determine Available RBP UCAP MW for the PJM 
Region and by Zone and modeled Locational Deliverability Area. 
(c) Determination of RBP-Related Load UCAP MW 
Before posting the Planning Parameters for each applicable RPM Auction, the Office of the 
Interconnection shall determine the portion of the incremental-load cohort used to establish or 
allocate the Reliability Backstop Procurement that is included in the Planning Parameters for the 
applicable RPM Auction. The Office of the Interconnection shall convert such load to UCAP 
MW using the Forecast Pool Requirement and the existing methodologies used to determine the 
applicable PJM Region and LDA Reliability Requirements. 
The determination shall exclude load associated with Qualifying RBP Offset UCAP MW, 
approved opt-outs, FRR Entities, and load not included in the applicable RPM Auction Planning 
Parameters. Load shall not be counted more than once under this section or under any other 
Tariff provision providing for exclusion or matched treatment of New Large Load. 
(d) RTO-Wide RBP Demand Adjustment 
For each applicable RPM Auction, the RBP Demand Adjustment for the PJM Region shall equal 
the lesser of: 
1. Available RBP UCAP MW; and  
2. RBP-Related Load UCAP MW included in the Planning Parameters for such RPM 
Auction.

(e) Exclusion of Available RBP UCAP MW 
Available RBP UCAP MW shall not be included in any Sell Offer, price-forming supply curve,

To the extent Available RBP UCAP MW exceeds RBP-Related Load UCAP MW, such excess 
shall remain excluded from RPM Auction price formation and shall not produce an additional 
reduction in any RPM reliability requirement. 
(f) Incremental Auctions 
For each Incremental Auction, the Office of the Interconnection shall recalculate Available RBP 
UCAP MW, RBP-Related Load UCAP MW, and the RBP Demand Adjustment using the 
updated Planning Parameters for the applicable Delivery Year. The RBP-Adjusted Reliability 
Requirement used in the Incremental Auction shall reflect the recalculated RBP Demand 
Adjustment and all RPM commitments established in prior RPM Auctions for the same Delivery 
Year. Available RBP UCAP MW shall not be included as an RPM commitment established in a 
prior auction. 
(g) Locational Treatment 
The Office of the Interconnection shall allocate the RBP Demand Adjustment among Zones 
based on the location of RBP-Related Load included in the applicable Planning Parameters. 
For each modeled LDA, the reduction in the LDA Reliability Requirement shall not exceed: 
1. RBP-Related Load UCAP MW located in such LDA; or  
2. Available RBP UCAP MW located in, or determined under the same CETO and CETL 
assumptions used in the applicable RPM Auction to be deliverable to, such LDA.  
The Office of the Interconnection shall not count the same RBP Demand Adjustment more than 
once among nested LDAs and their parent LDAs. Any portion of the RTO-wide RBP Demand 
Adjustment that cannot support an LDA adjustment under this subsection shall not reduce the 
reliability requirement for that LDA.

Recommended new and revised defined terms 
“Available RBP UCAP MW” shall mean, for an RPM Auction for a Delivery Year, the 
aggregate UCAP MW of RBP Resources committed for such Delivery Year that the Office of the 
Interconnection determines, using the accreditation, commercial-operation or registration, 
deliverability, and availability standards that otherwise would apply in determining the quantity 
of such resources eligible to participate in that RPM Auction, are expected to be available to 
provide capacity during such Delivery Year; provided that Available RBP UCAP MW shall not 
exceed the RBP UCAP MW committed for such Delivery Year and shall exclude any 
commitment rescinded pursuant to Tariff, Attachment DD, section 18.6(e). 
“RBP-Related Load UCAP MW” shall mean, for an RPM Auction for a Delivery Year, 
exclusive of any load associated with Qualifying RBP Offset UCAP MW, an approved opt-out, 
an FRR Entity, or load otherwise excluded from the applicable RPM Auction Planning 
Parameters, the sum of all Large Loads across the PJM region in the load forecast for the 
relevant Delivery Year minus the sum of all Large Loads across the PJM region that were 
forecasted to be on the system for the 2026/27 delivery year using the load forecast that was used 
for the 2028/29 Base Residual Auction. Peak-load megawatts shall be converted to UCAP MW 
using the Forecast Pool Requirement and the applicable existing zonal and LDA methodologies 
for such Delivery Year. 
“RBP Demand Adjustment” shall mean, for an RPM Auction for a Delivery Year, the lesser of 
Available RBP UCAP MW and RBP-Related Load UCAP MW, as determined and allocated to 
the PJM Region, Zones, and affected Locational Deliverability Areas in accordance with Tariff, 
Attachment DD, section 18.10.

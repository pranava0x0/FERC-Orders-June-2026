ANSWER OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY GENERATION, LLC
Constellation1 submits this answer to support PJM’s motion extending the compliance
deadline for implementing changes to Contract Demand Services2 among other changes with one clarification:3 that PJM remains obligated to file on August 17, 2026, a complete set of provisions

to facilitate Interim Network Integration Transmission Service (“NITS”), that will allow for a prompt start of service.

Constellation agrees that the implementation of Interim NITS should be prioritized and that Contract Demand Services may proceed on a separate track.4 Quickly implementing Interim NITS for new and pending large load applicants is the fastest approach to “release the bottleneck of large load investments across the PJM footprint”5 and obtain national and economic security. Unlike Interim NITS, which is “basically”6 NITS, developing the terms and conditions for Contract Demand Services may require an additional 90 days.

If the Commission grants PJM’s motion, it should clarify that PJM remains obligated to make all directed changes to Interim NITS as applicable to Eligible Customers taking service on behalf of Co-Located Loads, including that PJM:

 Reconsider its proposed June 1, 2029 effective date given that the interconnection of large loads is an “urgent” issue that “merit[s] serious consideration” of an accelerated timeframe, and explain whether Interim NITS can be “effective at an earlier date;”7

 Explain “what new and existing interconnection requirements and operational
practices” are “needed for necessary control technologies and/or protection
systems” for Eligible Customers taking Interim NITS;8

The Large Load Show Cause Order11 does not excuse PJM from including a full set of
rates, terms, and conditions to facilitate the prompt implementation of service. Furthermore, while the Co-Located Load and Large Load proceedings have overlapping concepts,12 they are separate proceedings with distinct compliance obligations that PJM must not conflate,13 especially when PJM did not seek rehearing.14 In other words, while Constellation supports PJM’s motion to partially delay its obligation to comply with the June 2026 Co-Location Order, that obligation is not dependent on any PJM stakeholder or other outcome in the Large Load Show Cause

proceeding.

ANSWER OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY GENERATION, LLC
Constellation submits this answer1 to respond to the Transmission Owners’ Request for
Clarification2 of the Large Load Show Cause Order.3 The Transmission Owners also request
rehearing in the above-captioned proceeding, and although Constellation disagrees with the
Transmission Owners’ rehearing arguments, including those in Exelon and FirstEnergy’s joint rehearing request,4 Constellation does not respond here because Exelon and FirstEnergy’s

arguments are premature and barred by Rule 713 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and
Procedure.5
The Transmission Owners request that with respect to the obligation to define “rates, terms, and conditions applicable to interconnection customers serving ‘electrically proximate large load’

In addition, the Commission should clarify that any eligibility requirements PJM and the Transmission Owners propose in their initial filings must not be so prescriptive as to improperly disqualify large loads, foreclosing any benefits that help avoid unnecessary transmission upgrades and reduce strain on the grid. The Commission should also clarify that Transmission Owners may not use the Point of Change in Ownership, or any other point of reference, to define “electrically proximate large load” in a manner that likewise undermines speedy interconnection or avoidance of unnecessary transmission upgrades. As the Co-Located Load proceeding demonstrates,9 clear direction from the Commission is necessary to ensure that PJM and Transmission Owners propose terms and conditions that facilitate, rather than frustrate, large load interconnections.

CONCLUSION

EL26-67-000 · 2026-08-03 · PJM Industrial Customer Coalition

Answer in Support of the PJM Industrial Customer Coalition to the Motions for Extension and Abeyance by PJM Interconnection, L.L.C

The PJM Industrial Customer Coalition files an answer supporting PJM's July 28 motions for a 90-day extension in the co-located load docket and for abeyance of the E-7 show cause proceeding, citing the overlap between the two.

Asks FERC to: Grant PJM's Co-Located Load Extension Motion and Large Load Abeyance Motion in an expedited fashion.

Changes the schedule. Answer supporting the abeyance of the E-7 60-day show cause deadline and the parallel extension in the E-2 co-location docket; FERC cited it when granting the abeyance on August 14.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-2 PJM: Interim NITS (P 2, p. 3)

Key points

  1. The Coalition agrees with PJM's coordinated approach to the two dockets, which would produce filings by mid-November.
    PJMICC agrees with PJM’s proposed approach, which facilitates administrative efficiencies and helps consolidate stakeholder and party resources and work efforts. PDF p. 2
  2. It calls 90 days proportionate, though potentially still an aggressive timeline.
    Even a 90-day extension is still potentially an aggressive timeline, but it better strikes the balance between the FERC’s urgent call for reforms and the need for time to create a durable compliance filing. PDF p. 3
  3. It also supports the Indicated Transmission Owners' parallel requests running to November 16, 2026.
    PJMICC also supports the requests of the Indicated Transmission Owners for extension and abeyance, respectively, until November 16, 2026. PDF p. 2

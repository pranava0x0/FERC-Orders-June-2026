EL26-68-000 · 2026-06-29 · Individual No Affiliation

Comments of Lisa Melton concerning the integration of large, high-density electric loads and the associated deterministic stability and cost-allocation methodologies

An individual commenter files a short comment in EL26-68 and EL26-71 urging that any cost-allocation or stability method FERC adopts assign burdens to the large loads that cause them, and reserves rights over a proprietary framework she authored.

Asks FERC to: Consider the comment and take note of the commenter's reservation of rights over her proprietary stability and diagnostic framework.

Answers the order. A pro se comment captioned in the SPP (EL26-68) and CAISO (EL26-71) show cause dockets that supports cost-causation assignment of large-load burdens and gives notice of a claimed proprietary interest.

E-9 SPP: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The commenter says the public record shows large-load burdens being socialized onto residential and small commercial ratepayers.
    the burdens created by these loads are being socialized onto residential and small-commercial ratepayers rather than assigned on a cost-causation basis. PDF p. 1
  2. She urges any adopted method to assign burden to the loads that cause it.
    assigns burden to the loads that cause it and preserves reliability invariants at the point of interconnection. PDF p. 2
  3. She reserves rights over a proprietary framework with pending patent applications and asserts no claim in these dockets.
    Commenter reserves all rights, including the right to seek appropriate relief in the proper forum. PDF p. 2

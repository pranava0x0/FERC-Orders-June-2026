EL26-69-000 · 2026-08-14 · FERC

Letter order granting the NYISO, New York Transmission Owners and Non-Incumbent Transmission Owners abeyance motions

FERC grants the abeyance motions of NYISO, the New York Transmission Owners and the Non-Incumbent Transmission Owners and holds the EL26-69 show cause proceeding for 90 days, moving responses to November 16, 2026 and answers to December 16, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Moves the E-12 NYISO 60-day show cause response from August 17 to November 16, 2026, using the 90-day abeyance option the June 18 order offered for stakeholder-developed section 205 filings.

E-12 NYISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-12 NYISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7)

Key points

  1. FERC grants all three abeyance motions filed August 3 and holds the proceeding for 90 days.

  2. NYISO plans a Management Committee vote on October 28 and a section 205 filing on November 16, 2026.
    with the final proposal presented to the Management Committee on October 28, 2026. Source text
  3. FERC finds the movants met the order's test of describing the future filing and giving a date.
    have provided a robust description of the filings they anticipate making, provided a date by which they intend to make such filings, and described the on-going stakeholder process Source text
  4. New due dates are November 16, 2026 for responses and December 16, 2026 for answers.
    responses to the Show Cause Order will be due on November 16, 2026, and answers to responses will be due on December 16, 2026. Source text
  5. A timely section 205 filing suspends the filer's duty to respond, and the proceeding stays in abeyance.
    the obligation of the respondent(s) who have made such an FPA section 205 filing to file a response to the Show Cause Order will be suspended Source text

DCC shares PJM’s commitment to ensuring that large load growth occurs in a way that is compatible with reliability and does not result in cost shifts to other customers.

But PJM’s proposal to impose a region-wide retail curtailment framework represents an unprecedented intrusion into the authority reserved to the states under the Federal Power Act (“FPA”) that must be rejected.  Facing strong opposition in this proceeding from a range of parties including state regulators, utilities, generation owners, and data center developers, PJM’s Answer attempts to recast IRAS as focused exclusively on the wholesale markets. PJM’s acknowledgment that IRAS requires enlisting Electric Distributors and state regulators to implement PJM’s directives highlights that IRAS treads into areas that are beyond PJM’s and FERC’s jurisdiction. PJM will issue directives to curtail New Large Load customers without regard to the preference of individual states. IRAS effectively conscripts state authorities into enforcing a

A. PJM’s Justifications Cannot Cure The Jurisdictional Defects With IRAS
1. IRAS Is Premised On An Overly Broad View Of FERC’s Jurisdiction
That Cannot Be Squared With The FPA
PJM argues that the jurisdictional concerns expressed by DCC and numerous other parties in this proceeding are misplaced.  According to PJM, whether IRAS “‘targets’ retail or wholesale customers turns on the justification for the rule, not on whether or the extent to which the rule affects or even compels action of retail or wholesale customers.”12  PJM then offers a series of justifications that it claims eliminate any doubts about the legality of IRAS including that it will “support[] system reliability in the PJM region” and provide a new tool to address situations where supply shortfalls jeopardize system reliability.13

It is worth pausing for a moment on the breadth of authority that PJM seeks to claim for itself.  Under PJM’s theory, the extent to which IRAS “affects or even compels action of retail” customers is immaterial to the jurisdictional analysis.14  All that matters is whether PJM can articulate some way in which its proposal will support reliability or allow it to more effectively manage its system when supply is tight.  But if the sole test for Commission jurisdiction is whether a filing utility can articulate some nexus between the rule at issue and the reliability of the

2. PJM Misinterprets Applicable Precedent Defining The Respective
Responsibilities Of FERC And The States
PJM’s arguments that the Commission need only consider PJM’s justification for IRAS are based on an unduly narrow and selective reading of applicable court and Commission precedent.  While PJM relies heavily on FERC v. EPSA, nothing in that case stands for the proposition that FERC may regulate any matter that has an impact on the reliability of wholesale markets.  To the contrary, the Court recognized that interpreting the FPA as providing FERC authority to regulate any matter that affects wholesale markets would extend FERC’s power beyond the scope of authority envisioned by Congress15 and that the FPA prohibited FERC from approving rules that target matters within the exclusive authority of the states “no matter how

F. The Commission Cannot Sever Components Of IRAS As PJM And Others
Have Proposed

Several parties—including PJM itself—make recommendations that the Commission sever
or reframe elements of PJM’s proposal in an attempt to improve its legal posture.97  But the Commission’s authority to direct modifications to PJM’s filing under Section 205 of the FPA is limited, even with PJM’s consent.  While the Commission may accept a proposed rate with “minor deviations” when the filing utility consents,98 any change that “impose[s] a new rate scheme” or follows “‘a completely different strategy’ than, or is ‘methodologically distinct’ from, the proposed rate” exceeds the Commission’s authority regardless of consent.99  This limitation exists because customers must receive “adequate notice of the proposed changes” and “an adequate opportunity to comment on the proposed changes.”100

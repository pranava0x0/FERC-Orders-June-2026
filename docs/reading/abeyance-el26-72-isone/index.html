EL26-72-000 · 2026-08-14 · FERC

Letter order granting the ISO-NE and PTO Administrative Committee joint abeyance motion

FERC grants ISO-NE and the Participating Transmission Owners a 90-day abeyance of the E-11 show cause proceeding, moving responses to November 16, 2026 and answers to December 16, 2026, and records the five reform categories ISO-NE plans to file.

Changes the schedule. Moves the E-11 order's 60-day deadline to November 16, 2026 and records ISO-NE's plan to address application and study, cost-recovery, BTMG netting, flexible service and proximate-generation study in one section 205 filing.

E-11 ISO-NE: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-11 ISO-NE: BTMG netting (P 60, p. 60) E-11 ISO-NE: Proximate-generation service (P 7, p. 7)

Key points

  1. FERC grants the August 3 abeyance motion for 90 days.
    we grant the Abeyance Motion filed by Joint Movants and hold this proceeding in abeyance for 90 days. Source text
  2. The stakeholder schedule runs through a PTO-AC vote in October and a NEPOOL Participants Committee vote in November.
    the PTO-AC plans to act on Tariff revisions in October, and the NEPOOL Participants Committee would vote on the FPA section 205 proposal at its November meeting. Source text
  3. Planned filing covers study procedures, including alternative transmission technologies, and a large load definition fitted to ISO-NE's system.
    the FPA section 205 filing will address the need for a transmission service application process and study procedures, including the evaluation of alternative transmission technologies. Source text
  4. It will bar netting behind-the-meter generation with load behind the same point of interconnection for Regional Network Service charges.
    behind-the meter generation (BTMG) cannot be netted with load behind the same point of interconnection for the purpose of determining Regional Network Service charges Source text
  5. Planned flexible services: interim non-firm network service and permanent firm and non-firm contract demand service.
    (1) interim non-firm network transmission service; (2) permanent firm contract demand transmission service; and (3) permanent non-firm contract demand transmission service. Source text
  6. Responses are due November 16, 2026 and answers December 16, 2026; a section 205 filing by November 16 suspends that respondent's duty to respond.
    responses to the Show Cause Order will be due on November 16, 2026, and answers to responses will be due on December 16, 2026. Source text

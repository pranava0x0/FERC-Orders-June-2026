EL26-71: the filed comments

Positions read for this site

Each is a plain summary of one filing, checked against its pages. Where a stance is given, it is what the filing asks FERC to do with the proposal.

Morongo Transmission LLC: Tribal transmission owner says it has no end-use customers

EL26-71-000 · 2026-08-12

Morongo said it leases transmission capacity and receives revenue through CAISO’s access charge. Because it serves no end-use customers, it argued that its own tariff needed no amendment. It still intended to participate in the proceeding.

Six Cities and Western Area Power Administration: Six municipal owners and WAPA distinguished themselves from investor-owned utilities

EL26-71-000 · 2026-08-14

These public owners committed to coordinate with CAISO and file changes if necessary. FERC rescinded the show cause order as to them; the other California respondents remained in the case.

Corporate Energy Buyers Association: Corporate buyers wanted time to test the California proposal

EL26-71-000 · 2026-08-07

CEBA backed the California parties’ requests for 90 more days. It argued that testing proposals with stakeholders before filing could improve their commercial viability and legal durability.

Eolian: Eolian wants nearby generation considered for permanent service

EL26-71-001 · 2026-09-21

Eolian sought clarification that generation located electrically close to a large load could qualify for permanent limited service if it could constrain grid withdrawals over the long term. FERC dismissed the request because the preliminary order was not final.

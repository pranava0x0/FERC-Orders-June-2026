EL26-67-001 · 2026-07-20 · Eolian, L.P.

Eolian, L.P. submits Limited Request for Clarification or, In the Alternative, Rehearing of the 06/18/2026 Order Instituting Proceedings Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act

Eolian, L.P. supports the June 18 orders and asks FERC to clarify or rehear one limit: permanent load-limited generator interconnection service for electrically proximate front-of-the-meter generation, and the flexibility RTOs have to adopt minimal generator study reforms.

Asks FERC to: Clarify that electrically proximate generation may access permanent load-limited interconnection service, that RTOs are encouraged to offer it, and that minimal generator study reforms do not suffice; otherwise grant rehearing.

Answers the order. Rehearing and clarification request across the PJM, NYISO, MISO, CAISO and ISO-NE show cause orders on generator interconnection service for generation serving electrically proximate large loads.

E-12 NYISO: Proximate-generation service (P 121, p. 72) E-11 ISO-NE: Proximate-generation service (P 7, p. 7) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56)

Key points

  1. Eolian says the orders allow permanent load-limited generator service only for co-located generation and make it temporary for electrically proximate generation.
    the Commission appears to categorically limit the availability of permanent generator interconnection services to co-located generation PDF p. 2
  2. It says limited generator service will limit uptake of the new permanent contract demand and Interim NITS services.
    large loads’ ability to make use of the new transmission services will be severely limited if new front-of-the-meter generation projects PDF p. 2
  3. Eolian asks FERC to confirm RTOs may offer permanent load-limited generator interconnection service and to encourage broad availability.
    the Commission strongly encourage RTO/ISOs to make such service broadly available. PDF p. 3
  4. Eolian asks FERC to close language that could let RTOs adopt minimal generator interconnection reforms.
    The Commission must clarify this discussion, so that it does not inadvertently create a loophole that would undermine the critical benefits of its Orders PDF p. 3
  5. Eolian says large loads cannot use the new services widely unless new generation can interconnect without queue cost and timing uncertainty.
    Large loads will not be able to transact with new front-of-the-meter resources if resource developers face significant commercial barriers to interconnection PDF p. 5

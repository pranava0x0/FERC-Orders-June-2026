Commodity fungibility is a core requirement for markets to reach efficient outcomes as the 
fungibility of the commodity allows buyers and sellers to find suitable, creditworthy 
counterparties with whom to transact on a bilateral basis, and to understand what is being

the underlying commodity, at going market prices without incurring significant transaction 
costs such as the cost for searching for the “appropriate counterparty” to meet a specific 
one-of-a-kind requirement.

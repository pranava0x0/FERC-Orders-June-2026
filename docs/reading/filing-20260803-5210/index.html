Request for Abeyance of the New York Transmission Owners and Request for Expedited Commission Action and Shortened Comment Period

The six New York Transmission Owners request a full 90-day abeyance of the EL26-69 show cause proceeding. They rely on their reserved section 205 filing rights and describe planned tariff work on cost recovery, revenue crediting, rate design, the Facilities Study and operating terms.

Asks FERC to: Hold the proceeding in abeyance for 90 days from August 17, 2026, including the 60-day response deadline, with expedited action and a shortened comment period.

Answers the order. Uses the E-12 order's abeyance option and previews the TO-side section 205 filing on cost recovery, revenue crediting, study procedures and alternative transmission technologies.

E-12 NYISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-12 NYISO: Cost transparency (P 73, p. 47) E-12 NYISO: Alternative transmission technologies (P 68, p. 79)

Key points

  1. The NYTOs file independently under section 3.10(a) of the NYISO-TO Agreement, which lets each TO file section 205 changes to recover costs and for retail access.
    section 3.10(a) confers upon each Transmission Owner the unilateral right to file with the Commission under FPA section 205 PDF p. 7
  2. They plan a cost recovery mechanism coordinated with the NYPSC, likely a pro forma agreement or tariff provisions.
    The NYTOs intend to incorporate the NYPSC's cost allocation determinations and to file with the Commission a proposed mechanism PDF p. 15
  3. Possible terms include a minimum financial contribution plus security to protect other customers if the load does not take the expected service.
    Such arrangements may include a minimum financial contribution from the large load customer or Eligible Customer, as applicable, toward the Transmission Owner's transmission revenue requirement PDF p. 16
  4. They will provide revenue credits where upgrade costs paid by a large load are also in rates paid by other customers.
    the NYTO will provide for a revenue credit to the extent the NYTO is recovering those Network Upgrade costs. PDF p. 17
  5. NYISO's planned Firm and Non-Firm Withdrawal Service would need changes to billing determinants and cost allocation.
    The different forms of service may require changes in the billing determinants, billing units, demand allocators, and cost allocation mechanisms. PDF p. 20
  6. The NYTOs will address Facilities Study process, timeline and evaluation of alternative transmission technologies in the section 205 filing.
    the NYTOs intend to address whether and how the Facilities Study process should require evaluation of alternative transmission technologies PDF p. 24

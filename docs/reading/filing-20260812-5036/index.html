EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-12 · Sparkfund

Comments of Sparkfund on Midcontinent Independent System Operator Inc.’s Informational Report filed on 07/20/2026

Sparkfund comments on MISO's informational report to show that distributed batteries and other distributed resources already supply capacity to integrate large loads, citing Xcel Energy's Capacity*Connect program in Minnesota.

Answers the order. Comments on MISO's July 20 informational report, which answers the E-8 directive to explain how MISO will ensure adequate generation for large loads; Sparkfund offers distributed capacity as a supply source.

E-8 MISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7)

Key points

  1. Sparkfund says aggregators and utilities in MISO can deploy distributed resources as capacity today under existing participation models.
    aggregators and utilities within MISO are able to deploy distributed resources as capacity today using the existing participation models. PDF p. 1
  2. Minnesota approved Xcel's Capacity*Connect: up to 200 MW of distribution-sited batteries by the end of 2028, with potential for 500 MW by 2031.
    to deploy up to 200 MW of front-of-the-meter, distribution-sited 1-3 MW battery energy storage systems (BESS) by the end of 2028 PDF p. 2
  3. Google's Pine Island, MN agreement includes $50 million toward the battery portfolio for matched capacity credits.
    Google’s new Pine Island, MN data center facility has entered an Electric Service Agreement that includes $50 million towards Xcel’s Capacity*Connect battery portfolio PDF p. 2
  4. Xcel's compliance filing shows average residential savings of about $0.13 per month, rising to about $0.17 with Google's contribution.
    Capacity*Connect will deliver average residential bill savings of approximately $0.13 per month over the twenty-year analysis period PDF p. 3
  5. Sparkfund says several utilities each deploying 200-500 MW could total gigawatts in MISO.
    multiple utilities deploying 200-500 MW of utility-owned and operated distributed capacity could amount to gigawatts across the MISO footprint PDF p. 3

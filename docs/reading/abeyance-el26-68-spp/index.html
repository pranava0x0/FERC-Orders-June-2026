EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-14 · FERC

Letter order granting the SPP Joint Movants' abeyance motion for 95 days, to November 20, 2026

FERC grants the Joint Movants' motion and holds the EL26-68 SPP show cause proceeding in abeyance for 95 days, to November 20, 2026, with answers due December 21, 2026. SPP plans a section 205 filing after its November 17 Board vote.

Changes the schedule. Moves the E-9 SPP 60-day show cause response from August 17 to November 20, 2026, accepting SPP's stakeholder schedule for a section 205 filing.

E-9 SPP: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-9 SPP: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7)

Key points

  1. FERC grants the August 3 motion of SPP and a group of transmission owners for 95 days.
    we grant the Abeyance Motion filed by Joint Movants and hold this proceeding in abeyance for 95 days, until November 20, 2026. Source text
  2. Joint Movants describe SPP's planned work on alternative technologies, cost shifting protections, co-location and flexible-load services.
    SPP anticipates developing revisions to its High Impact Large Load (HILL) process that will incorporate the evaluation of alternative transmission technologies into the study process. Source text
  3. SPP expects stakeholder approval of its Price Adaptive Load proposal for non-firm service on November 17, 2026.
    SPP anticipates final stakeholder approval of the proposal on November 17, 2026. Source text
  4. The five days beyond the usual 90 reflect a Board meeting moved from November 3 to November 17.
    the date of the SPP Board meeting was originally scheduled on November 3, 2026 but was changed to accommodate upcoming elections Source text
  5. The Regional State Committee, Missouri Commission and CEBA filed answers supporting the motion.
    The RSC, Public Service Commission of the State of Missouri, and Corporate Energy Buyers Association filed timely answers in support of Joint Movants' Abeyance Motion. Source text
  6. Responses are due November 20 and answers December 21, 2026; a timely section 205 filing suspends the response duty.
    responses to the Show Cause Order will be due on November 20, 2026, and answers to responses will be due on December 21, 2026. Source text

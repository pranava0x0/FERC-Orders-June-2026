ER27-515-000 · 2026-11-16 · SunZia Transmission, LLC

SunZia Transmission - Trans. Owner Tariff Response to Show Cause Order EL26-71-

SunZia Transmission, LLC files limited tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in ER27-515-000. SunZia serves no load, so it amends Section 8 of its transmission owner tariff to say the CAISO tariff controls interconnection of generation and co-located load, and it reserves the right to amend once it sees the other owners' filings.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective 60 days after FERC's order, and find them consistent with the show cause order.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-10 show cause order. It adds no large load study process, cost recovery agreement or flexible service, and says the CAISO tariff is where those changes belong.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: BTMG netting (pp. 60-61, p. 60)

Key points

  1. The line is a 552-mile high voltage direct current line in New Mexico and Arizona.
    an approximately 552-mile, bi-pole, high voltage direct current (“HVDC”) 500 kV transmission line PDF p. 2
  2. All of the line's capacity is committed to two wind subscribers.
    the entire capacity of the Transmission System is committed to two Subscribers PDF p. 2
  3. Its tariff is silent on load interconnection because it does not serve load, so it says no large load provisions are needed.
    SunZia Transmission does not directly serve load and, accordingly, there are no provisions in its TO Tariff that address the interconnection of the load to the SunZia Transmission System PDF p. 5
  4. Interconnection requests already go to CAISO first.
    Section 8 of the SunZia Transmission TO Tariff states that all requests for interconnection to the SunZia Transmission System must be directed in the first instance to CAISO PDF p. 5
  5. The only edit confirms the CAISO tariff controls generator and co-located interconnection.
    Section 8 of the TO Tariff has been amended to clarify that the CAISO Tariff will continue to control with respect to the interconnection of Generating Units PDF p. 5
  6. SunZia keeps the option to amend after reviewing the other owners' filings.
    reserves the right, following review of the proposed tariff changes filed by the other Participating Transmission Owners PDF p. 5
  7. SunZia wants CAISO to start its electrically proximate load work as soon as possible.
    SunZia Transmission continues to support the need to apply these enhanced Large Load interconnection procedures to Electrically Proximate Loads PDF p. 5
  8. The effective date is tied to FERC's order. The tariff records carry a placeholder date until then.
    SunZia Transmission proposes an effective date of sixty days after the Commission issues an order on this compliance filing for the changes to its TO Tariff PDF p. 6

Comments of PJM Industrial Customer Coalition on PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.’s Reliability Backstop Procurement Proposal

The PJM Industrial Customer Coalition comments on PJM's July 31 Reliability Backstop Procurement proposal. It identifies three gaps in how wholesale backstop costs reach state retail rates: cost allocation alignment, regulatory accountability and information sharing.

Asks FERC to: Consider the Coalition's comments, and require that retail allocation methodologies be on file and that PJM tag backstop costs by load for states.

Parallel proceeding. Comments in PJM's separate backstop procurement docket ER26-3380. Its call to trace backstop costs to individual large loads parallels the E-7 cost transparency directive and the E-7 adequacy report on generation for new load.

E-7 PJM: Cost transparency (P 71, p. 51) E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8)

Key points

  1. The Coalition says PJM's backstop filing lacks tools to align wholesale charges with state retail cost allocation.
    PJM’s filing lacks the tools needed to ensure that retail costs are, and will be, allocated in a just and reasonable manner at the state level. PDF p. 3
  2. Gap 1: wholesale costs must be designed with retail allocation in mind, tied to cost causation.
    Wholesale costs must be designed with retail allocation in mind; otherwise, simply claiming that states can allocate costs to retail customers may prove inadequate. PDF p. 4
  3. Gap 2: utilities' retail allocation methodologies should be on file with FERC or a state commission.
    While not mandating retail pricing, the FERC should require that methodologies be on file with the FERC or a relevant state commission. PDF p. 7
  4. Gap 3: PJM should add a separate billing line item for backstop costs, broken out by the load growth that caused them.
    PJM should create a new billing line item (similar to how PJM separately reports Transmission Enhancement, Generation Deactivation, or other itemized costs) tagged specifically as “Reliability Backstop Procurement” costs PDF p. 8
  5. PJM should add subaccounts identifying each large load by interconnection point and issue billing guidance.
    Under each billing line item related to RBP, PJM should add subaccounts identifying each individual large load customer by interconnection point. PDF p. 8

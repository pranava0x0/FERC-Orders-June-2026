ER26-3380-000 · 2026-08-14 · PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.

PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. submits tariff filing per : Supplemental Filing of Croop Affidavit in ER26-3380 to be effective N/A

PJM files a supplemental affidavit of Danielle Croop that replaces the worked examples of how its Reliability Backstop Procurement selects offers, and argues the supplement does not reset FERC's action clock.

Asks FERC to: Continue to act on the June 30, 2026 backstop filing by the requested action date of September 29, 2026.

Parallel proceeding. PJM's separate backstop procurement in ER26-3380 is a supply-side adequacy mechanism; the E-7 order asks PJM for a report on ensuring adequate generation for large loads.

E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8)

Key points

  1. PJM says the June 30 example did not show least-cost selection by Delivery Year, so it submits corrected examples.
    the example provided in Exhibit 1 of Mrs. Croop’s June 30, 2026 affidavit did not accurately demonstrate the least cost selection by Delivery Year PDF p. 1
  2. PJM argues the supplement changes no rate, term or condition and so is not an amendment.
    the supplemental affidavit merely updates the examples, also shown in redline, that were included in Mrs. Croop’s June 30, 2026 affidavit and does not revise or amend a proposed rate schedule, term, or condition. PDF p. 1
  3. PJM asks FERC to keep the September 29, 2026 action date.
    the Commission should continue to issue an order by the requested action date of September 29, 2026. PDF p. 2
  4. The affidavit corrects an inverted numerator and denominator in the MW-weighted average levelized cost explanation.
    incorrectly inverted the numerator and denominator PDF p. 5
  5. Offers are levelized at a 9.5% after-tax WACC and selected under a maximum willingness to pay, illustrated at $100/MW-day against the proposed $555/MW-day.
    rather than a maximum willingness to pay of $555/MW-day as PJM is proposing to use for the RBP, we assume that the maximum willingness to pay is $100/MW-day PDF p. 8
  6. In Example 3 the procurement clears 100 MW short of the 6,800 MW target to stay under the price cap.
    the procurement would clear 100 MW short the target, with the maximum UCAP MW procured equal to 6,700 MW for the 2031/32 Delivery Year. PDF p. 12

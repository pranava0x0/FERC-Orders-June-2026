Lines 12–14 reconcile projected credits to actual receipt-based credits for the ordinary formula-rate true-up, with 
applicable interest. The difference is Update less projection: a negative credit difference increases the actual net revenue 
requirement, holding other inputs constant. This is separate from the customer-level true-up on line 8, which remains 
based on amounts billed and is calculated without interest. 
For line 10, the Annual Update includes Supplemental MTRC actually received by LSPGNY during the Rate Year, 
including collections of prior-year bills and Security proceeds received and applied to Supplemental MTRC obligations, 
counted once, less refunds of previously credited receipts. Attach a receipt schedule identifying each component by 
Eligible Customer, Covered Upgrade, billing year, and receipt date, and reconcile it to cash, settlement, and accounting 
records and FERC Form No. 1. Amounts merely billed, or Security merely posted, drawn, or held without receipt by 
LSPGNY, are not actual revenue credits. Security applied to a Termination Fee follows the Annual Termination Fee 
Remittance treatment; the full Termination Fee is not an immediate formula-rate credit. 
CRA references: Sections 6.5, 6.6 and 6.8.

EL26-68: the filed comments

Positions read for this site

Southwest Power Pool

· EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-14

SPP told FERC its board meeting had moved because of the elections. The revised November 17 vote made a November 16 filing impractical; FERC granted a November 20 response date.

Missouri Public Service Commission

· EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-07

Missouri backed the 95-day request because SPP’s November 17 board vote fell just beyond the 90-day window. It said the extra days allowed meaningful state input without materially delaying the case.

SPP Regional State Committee

· EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-07

The committee endorsed a November 20 deadline while its cost-allocation team studied who should secure network costs if promised demand fails to arrive or leaves early. The filing endorsed the schedule only. The cost-allocation formula is still being studied.

SPP Regional State Committee

· EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-07

In its later comments on the filing schedule, the committee said its cost team was examining security for upgrades if a large customer never arrives or leaves early. It did not approve a particular conditional-service charge.

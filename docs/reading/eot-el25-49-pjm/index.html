Unpaginated filing

FERC gives PJM and its owners 90 more days, to November 16, on co-location.

The deadline for PJM to comply with certain directives of the Co-Located Load Order, and the deadline for the PJM transmission owners to comply with the directives of the Co-Located Load Order, is extended to and including November 16, 2026. Source text

Basis for Intervening:
Pursuant to Rules 212 and 214 of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s (“Commission”) Rules of Practice and Procedure, 18 C.F.R. §§ 385.212, 385.214 (2026), Enchanted Rock, LLC (“Enchanted Rock”) respectfully files this motion to intervene (“Motion”) in Docket No. ER26-3515-000.

Enchanted Rock serves data centers, hospitals, water treatment plants, manufacturing facilities and other high load customers and their communities, providing electrical resiliency to this critical infrastructure.  To date, Enchanted Rock has commissioned more than 700 megawatts across more than 300 sites, with hundreds of additional megawatts under development.  Enchanted Rock’s multi-purpose microgrids have served customers through severe weather events such as Hurricane Harvey and Winter Storm Uri and during unexpected long-duration outages on days unaffected by weather, with over 14,000 hours of utility outages covered and 99.9998% combined reliability.

On August 13, 2026, pursuant to section 205 of the Federal Power Act, PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (“PJM”) submitted proposed revisions to the Reliability Assurance Agreement Among Load Serving Entities and the PJM Open Access Transmission Tariff to establish Interim Resource Adequacy Service and a Large Load Registry.  

Good cause exists to grant the Motion.  As an entity that may be impacted by the outcome of this proceeding, Enchanted Rock has an interest in this proceeding that cannot be adequately represented by any other party.  Accordingly, and since it is in the public interest, Enchanted Rock respectively requests that the Commission grant the Motion.

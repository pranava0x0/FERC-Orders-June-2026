EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-08 · MAVEN SOLUTIONS

Comments of Maven Solutions on the Show Cause Order regarding Civic Bankability Rating Standard to FERC's Five Reform Categories

Maven Solutions comments on the E-7 show cause order, scoring PJM's large-load tariff posture Conditional under its own Civic Bankability Rating Standard and recommending tariff revisions for each of the five reform categories.

Asks FERC to: Require PJM to file tariff revisions or a detailed evidentiary showing on all five reform categories, including time-bounded study commitments, a large-load cost-causation method and a flexible large-load tariff class.

Answers the order. Comments on the E-7 show cause order in EL26-67 that map Maven's recommendations to the order's five reform categories: study process, cost shifting, co-location, flexible load service and proximate generation.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: Cost-recovery agreement (P 77, p. 28) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56) E-7 PJM: Application / study process (P 6(a), p. 7)

Key points

  1. Maven rates PJM's posture Conditional with two keystone flags, Ratepayer Protection and Financial Surety, which it says warrant Commission intervention.
    PJM's current tariff posture on large-load interconnection scores CONDITIONAL under the Civic Bankability Rating Standard with two active keystone flags PDF p. 3
  2. Maven cites the 2027/28 auction as the first to miss the reliability requirement, 6,516.6 MW UCAP short at a 14.4% reserve margin.
    which PJM reported as 6,516.6 MW UCAP below the RTO Reliability Requirement. PDF p. 4
  3. Maven says large-load study processes lack a bounded time-to-service commitment or public reporting of completion times.
    proceed without a bounded time-to-service commitment, without public reporting on median or 90th-percentile completion timelines, and without a customer-facing remedy mechanism for delayed studies. PDF p. 8
  4. Recommendation 2 asks that upgrade and capacity costs caused by large loads go to the responsible load class, with per-upgrade disclosure.
    attributes transmission upgrade costs and capacity procurement costs caused by large-load interconnection to the responsible load class, not to Load Serving Entities or residential rate classes PDF p. 12
  5. Recommendation 4 asks for flexible large-load tariff classes with defined compensation, reliability crediting and financial surety.
    provide for curtailable or interruptible service with defined compensation; (ii) include contribution-to-reliability crediting PDF p. 12
  6. Maven says this is one of six coordinated comments across dockets EL26-67 through EL26-72.
    These Comments are one of six substantive Comments Maven files this cycle across the six Show Cause dockets initiated on June 18, 2026 (EL26-67 through EL26-72). PDF p. 5

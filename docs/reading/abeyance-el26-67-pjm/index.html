EL26-67-000 · 2026-08-14 · FERC

Letter order granting the PJM, Indicated PJM Transmission Owners and Silver Run Electric abeyance motions

FERC grants the abeyance motions of PJM, the Indicated PJM Transmission Owners and Silver Run, holding the E-7 show cause proceeding for 90 days. Responses move to November 16, 2026 and answers to December 16, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Postpones the E-7 60-day show cause deadline for PJM and its Transmission Owners to November 16, 2026, in exchange for section 205 filings on study, cost and flexible service topics.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-7 PJM: Service for flexible loads (P 85, p. 56) E-7 PJM: Cost-recovery agreement (P 77, p. 28)

Key points

  1. FERC holds the whole EL26-67 proceeding in abeyance for 90 days, moving show cause responses to November 16 and answers to December 16, 2026.
    responses to the Show Cause Order will be due on November 16, 2026, and answers to responses will be due on December 16, 2026. Source text
  2. A section 205 filing by November 16 suspends the filer's obligation to respond to the show cause order.
    then the obligation of the respondent(s) who have made such an FPA section 205 filing to file a response to the Show Cause Order will be suspended Source text
  3. PJM says its planned filing will cover study processes, cost shifting, services for flexible large loads, and generation serving proximate large loads.
    (1) achieve efficient transmission service application and study processes, including through the consideration of alternative transmission technologies; (2) prevent cost shifting and enhance transparency into transmission costs Source text
  4. PJM plans an advisory stakeholder vote at the Members Committee on October 28, 2026.
    with the goal of taking an advisory stakeholder vote on the proposal at the Members Committee meeting on October 28, 2026. Source text
  5. The Indicated Transmission Owners expect to file pro forma cost recovery agreements and penalties for exceeding contractual service limits.
    pro forma cost recovery agreements and rates for new transmission services for flexible large loads and electrically proximate large loads or co-located loads Source text
  6. FERC encourages filers to explain which proposed revisions resolve the show cause findings and to set effective dates that respect existing commercial arrangements.
    we encourage PJM and/or Transmission Owners to explain, with specificity, which of their proposed Tariff revisions they believe resolve all or some of the Commission’s preliminary findings Source text

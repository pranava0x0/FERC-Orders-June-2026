Comments of the Arkansas Public Service Commission in Support of MISO Application

The Arkansas Public Service Commission files short comments supporting MISO's Large Load Addition Resource Study filing, adopting the Mississippi and Louisiana commissions' comments and calling LARS a reasonable response to the show cause order.

Asks FERC to: Accept MISO's LARS filing.

Answers the order. Supports MISO's LARS filing as an answer to the E-8 show cause order, citing the order's preliminary finding on generator interconnection study procedures for electrically proximate large loads.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The Arkansas PSC supports LARS and joins the Mississippi and Louisiana commissions' comments.
    The APSC supports MISO's filing to establish the LARS process, and agrees with the comments made by the Mississippi Public Service Commission and the Louisiana Public Service Commission. Source text
  2. It says LARS aligns with the show cause order's requirements.
    AR agrees that LARS is a reasonable response to and aligns with the requirements of the Commission's Show Cause Order. Source text

 Dear Governor Youngkin: 
 
  
Thank you for your October 31, 2024, letter regarding the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission’s (Commission) technical conference on large loads co-
located at generating facilities in Docket No. AD24-11-000.  Your insights are 
essential to the Commission’s consideration of this matter.   
 
In your letter, you highlighted the importance of data center development to 
the economy of the Commonwealth of Virginia.  You also requested that the 
Commission continue to recognize the important role that co-located load 
arrangements could play in Virginia’s electric future, noting that the rapid load growth 
Virginia is experiencing requires innovative ways to maximize the attraction of new 
large load sources while minimizing bill impacts to traditional ratepayers, such as co-
locating load with new generation behind the meter. 
Because the issues referenced in your letter are relevant to contested pending 
proceedings, I cannot discuss the merits of these issues at this time.  I agree that co-
located load arrangements will play an important role in the nation’s electric future.  
As I emphasized in my statement issued February 21, 2025, after the Commission 
unanimously voted to launch a review of issues associated with the co-location of 
large loads such as AI-enabled data centers at generating facilities in PJM, including 
whether the PJM tariff needs to establish rules to create clarity while ensuring grid 
reliability and fair costs to customers, we want to act quickly on this issue.  While I 
cannot announce a specific date for final action, it is our intent to conduct and 
complete this important proceeding expeditiously, so we can provide clarity and 
certainty to all, from consumers to investors to load-serving utilities and independent 
generators.   
 
You have my commitment that we will continue to actively work on this issue.  
I take seriously our obligation to ensure that any action the Commission takes is in the

Special Members Committee agenda, Nov 5, 2026 (as of Oct 29)

PJM posts the agenda for a Special Members Committee meeting on November 5, 2026. Its single item is consultation on a suite of section 205 filings responding to the Large Load Show Cause Order.

Answers the order. Shows PJM taking the section 205 route offered by the E-7 show cause order: a suite of tariff filings, with consultation set for November 5, 2026, ahead of the abeyance deadline of November 16.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7)

ER26-3650-000 · 2026-08-28 · Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.

MISO proposes interconnection reliability requirements for large loads

MISO files Tariff revisions in ER26-3650 setting Interconnection Reliability Requirements for Large Loads of 50 MW or more: visibility, PMU, ramp-rate and ride-through rules, with grandfathering for loads in service by December 4, 2026.

Asks FERC to: Accept the Large Load Interconnection Reliability Requirements Tariff revisions effective December 4, 2026.

Answers the order. MISO's first large-load tariff filing after the E-8 order, covering operational requirements and leaving cost-recovery, transmission products and proximate-generation study for filings due by the abeyance deadline of November 16, 2026.

E-8 MISO: Application / study / operating requirements (P 6(a), p. 6)

Key points

  1. MISO says the filing answers some E-8 concerns (visibility, ramp rate, ride-through) and leaves study, products, cost-shift and proximate generation issues for later filings.
    proposing visibility, ramp rate, and disturbance ride-through requirements applicable to Large Loads, along with transition provisions to minimize disruption to commercial arrangements. PDF p. 1
  2. Large Load means 50 MW or more at a single substation behind one or more points of withdrawal.
    Load with Demand of 50 MW or more associated with any facility or aggregation of facilities located at a single substation PDF p. 1
  3. MISO requests a December 4, 2026 effective date.
    MISO requests that the Tariff revisions submitted herewith be made effective December 4, 2026 PDF p. 2
  4. The proposal applies to transmission customers serving the load and covers four categories: visibility, PMU, ramp and ride-through.
    The proposed framework focuses on four categories of reliability requirements that will be applicable to Large Loads. PDF p. 3
  5. MISO says its study-process enhancements will come in a future section 205 filing.
    MISO intends to address enhancements to stability assessment study processes in a future Federal Power Act section 205 filing. PDF p. 4
  6. MISO lists further proposals due before the November 16, 2026 abeyance deadline, including LARS for proximate load and a Firm Service Step Up.
    MISO is refining its Load Addition and Resource Study (“LARS”) process to facilitate the parallel study of load and generation that will address service to proximate load PDF p. 4
  7. Initial ramp limits for Computational Load are 30 MW per minute up and down.
    MISO is proposing initial up- and down-ramp limits of 30MW/minute. PDF p. 20
  8. Loads with a Service Commencement Date on or before December 4, 2026 are exempt from the new Section 37A for service already procured.
    MISO proposes to fully exempt, or “grandfather,” any Large Load that has a Service Commencement Date on or prior to December 4, 2026 PDF p. 14

Performance standards ahead of NERC

MISO argues that rapidly changing power demand makes it too risky to wait for a national standard before setting regional expectations.

the MISO region cannot wait to put in place needed interconnection reliability requirements PDF p. 3

Ride-through · PDF pp. 3–4

What happens during a grid disturbance

MISO would require computational loads to withstand certain frequency and voltage disturbances. The concern is that a sudden load drop can itself upset grid operation.

reduce the risk of unnecessary disconnection or customer-initiated rapid reductions in Demands PDF p. 4

Customer obligations · PDF pp. 10–21

Who is responsible for compliance?

MISO would hold the transmission customer responsible because the owner of a large load need not have a direct contract with the grid operator.

The Transmission Customer taking Transmission Service on behalf of a Large Load will be responsible for ensuring compliance PDF p. 12

Enforcement · PDF pp. 22–27

When MISO can delay or limit service

Under proposed section 37A, failure to meet a requirement could delay the start of service or lead to corrective work or operating restrictions.

failure to comply with applicable requirements may result in a delayed Service Commencement Date PDF p. 23

EL26-70-000 · 2026-08-03 · Midcontinent Independent System Operator Inc.MISO Transmission Owners

MISO requests more time to answer the June 18 show cause order

MISO and its Transmission Owners ask FERC to hold the E-8 show cause proceeding in abeyance for 90 days, through November 16, 2026, while they use the Large Load Working Group to prepare a series of section 205 filings.

Asks FERC to: Hold the proceeding in abeyance for 90 days starting August 17, 2026, with a shortened answer period and expedited treatment.

Answers the order. Uses the abeyance option in the E-8 order (P 37) to postpone the 60-day show cause deadline and lays out the filings MISO plans, including LARS for proximate generation and a firm step-up service.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-8 MISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7)

Key points

  1. The movants say the stakeholder process needs the 90 days to craft section 205 filings answering the order.
    The Motion is necessary to permit MISO and the MISO Transmission Owners to continue the MISO stakeholder process to develop and submit FPA section PDF p. 2
  2. MISO says some issues in the order were not in its original workplan, and the 60-day deadline blocks stakeholder development.
    The current, imminent compliance deadline in the Show Cause Order effectively prohibits the full development and robust consideration of any potential stakeholder solutions to integrating large loads. PDF p. 5
  3. Planned filings: a Zero Injection Generator Interconnection Agreement, a large load definition, interconnection reliability requirements, a firm step-up service and a parallel load and resource study (LARS).
    a parallel study process for large load and generation resources (i.e., Load Addition and Resource Study process or “LARS”) PDF p. 4
  4. MISO expects to file the ZGIA by the end of August.
    MISO expects to submit the ZGIA filing by the end of August. PDF p. 10
  5. LARS would study load and generation in parallel within 120 days and address the order's proximate generation concern.
    The studies will be run in parallel, and will finish at approximately the same time—within 120 days PDF p. 11
  6. MISO expects to file the firm service step-up Tariff revisions by November 16, 2026, or earlier if feasible.
    MISO expects to make a section 205 filing proposing Tariff revisions incorporating the firm service step-up option by November 16, 2026, or earlier, if feasible. PDF p. 12
  7. The Large Load Working Group has six scheduled meetings before the abeyance ends.
    the LLWG is scheduled to meet six times between the date of this filing and the end of the requested abeyance period PDF p. 13

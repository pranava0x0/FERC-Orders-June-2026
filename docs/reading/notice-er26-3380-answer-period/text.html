NOTICE ESTABLISHING ANSWER PERIOD

(October 16, 2026)

On October 13, 2026, pursuant to Rule 212 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure, PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (PJM) filed a motion to hold the paper hearing proceedings and the submission of briefs in the above-captioned proceeding in abeyance pending resolution of PJM’s anticipated forthcoming filing that will be submitted under section 205 of the Federal Power Act addressing the issues raised in a September 29, 2026, Commission order.

Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that answers to PJM’s motion are due by 5:00 pm Eastern Time on October 21, 2026. 





Carlos D. Clay,
Deputy Secretary.

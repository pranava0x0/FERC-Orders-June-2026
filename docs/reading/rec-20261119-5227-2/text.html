AFFIDAVIT OF NATASHA HENDERSON
ON BEHALF OF
SOUTHWEST POWER POOL, INC.
1. My name is Natasha Henderson.  I am employed by Southwest Power Pool, Inc. (“SPP”) as Senior Director, Grid Asset Utilization.  My business address is 201 Worthen Drive, Little Rock, AR 72223.  In my current position, my primary duties are to oversee the policy development for the engineering department, including transmission planning, load connection, resource adequacy and generation interconnection, as well as the performance of technical studies and assessments necessary to respond to requests for special generator interconnection service and long-term transmission service, and innovative research. I establish strategic direction for SPP’s Open Access Transmission Tariff (“Tariff”) services and resource utilization initiatives, and direct SPP’s Tariff services and resource utilization activities across all applicable areas of the organization in accordance with SPP’s Tariff and established strategy.

2. I received a Bachelor of Business Administration, Economics and Master of Arts in Economics, Financial Track, from Wichita State University in 2004 and 2006, respectively.  Before being named to my present position as Senior Director of Strategic Policy and Load Integration in June of 2026, I held the Senior Director of Grid Asset Utilization role, and prior to that I held the Director, System Planning role at SPP.  Prior to my time at SPP, which began in 2023, I was Director of Compliance, Regulatory and Market Affairs at Golden Spread Electric Cooperative, Inc. (“Golden Spread”), where I led

Golden Spread’s compliance and market advocacy in the regions of SPP and the Electric Reliability Council of Texas, before the Public Utility Commission of Texas, and before the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (“Commission”).

3. I am submitting this affidavit to support the accuracy of the information included in Attachment A to this affidavit. Attachment A is a spreadsheet outlining the upgrade costs resulting from Notifications to Construct (“NTCs”) that have been issued by SPP through its delivery point study processes from 2012 until 2025, which includes for each load request (1) the load amount; (2) total cost of the Network Upgrades identified;

(3) cost per MW of load requested; (4) the average (if multiple upgrades) date when the Network Upgrades were put into service; and (5) the cost per MW percentile compared to the other requests included on Exhibit 1.

4. To the best of my knowledge, the information included in Attachment A is true and correct.

ANSWER OF CORPORATE ENERGY BUYERS ASSOCIATION  
IN SUPPORT OF THE MOTIONS FOR ABEYANCE 
 
Pursuant to Rule 213 of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s (Commission) 
Rules of Practice and Procedure,1 Corporate Energy Buyers Association (CEBA) respectfully 
submits this Answer in support of the motions for abeyance submitted separately by California 
Independent System Operator Corporation (CAISO);2 certain CAISO transmission owners (Joint 
PTOs);3 DCR Transmission, L.L.C.;4 and the Cities of Anaheim, Azusa, Banning, Colton, 
Pasadena, and Riverside, California (Six Cities)5 (collectively, Movants). Movants ask the 
Commission to hold this proceeding in abeyance by 90 days and, in so doing, extend certain 
deadlines for responding to the June 18, 2026 Order to Show Cause (Show Cause Order).6 
The issues raised in the Show Cause Order raise complex and substantial issues that 
require due consideration to craft solutions that are appropriately tailored to the region. Granting 
the requested abeyance will promote administrative efficiencies by allowing time for CAISO and 
stakeholders—including CEBA and its members—to work collaboratively, as opposed to a 
rushed proposal that would unnecessarily shift stakeholder debate to the Commission docket.

Lastly, the requested abeyance strikes an appropriate balance: a 90-day extension would not 
materially impede the Commission’s objectives for advancing reforms quickly yet offers 
significant benefits in terms of enabling proposals to receive sufficient stakeholder feedback to 
promote both commercial viability and legal durability of the proposals that CAISO and CAISO 
transmission owners will each file in mid-November. 
For these reasons, CEBA respectfully requests that the Commission grant these motions 
in an expedited manner.

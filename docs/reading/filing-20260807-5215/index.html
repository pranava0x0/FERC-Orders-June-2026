EL26-71-000 · 2026-08-07 · Corporate Energy Buyers Association

Answer of Corporate Energy Buyers Association in Support of California Independent System Operator Corporation's et al. 08/03/2026 et al. Motion for Abeyance

The Corporate Energy Buyers Association answers in support of the abeyance motions of CAISO, the Joint PTOs, DCR Transmission and the Six Cities. CEBA takes no position on the Six Cities' motion to rescind.

Asks FERC to: Grant the abeyance motions in an expedited manner.

Answers the order. Supports the 90-day abeyance option in the E-10 CAISO show cause order. FERC cited this answer when it granted the abeyance on August 14, 2026.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. CEBA says the show cause issues are complex and need region-specific solutions worked out with stakeholders.
    Granting the requested abeyance will promote administrative efficiencies by allowing time for CAISO and stakeholders—including CEBA and its members—to work collaboratively PDF p. 1
  2. A 90-day abeyance would not materially slow the reforms and would give the mid-November filings stakeholder feedback.
    a 90-day extension would not materially impede the Commission’s objectives for advancing reforms quickly PDF p. 2
  3. CEBA asks for expedited action on the motions.
    CEBA respectfully requests that the Commission grant these motions in an expedited manner. PDF p. 2

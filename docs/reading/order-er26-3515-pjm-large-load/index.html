Existing generators may gain from the rejection

Jefferies analysts, quoted by Utility Dive · Direct response

Jefferies saw the rejection of interim service as favorable to existing independent generators. That was an analyst assessment of market effects, separate from the commission’s reasons for its decision.

Ethan Howland, October 12, analyst reaction

A longer wait for private deals

Mary Yang, 804 Advisory, interviewed by Latitude Media · Direct response

Yang, a former PJM regulatory counsel, expected other priorities to delay a rewrite. She said that uncertainty could slow bilateral arrangements between new loads and generators.

Maeve Allsup, October 13, interview with Mary Yang

Which customers should lose power first?

Commissioner David Rosner · Direct response

Rosner wanted voluntary demand response called first, followed by noncritical large loads before homes and other ordinary customers. He objected to keeping demand out of the capacity market forecast. His October concurrence predates the November compliance filing.

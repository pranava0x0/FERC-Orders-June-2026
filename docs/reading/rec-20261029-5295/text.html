A. RBP Proposal
PJM filed the RBP Proposal on July 31, 2026 as a one-time procurement of new capacity for terms of up to 15 years, beginning no earlier than the 2028/2029 Delivery Year and ending no later than the 2042/2043 Delivery Year.5 At the zone/area level, PJM proposed to allocate the Initial RBP Target to Electric Distributor zone/areas based on each zone/area’s pro rata share of the difference in forecasted Load Adjustments between the 2026/2027 and 2028/2029 Delivery Years, reduced by qualifying offset capacity and opted-out load, and to fix that allocation for the

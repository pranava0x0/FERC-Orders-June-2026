C. The Commission’s June Order
On June 18, 2026, the Commission issued the June Order, addressing the Sixty- Day Compliance Filing, the paper hearing submissions, and requests for rehearing and clarification of the December Order.35

The June Order found that PJM’s Sixty-Day Compliance Filing partially complies with the directives of the December Order, and the Commission accepted in part and rejected in part PJM’s proposed Tariff revisions and ordered PJM to submit a further compliance filing within 60 days.36  Additionally, the June Order found that in response to the record developed in the paper hearing, certain rates, terms, and conditions for the new transmission services as directed in the December Order (i.e., Interim NITS, Firm Contract Demand transmission service, and Non-Firm Contract Demand transmission service) are just and reasonable and directed PJM to make a compliance filing to revise its Tariff.37

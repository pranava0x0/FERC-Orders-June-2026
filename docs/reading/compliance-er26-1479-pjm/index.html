Responses and related views

Constellation challenges the July 2028 date

Constellation and PJM · Direct response

Constellation questioned the proposed July 2028 start for interim network service. PJM answered that administering interruptions required new automation, but said it expected to take requests from September 2027.

PDF pp. 2–3

AMP identifies a missing class of behind-the-meter generation

American Municipal Power and PJM · Direct response

AMP asked that the new service also cover non-retail behind-the-meter generation. PJM agreed to revise the language. On a separate objection from Amtrak and industrial customers, PJM said its changes did not impose the netting restriction they feared.

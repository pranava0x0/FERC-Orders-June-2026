EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-28 · PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.

PJM requests more time to answer the June 18 show cause order

PJM moves to hold the E-7 show cause proceeding in abeyance for 90 days from August 17, 2026 so it can develop a section 205 filing alongside its co-located load compliance work, with stakeholder sessions and an October 28 advisory vote.

Asks FERC to: Grant a 90-day abeyance starting August 17, 2026, shorten the answer period to five days, and rule by August 6, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Asks to defer the E-7 60-day show cause response, tying it to the E-2 co-location compliance order and its Interim NITS service. FERC granted the abeyance on August 14, 2026.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7) E-2 PJM: Interim NITS (P 2, p. 3) E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8)

Key points

  1. PJM seeks a 90-day abeyance because the show cause reforms build on co-located load compliance changes.
    Many of the changes contemplated in the Large Load Order will build upon compliance changes recently ordered for Co-Located Load in Docket Nos. EL25-49-000, et al. PDF p. 1
  2. PJM expects its section 205 filing in early to mid-November.
    PJM anticipates submitting that filing by early to mid-November. PDF p. 2
  3. The overlap covers expanded transmission services, study procedures and operational requirements.
    (1) expansion of new transmission services; (2) study procedures; and (3) operational requirements. PDF p. 4
  4. PJM's planned filing covers study processes with alternative transmission technologies, cost shifting and transparency, flexible load services, and proximate generation study.
    achieve efficient transmission service application and study processes, including through the consideration of alternative transmission technologies PDF p. 6
  5. PJM schedules stakeholder sessions on July 30, August 18 and September 16, with an advisory vote on October 28, 2026.
    with the goal of taking an advisory stakeholder vote on the proposal at the meeting of the Members Committee on October 28, 2026 PDF p. 7

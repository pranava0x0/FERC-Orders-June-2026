Re:  Submission 1788574 - Protest, Request for Clarification and Comments of EQUINIX INC. under ER26-3515

Dear Ms. Reese:
After difficulties with interactions of our internal filing system and the FERC online system, I have learned that I inadvertently uploaded and filed the original submission made by PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (“PJM”) initiating Docket ER26-3515, instead of the “Protest, Request for Clarification and Comments of EQUINIX INC. under ER26-3515.”

I only became aware of this issue when I received the attached notice from FERC Online Support, requesting removal of the FERC watermark from the submission that I made at 4:20:02 PM today, as shown in the attached copy of the receipt of filing. I found this confusing, as I did not insert any watermarks on the document I had prepared, which has a time signature of 4:13 PM today, and as I saw none when I checked the document. I then looked at the online summary of filing, and realized the file name was for the original PJM filing rather than our document. Clearly, after several failed attempts to upload the correct document, I inadvertently attached the wrong document. I am filing a copy of this letter and its two attachments, along with the proper document with the 4:13 PM time stamp. I hope that the original document can be accepted for the Commission’s consideration in this proceeding.

Respectfully submitted,

   /s/ Arthur Haubenstock

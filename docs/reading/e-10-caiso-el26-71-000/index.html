Tribal transmission owner says it has no end-use customers

Morongo Transmission LLC · Direct response

Morongo said it leases transmission capacity and receives revenue through CAISO’s access charge. Because it serves no end-use customers, it argued that its own tariff needed no amendment. It still intended to participate in the proceeding.

PDF pp. 1–3

CAISO describes the board’s plan before filing

Elliot Mainzer, CAISO president and CEO, quoted by APPA · Related public statement

After the October board action, Mainzer said CAISO expected its response to improve operational visibility, protect other customers from cost shifts and speed connections for flexible loads. His statement described a plan, not FERC approval.

October 28, comments following board action

Eolian wants nearby generation considered for permanent service

Eolian · Direct response

Eolian sought clarification that generation located electrically close to a large load could qualify for permanent limited service if it could constrain grid withdrawals over the long term. FERC dismissed the request because the preliminary order was not final.

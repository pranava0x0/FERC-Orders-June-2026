This FACILITIES STUDY AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of 
this [___] day of [_______], 20[__] (the “Effective Date”) by and between [TRANSMISSION 
OWNER LEGAL NAME], a [corporation] organized and existing under the laws of the State of 
New York, acting in its capacity as either the Connecting Transmission Owner (“CTO”) or an 
Affected Transmission Owner (“ATO”), as applicable, and hereinafter referred to as the 
“Transmission Owner,” and [LARGE LOAD CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME], a [entity type] 
organized and existing under the laws of the State of [_____], hereinafter referred to as the “Large 
Load Customer.” Transmission Owner and Large Load Customer may be referred to herein 
individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.” 
RECITALS 
WHEREAS, Transmission Owner owns, operates, or has responsibility for certain transmission 
facilities that form part of the New York State Transmission System, including the [[___] kilovolt 
(“kV”) [_____] transmission line or the [_____] Substation located at [Utility Facility Location]] 
(collectively, the “Transmission Owner Facilities”); 
WHEREAS, Large Load Customer is proposing to develop a Large Load Project of approximately 
[___] megawatts (“MW”), together with associated Large Load Customer-owned substation 
facilities, located at [Project Site], [_____] County, New York (the “Large Load Project”), 
consistent with the Large Load Integration Request submitted by Large Load Customer to the New 
York Independent System Operator, Inc. (“NYISO”) and designated [Queue No. _____]; 
WHEREAS, Large Load Customer desires to interconnect the Large Load Project with the New 
York State Transmission System at the Transmission Owner Facilities; 
WHEREAS, NYISO performed a System Impact Study and is expected to complete and provide 
the results of the Assessment of Load Integration Report (“ALI”) to the Parties, and the ALI is 
expected to identify certain violations and needs to the Transmission Owner Facilities and related 
facilities; 
WHEREAS, Large Load Customer has requested that Transmission Owner perform, or cause to 
be performed, a facilities study in two parts, with Part 1 addressing the local Network Upgrades 
and Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities and proceeding in parallel with the ALI, and 
potentially Part 2 addressing non-local Network Upgrades and coordination and alignment with 
ALI results after NYISO provides them, in each case to specify and estimate the cost of the 
equipment, engineering, procurement and construction work needed to complete the Network 
Upgrades and any Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities; and 
WHEREAS, the Parties intend that the results of the Facilities Study will define the Large Load 
Project to a level sufficient to permit the Parties to negotiate one or more subsequent agreements

construction begin. 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, and other good 
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the 
Parties, intending to be legally bound, agree as follows: 
1.0 
Definitions. When used in this Agreement with initial capitalization, the terms specified 
shall have the meanings indicated herein or, if not defined herein, the meanings specified 
in Section 1 of the NYISO Open Access Transmission Tariff (“OATT”) or Section 44.1 of 
Attachment LL to the OATT, as applicable. “Good Utility Practice” and “Applicable Laws 
and Regulations” have the meanings given in the OATT. If there is a conflict between this 
Agreement and the OATT or Attachment LL, the latter shall control to the extent required 
by Applicable Laws and Regulations. 
2.0 
Performance of Facilities Study. Large Load Customer elects, and Transmission Owner 
shall cause, a facilities study (the “Facilities Study”) to be performed in accordance with 
this Agreement, the applicable criteria, guidelines, standards, specifications, policies, 
procedures and practices of the North American Electric Reliability Corporation 
(“NERC”), the Northeast Power Coordinating Council (“NPCC”), the New York State 
Reliability Council (“NYSRC”), NYISO, Transmission Owner, and Good Utility Practice. 
3.0 
Scope of Work. The scope of the Facilities Study, including the review of local and non-
local Network Upgrades and Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities identified as of 
the Effective Date, is set forth in Exhibit B (the “Scope of Work”) and shall be subject to 
the data provided by Large Load Customer in Exhibit A and to information provided by 
NYISO, including ALI results. Each Party accepts the Scope of Work and acknowledges 
that it is consistent with such Party’s applicable standards, specifications, requirements and 
practices. Any material change in scope shall be addressed under Sections 7.0, 8.0 and 
10.0. 
3.1 
Dynamic Nature of the Facilities Study. Due to the dynamic nature of the Transmission 
System and because the Facilities Study will be performed in parallel with the ALI, 
additional violations and needs may be identified as the ALI and Transmission Owner’s 
analyses progress. The Facilities Study and any draft Facilities Study report may be 
updated to incorporate additional Network Upgrades and Transmission Owner Attachment 
Facilities within the categories described in Exhibit B without amendment of this 
Agreement, and the time for completion of the Facilities Study shall be extended by a 
period commensurate with any such update. Any such update shall be performed as 
additional work at Large Load Customer’s expense pursuant to Sections 8.0 and 10.0 of 
this Agreement. 
3.2 
Additional Studies. Additional studies beyond the scope of this Agreement, including 
special studies and detailed design studies (such as harmonic analyses or electromagnetic 
transient studies), may be required to interconnect the Large Load Project, including after 
completion of the Facilities Study. Transmission owners who are not a party to this 
Agreement may need to conduct studies beyond the scope of this Agreement based on the 
impact of the Large Load Project on the Transmission Owner’s Transmission System. Any

PDF page 6

such additional studies, and any Network Upgrades identified through them, shall be at 
Large Load Customer’s expense. 
3.3 
Planning Purposes Only. The work performed under this Agreement is for planning and 
estimation purposes only. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Transmission Owner to 
construct any Network Upgrades and/or Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities or to 
interconnect or provide service to the Large Load Project, guarantees any interconnection 
or service, or reserves for Large Load Customer any capacity, queue position, or priority. 
Any interconnection or service shall be governed by separate agreement(s) and applicable 
regulatory requirements. 
3.4 
Restudy. If Large Load Customer requests a material modification to the Large Load 
Project in accordance with Section 44 of Attachment LL (e.g., an increase in the requested 
load or a change to the proposed operating characteristics) such that restudy is required, 
the restudy shall be performed as additional work at Large Load Customer’s expense 
pursuant to Sections 8.0 and 10.0 of this Agreement and Section 44 of Attachment LL. The 
final design of the Large Load Project must comply with all federal, regional, state, and 
local regulations as well as all applicable criteria, guidelines, standards, specifications, 
policies, procedures and practices of Transmission Owner, and Good Utility Practice. 
3.5 
Accuracy of Information. Except as Large Load Customer may otherwise specify in 
writing when it provides information to Transmission Owner under this Agreement, Large 
Load Customer represents and warrants that to the best of its knowledge and belief the 
information it has provided or subsequently provides to Transmission Owner is and shall 
be accurate and complete as of the date the information is provided. Large Load Customer 
shall promptly provide Transmission Owner with any additional information needed to 
update information previously provided. 
3.6 
Disclaimer of Warranty. In performing the Facilities Study or restudy, Transmission 
Owner and any subcontractors and/or consultants engaged by Transmission Owner will 
have to rely on information provided by Large Load Customer, and possibly by third 
parties, and may not have control over the accuracy of such information. Accordingly, 
neither Transmission Owner nor any subcontractor or consultant engaged by Transmission 
Owner makes any warranties, express or implied, whether arising by operation of law, 
course of performance or dealing, custom, usage in the trade or profession, or otherwise,

Study or restudy. Large Load Customer acknowledges that it has not relied on any 
representations or warranties by Transmission Owner or its subcontractors or consultants 
not specifically set forth herein and that no such representations or warranties have formed 
the basis of its bargain hereunder. 
4.0 
Contents of Facilities Study. The Facilities Study shall specify and estimate the cost of 
the equipment, engineering, procurement and construction work (including overheads) 
needed to implement the conclusions of the ALI and any other applicable NYISO study or 
process, as of the Effective Date of this Agreement, and to construct the local and non-
local Network Upgrades and Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities. The Facilities 
Study shall also identify: (1) the scope and estimated cost of the Network Upgrades and 
any Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities; (2) an estimate of the time required to

complete the construction and installation of such facilities; (3) the preliminary one-line 
diagram reflecting the preliminary understanding of the Point of Change in Ownership and 
the Point of Interconnection as reflected in Exhibit D, as of the Effective Date of this 
Agreement; and (4) the assumptions and exclusions necessary to develop the cost of the 
Network Upgrades and any Transmission Owner Attachment Facilities. For each Network 
Upgrade associated with a violation to which more than one Large Load Project in a 
[Cluster] contributes, the Facilities Study shall identify the allocation of upgrade costs on 
an upgrade-specific basis, using the contribution factors NYISO identified in the ALI for 
[Bulk Electric System] violations and the contribution factors Transmission Owner 
determined for non-[Bulk Electric System] violations. 
5.0 
Point of Change in Ownership. As applicable, the Parties’ preliminary understanding of 
the point at which ownership of facilities changes from Transmission Owner to Large Load 
Customer (the “Point of Change in Ownership”) is set forth in Exhibit D. The Point of 
Change in Ownership is preliminary and non-binding, and shall be confirmed or revised in 
the Facilities Study and in any subsequent interconnection, cost recovery, security, or 
transmission service agreements. 
6.0 
Large Load Customer’s Responsibilities. Exhibit A contains the tasks, forms, and 
drawings that must be completed and provided by Large Load Customer to Transmission 
Owner, and the time by which each must be provided, to ensure accurate and timely 
completion of the Facilities Study under this Agreement. Failure by Large Load Customer 
to provide accurate, complete, and on-time responses to the requests made by Transmission 
Owner, including but not limited to those made in Exhibit A, may result in delays to the 
completion of the Facilities Study and/or an increase in study costs pursuant to Section 8.0, 
and may result in termination of this Agreement under Section 15.0 and withdrawal of the 
Large Load Project, subject to applicable tariff requirements. 
7.0 
Study Deposit. Large Load Customer shall provide to Transmission Owner a deposit in 
the amount set forth in Exhibit C (the “Study Deposit”) within ten (10) Business Days after 
execution of this Agreement and, in all events, before Transmission Owner begins study 
work. Transmission Owner shall not be obligated to begin or continue the Facilities Study 
until it has received the Study Deposit and any supplemental deposit required under this 
Agreement. If Large Load Customer does not provide the Study Deposit within ten (10) 
Business Days after execution of this Agreement, the Large Load Project shall be subject 
to withdrawal, without a withdrawal cure period. If Exhibit C is revised under Sections 8.0 
or 10.0, Large Load Customer shall pay any supplemental deposit reasonably requested by 
Transmission Owner within ten (10) Business Days after notice, subject to any applicable 
tariff or deadline of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (“FERC”). 
8.0 
Study Costs and Invoicing. Study costs shall be based on Transmission Owner’s actual, 
documented and reasonably incurred costs, including applicable taxes and overheads, and 
shall be invoiced to Large Load Customer periodically. Large Load Customer 
acknowledges that the estimated rates and amounts in Exhibit C are estimates only, are not 
an exhaustive list of charges, and that Large Load Customer shall pay the actual costs 
incurred by Transmission Owner under this Agreement. Transmission Owner shall provide 
reasonable periodic status updates and shall notify Large Load Customer promptly, and 
before incurring material additional costs where practicable, if it anticipates that actual

costs will exceed the Study Deposit by more than thirty percent (30%). The notice shall 
include the reasons for the variance, a revised estimate, and any proposed schedule impact. 
The Parties shall address the resulting supplemental deposit or scope change under Sections 
7.0 and 10.0. 
9.0 
Payment, Refunds and Disputed Amounts. Transmission Owner shall issue a final 
invoice after delivery of the final Facilities Study report or, if this Agreement terminates 
earlier, within thirty (30) Calendar Days after termination. Large Load Customer shall pay 
all invoiced amounts in excess of the Study Deposit within thirty (30) Calendar Days after 
receipt of the invoice. If the Study Deposit exceeds the final invoiced amounts, 
Transmission Owner shall refund the excess within thirty (30) Calendar Days after issuing 
the final invoice, without interest. If Large Load Customer disputes an invoice, it shall 
provide written notice describing the basis of the dispute within ten (10) Business Days 
after receipt, and the dispute shall be resolved in accordance with Section 9.1. A dispute 
shall not delay payment of undisputed amounts. Transmission Owner may suspend study 
work after notice if Large Load Customer fails to pay undisputed amounts or a required 
supplemental deposit when due, subject to applicable tariff requirements. 
9.1 
Dispute Resolution. The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising 
under this Agreement through negotiation between senior representatives for a period of 
[thirty (30)] Calendar Days after written notice of the dispute. Disputes not so resolved 
may be pursued [under the dispute resolution procedures of the NYISO OATT] or before 
FERC, the New York State Public Service Commission, or a court of competent 
jurisdiction, as applicable to the subject matter of the dispute. Pending resolution, each 
Party shall continue to perform its obligations under this Agreement, Large Load Customer 
shall pay all undisputed amounts in accordance with Section 9.0, and Large Load Customer 
shall pay any disputed amount to Transmission Owner or into a non-interest-bearing 
escrow account mutually acceptable to the Parties. Upon resolution of the dispute, the 
disputed amount shall be retained by or released to the Party in whose favor the dispute is 
resolved, without interest. 
10.0 
Modifications; Mandated Changes. Neither Party shall change, modify, adjust or alter 
its responsibilities under this Agreement or the Exhibits without the prior written consent 
of the other Party; provided, however, that such consent shall not be required to the extent 
the change is necessary to comply with (i) a change in Good Utility Practice made after the 
Effective Date, (ii) a change in Applicable Laws and Regulations promulgated after the 
Effective Date, (iii) any change in Transmission Owner’s procedures, policies, standards, 
or business practices, or (iv) a new or modified final directive, order, standard, requirement, 
rule or other action of a Governmental Authority having jurisdiction issued after the 
Effective Date (each, a “Mandated Change”). The Party required to make a Mandated 
Change shall notify the other Party in writing within fifteen (15) Business Days after it first 
becomes aware of the need for the Mandated Change, or sooner if required to comply 
timely with Applicable Laws and Regulations, together with its expected effect on study 
cost, scope, or schedule. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement the 
Mandated Change without impairing compliance with the OATT or applicable NYISO 
requirements.

12.0 
Draft Report. Upon completion of the Facilities Study, Transmission Owner shall prepare 
and promptly transmit a draft Facilities Study report to Large Load Customer. Within ten 
(10) Calendar Days after providing the draft report, the Parties may meet to discuss the 
results of the Facilities Study. 
13.0 
Comments and Final Report. Large Load Customer may, within ten (10) Business Days 
after receipt of the draft report, provide written comments for Transmission Owner’s 
consideration. Transmission Owner shall consider the comments in good faith but shall not 
be required to accept or include every comment in the final report. Transmission Owner 
shall issue the final Facilities Study report within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving 
Large Load Customer’s comments or promptly after receiving Large Load Customer’s 
statement that it will not provide comments. If Large Load Customer does not provide 
written comments within ten (10) Business Days after receiving the draft report, 
Transmission Owner shall be authorized to issue the final Facilities Study report. 
Transmission Owner may reasonably extend that period on notice to Large Load Customer 
if the comments require additional analyses or other significant modifications, and shall 
provide a revised issuance date. Any unresolved issue shall be resolved in accordance with 
Section 9.1.

Description of Schedule and Cost Estimate for the Facilities Study 
Study schedule. Transmission Owner shall use reasonable efforts to complete the Facilities Study 
within twelve (12) months, consisting of six (6) months for the study of Transmission Owner 
Attachment Facilities and local Network Upgrades and six (6) months for the study of non-local 
Network Upgrades. The twelve (12)-month study period shall commence on the later of (i) 
Transmission Owner’s receipt of the Study Deposit or (ii) Transmission Owner’s determination 
that the Study Data Package is complete. If Transmission Owner determines that the Facilities 
Study cannot be completed within the applicable twelve (12)-month study period, Transmission 
Owner shall notify Large Load Customer and provide an explanation for the delay and a revised 
estimated completion date. 
Study Deposit. Based on the size of the Large Load Project, as follows: 50 MW to 199 MW – 
$300,000; 200 MW to 499 MW – $350,000; 500 MW and above – $400,000. The Study Deposit

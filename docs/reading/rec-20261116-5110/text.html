On June 18, 2026 the Commission issued its Order Instituting Proceeding Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act (“Show Cause Order”),1 requiring each Participating Transmission Owner (“PTO”) within the California Independent System Operator’s (“CAISO”) system to address issues associated with serving large load and co-location arrangements.  In this filing, pursuant to Section 205 of the Federal Power Act, Citizens S-Line Transmission LLC (“Citizens S-Line”) files proposed revisions to its Transmission Owner Tariff (“TO Tariff”) to address the issues raised in the Show Cause Order.  Citizens S-Line respectfully requests that the Commission accept these TO Tariff revisions to become effective January 15, 2027, which is sixty (60) days from the date of this filing.

I. BACKGROUND ON CITIZENS S-LINE

Citizens S-Line is a wholly owned subsidiary of Citizens Energy Corporation, a non-profit organization dedicated to making life’s basic needs more affordable through successful business ventures.  To date, Citizens

Energy Corporation has donated more than $600 million to programs supporting the most vulnerable in our communities.

In 2024 Citizens S-Line invested approximately $40 million with the Imperial Irrigation District (“IID”) in the S-Line Upgrade Project (“S-Line Project”) in exchange for a 40-year leasehold interest in the incremental transfer capability created by the S-Line Project.2  Citizens S-Line does not own any of the actual facilities.  IID retains ownership and operation and maintenance responsibilities.  Citizens S-Line became a CAISO PTO, turned its interest in the S-Line Project over to the CAISO’s operational control as an Entitlement and recovers its costs through its TO Tariff.  As reflected in its TO Tariff, Citizens S-Line is a “Non-Load-Serving Participating TO and has no End-Use Customers.”3

As part of Citizens Energy Corporation’s charitable mission, Citizens S-Line is contractually committed to dedicate 50% of its net after-tax cash flow from the S-Line Project to programs assisting IID’s low-income families. To date, the S-Line Project has generated $1.1 million in charitable donations, which are currently being used to support bill relief through IID’s Safety Net Program and to support critical social service organizations such as IID’s Neighborhood Partner Support Program.  Citizens S-Line estimates its involvement in the S-Line Project will generate approximately $18 million in charitable assistance over the term of the lease.

II. RESPONSE TO SHOW CAUSE ORDER

The Commission’s Show Cause Order determined that the TO Tariffs of the CAISO PTOs, including Citizens S-Line, “lack provisions that address, with sufficient clarity and consistency” how each PTO “will timely study…the provision of transmission service to Eligible Customers on behalf of large loads.”4  The Commission directed each PTO to either justify why its TO Tariff remained just and reasonable, or to provide TO Tariff revisions necessary to remedy the Commission’s concerns.

As discussed in more detail below, Citizens S-Line has a limited leasehold interest in an Entitlement to incremental transfer capacity associated with a single upgrade project on the S-Line.   Ownership of the transmission line, as well as responsibility for operating and maintaining the line, remains with IID.  For these reasons, Citizens S-Line proposes revisions to its TO Tariff to make clear that any study, planning, interconnection or expansion requests related to Citizens S-Line’s interest in the S-Line Project will be governed by the relevant provisions and policies of IID, including its Open Access Transmission Tariff.

Citizens S-Line additionally proposes revisions to its TO Tariff to make it consistent with the TO Tariffs of Citizens Sunrise Transmission LLC and Citizens Sycamore-Penasquitos Transmission LLC, two other CAISO PTOs that are also wholly owned subsidiaries of Citizens Energy Corporation.  The S-Line transmission line is located within IID’s Balancing Authority Area and is therefore not within the footprint of the CAISO’s Balancing Authority Area, nor is IID a CAISO PTO.  As a result, Citizens S-Line’s interest in the Project is limited to an Entitlements interest in certain transmission capacity, and it’s initial TO Tariff included more limited provisions than those of Citizens Sunrise Transmission LLC or Citizens Sycamore- Penasquitos Transmission LLC, two other CAISO PTOs that are also wholly owned subsidiaries of Citizens Energy Corporation.  In particular, Citizens S- Line’s TO Tariff contained a more limited Section 3 (TO Definitions), Section 8 (Obligations to Interconnect or Construct Transmission Expansions and Facility Upgrades), Section 9 (Expansion Process), and Section 10 (Interconnection Process).

In response to the Show Cause Order, Citizens S-Line is proposing revisions that would expand these sections so that they mirror those of the other Citizens Energy Corporation PTOs, which Citizens S-Line believes will allow for more clarity on the provision of service to any large load or co- located arrangement. These revisions include the following:

• Revisions to Section 3, (TO Definitions) to expand the definition section, including, but not limited to adding definitions for Expedited Interconnection Agreement, Facilities Study Agreement, Facility or Facilities Study, Interconnection, Interconnection Agreement, Interconnection Application, Reliability Upgrade, Requests for Expedited Interconnection Procedures, System Impact Study, and

System Impact Study Agreement, each to clarify that IID’s policies and provisions will govern.5

• Expanded and added new language to Section 8, Obligations to Interconnect or Construct Transmission and Expansions and Facility Upgrades, to confirm that all conduct that falls under Section 8 will be governed by IID’s policies and provisions.

• Expanded and added new language to Section 9, Expansion Process, to confirm that all conduct that falls under Section 9 will be governed by IID’s policies and provisions.

• Expanded and added new language to Section 10, Interconnection Process, to confirm that all conduct that falls under Section 10 will be governed by IID’s policies and provisions.

This provision is similar to the provisions included in the TO Tariffs of Citizens Sunrise Transmission LLC and Citizens Sycamore Transmission LLC and is necessary to confirm that Citizens S-Line’s interest in the S-Line Project is limited by the terms of the Lease between IID and Citizens S-Line.  The Lease is included as Appendix II to the Citizens S-Line TO Tariff, and confirms that IID has all ownership, operational and maintenance responsibilities,6 and that any interconnections to the S-Line Project will be governed by the terms of the IID Open Access Transmission Tariff.7

• Citizens S-Line additionally makes a series of administrative revisions to address typos, update addresses, and similar type changes with this filing.

Each of these revisions is shown in redline in Attachment B to this filing, which is a redline of the Citizens S-Line TO Tariff.  A clean version of the revised TO Tariff is included as Attachment A.

Citizens S-Line respectfully submits that these revisions address the concerns raised in the Show Cause Order within the bounds of Citizens S- Line’s limited interest in, and control over, the S-Line Project.  Put simply, Citizens S-Line is bound by the terms of its Incremental Transfer Capability Lease with IID, and that agreement makes it clear that IID, as the owner and operator of the S-Line Project, has the authority and responsibility for all operational and interconnection related matters.

To the extent that the CAISO proposes TO Tariff revisions that impact the revisions Citizens S-Line proposes in this filing, Citizens S-Line commits to making a compliance filing to ensure its TO Tariff remains consistent.

III. REQUESTED EFFECTIVE DATE

Citizens S-Line requests an effective date for the revised TO Tariff of January 15, 2027.

VIII. CONCLUSION

Citizens S-Line respectfully requests that the Commission accept these proposed tariff revision effective as of January 15, 2027, and determine that Citizens S-Line has satisfied the concerns the Commission identified in its Show Cause Order.

ANSWER OF
EAST KENTUCKY POWER COOPERATIVE, INC. TO
MOTION FOR EXTENSION OF TIME TO FILE COMMENTS

Pursuant to Rule 213 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure1 and the August 31, 2026 Notice Shortening Answer Period in this proceeding,2 East Kentucky Power Cooperative, Inc. (“EKPC”)3 submits this Answer in support of the Motion for Extension of Time filed by the Organization of PJM States, Inc. (“OPSI”).4 In this proceeding, PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (“PJM”) has proposed revisions to its Open Access Transmission Tariff (“Tariff”) and Reliability Assurance Agreement Among Load Serving Entities (“RAA”) to implement Interim Resource Adequacy Service (“IRAS”). PJM’s proposal is complex and materially different from the “Connect and Manage” (the predecessor to IRAS) proposal considered in the stakeholder process. Allowing participants additional time to review PJM’s proposal and provide substantive comments will provide for a more thorough and complete record for the Commission to review as it considers PJM’s proposal. An extension of time to September 14, 2026 is reasonable given the complexities of PJM’s proposal, and the requested extension is consistent with Commission precedent in recent

ER27-527-000 · 2026-11-16 · Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.

MISO files the Grid Responsive Integration Transmission Service under section 205

MISO files tariff revisions under section 205 on November 16, 2026, in a new docket, ER27-527-000, creating the Grid Responsive Integration Transmission Service (GRITS). It is a permanent mix of firm and non-firm contract demand service for large loads willing to limit their withdrawals. MISO says it cannot run before January 1, 2029.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective March 16, 2027, 120 days after filing, with implementation in early 2029.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-8 show cause order's call for a permanent contract demand service for flexible loads in MISO.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-8 MISO: Services for flexible / co-located loads (P 6(d), p. 7) E-8 MISO: Alternative transmission technologies (P 119, p. 74)

Key points

  1. GRITS is a permanent firm and non-firm contract demand service for large loads that can cut their withdrawals.
    GRITS provides a permanent firm and non-firm contract demand-based, Transmission Service option for Large Loads willing and able to limit their withdrawals under specified price and reliability conditions. PDF p. 1
  2. MISO says it answers FERC's concern that no permanent contract demand service exists in MISO.
    the need for a permanent contract demand transmission service in the MISO region PDF p. 2
  3. The earliest start is January 1, 2029, because of software changes.
    the earliest MISO will be able to implement GRITS is January 1, 2029 PDF p. 2
  4. Example: a 500 MW load could take 200 MW firm and 300 MW non-firm.
    a Large Load Transmission Customer representing a Large Load of 500 MW may desire to only take 200 MW of firm transmission service PDF p. 4
  5. Customers need not move toward firm service.
    does not require the Large Load Transmission Customer to progress toward firm service for any or all of its GRITS Load Limit PDF p. 3
  6. GRITS is not limited to loads served by a co-located generator.
    is not limited to Large Load being served by a co-located generator PDF p. 4
  7. Rates are still being developed with the transmission owners.
    to continue the development of rates applicable to GRITS in concert with the MISO Transmission Owners PDF p. 2
  8. A customer must request GRITS through the Large Load Project Review, the same study route as other large-load service.
    A Large Load Transmission Customer must request GRITS through the applicable LLPR process PDF p. 4

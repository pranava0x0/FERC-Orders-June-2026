Chang explains why the June record needs upgrade costs

Commissioner Judy Chang · Direct response

Answering Senator Hirono, Chang linked the June section 206 proceedings to tracking which data center triggered a network upgrade and identifying who would otherwise pay. She described the information needed to limit cost shifting, not the outcome of a regional case.

Who pays when a data center brings its own generator?

Commissioner David LaCerte · Direct response

Answering Senator Cotton, LaCerte said beneficiaries should pay for the new infrastructure and states should hold hyperscalers to their commitments in retail rates. He did not specify a PJM cost formula.

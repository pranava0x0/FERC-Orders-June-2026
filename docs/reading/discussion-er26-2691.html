Positions read for this site

Commissioner Judy Chang

· ER26-2691-000 · 2026-10-09

In the separate Duke case, Chang dissented because Duke had not shown that its security requirements backed the full 15-year payment promise. The comparison bears on PJM’s proposed collateral; Chang was not evaluating PJM’s dollar amount.

Commissioner Judy Chang

· ER26-2691-000 · 2026-10-09

Chang said Duke had not demonstrated that a promise to pay for 75% of forecast demand would protect existing customers if the responsible utility defaulted. She wanted security commensurate with the 15-year commitment.

Commissioner Lindsay See

· ER26-2691-000 · 2026-10-09

See saw the minimum revenue commitment as an improvement over charging for use alone. She said 75% might be near the low end of an acceptable range and urged Duke to consider incremental charges where appropriate.

Commissioner David Rosner

· ER26-2691-000 · 2026-10-09

Rosner said Duke’s previous tariff contained no comparable protection for households or small businesses. He accepted the added protection and said FERC should consider more if action by the states proved insufficient.

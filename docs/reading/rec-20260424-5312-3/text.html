706 Rates and Charges
Eligible Customers taking Interim Network Integration Transmission Service shall pay the charges set forth in this Section 706.

706.1 Monthly Demand Charge
[Reserved]
Eligible Customer shall pay a monthly Demand Charge for Zone Network Load and Non-Zone Network Load, which shall be determined as follows:  Eligible Customer’s measured demand amount (in MWs) multiplied by the applicable zonal Network Integration Transmission Service rate established pursuant to Section 34.1.

Revenue Crediting.  Each month, revenue credits will be applied to the gross Monthly Demand Charge to determine the actual charge to the Transmission Customer.  Revenue credits shall be calculated based on the sum of a Zone’s share of revenues collected during the month from:  (i) the Grid Reliance Charge under Schedule [GRC]; and (ii) the Non-Firm Contract Demand Charge under Schedule [NFCD].  The sum of these revenues will appear as a reduction to the gross monthly rate stated above on a Transmission Customer’s bill in that month for service under this schedule.

706.2 Redispatch Charge
The Eligible Customer and each Transmission Owner shall pay any redispatch costs as set forth in Tariff, Attachment K.

714 Rates and Charges for Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service
Eligible Customers taking Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service shall pay the charges set forth in this Section 714.

714.1 Monthly Demand Charge
[Reserved]
Eligible Customer shall pay a Monthly Demand Charge based on the Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service amount (MW), as specified in Eligible Customer’s Service Agreement, in accordance with Tariff, Schedule [FCD].

714.2 Network Upgrade Charge
[Reserved]
714.3 Redispatch Charge

SCHEDULE [FP]
Financial Penalty Charge for
Unreserved Use by Co-Located Load
An Eligible Customer taking Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service, Non-Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service, or Interim Network Integration Transmission Service on behalf of Co-Located Load shall be subject to financial penalties for its unreserved use of the transmission system.

The financial penalty charge for unreserved use shall include two components: (1) a base penalty charge, which applies to each individual hour in which the reserved transmission service limit is exceeded, and (2) a penalty ratchet, which applies when such exceedances occur repeatedly. Base Penalty Charge

When an Eligible Customer taking service on behalf of Co-Located Load exceeds its reserved transmission service limit, a base penalty charge shall be assessed for any hour in which at least one five-minute interval occurs during which the Eligible Customer’s telemetered load at the Point of Delivery exceeds its reserved transmission service limit.  This five-minute interval is defined as an Exceedance Interval, and such an hour is defined as an Exceedance Hour.  The base penalty charge shall apply to each Exceedance Hour.

For an Eligible Customer taking only Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service, the reserved transmission service limit shall equal the Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service minus any curtailed MW.

For an Eligible Customer taking only Non-Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service, the reserved transmission service limit shall equal the approved Non-Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service minus any curtailed MW.

For an Eligible Customer taking Firm Contract Demand and Non-Firm Contract Demand Transmission Services, the reserved transmission service limit shall equal the Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service plus the approved Non-Firm Contract Demand Transmission Service minus any curtailed MW.

For an Eligible Customer taking Interim Network Integration Transmission Service, the reserved transmission service limit shall equal the Interim Network Integration Transmission Service demand minus any curtailed MW.

Each five-minute interval beginning with the first minute of each hour shall constitute a separate interval and each hour from 0100 through 2400 shall constitute a separate hour for purposes of determining whether one or more exceedances have occurred.

The base penalty charge shall be applied to each Exceedance Hour and shall be calculated as: Hourly Exceedance MW × Equivalent Hourly Firm Contract Demand (“FCD”) rate × number of Exceedance Intervals within the hour

Where:
Hourly Exceedance MW = Maximum telemetered load during the hour – Reserved Transmission Service Limit for the hour

Equivalent Hourly FCD rate = FCD rate / 8,760 hours
Penalty Ratchet
A penalty rachet shall apply when multiple Exceedance Hours occur during a particular time period in order to escalate the penalty charges as the frequency of unreserved transmission use increases. If more than one Exceedance Hour occurs on the same calendar day, an additional daily penalty shall apply.

If exceedances occur on multiple calendar days within the same calendar week, an additional weekly penalty shall apply.

If exceedances occur in multiple calendar weeks within the same calendar month, an additional monthly penalty shall apply.

If exceedances occur in multiple calendar months within the same calendar year, an additional yearly penalty shall apply.

The penalty ratchet shall be based on an Eligible Customer’s exceedance pattern and applies escalating FCD-equivalent rates as the frequency of unreserved transmission use increases. Depending on whether exceedances occur within the same calendar day, calendar week, calendar month, or calendar year, the penalty ratchet shall apply daily, weekly, monthly, or annual penalty charges, respectively, as follows:

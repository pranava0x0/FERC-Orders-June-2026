Comments of Jessica Kivell asking the Commission to keep transparency, cost responsibility, reliable planning, and protection of existing customers at the center of the proceeding

An Arkansas resident and electric customer files a short public comment in the MISO show cause docket urging FERC to keep transparency, cost responsibility and protection of existing customers central as large loads grow.

Asks FERC to: Keep transparency, cost responsibility, reliable planning and protection of existing customers at the center of FERC's consideration of large loads.

Answers the order. Public comment in the E-8 docket on the transparency and cost-responsibility concerns the MISO show cause order raises.

ER27-25-000 · 2026-10-02 · MISO

Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.'s Large Load Addition Resource Study Process (transmittal letter; requested effective date December 2, 2026)

MISO files tariff revisions under FPA section 205 to create the Large Load Addition Resource Study, a parallel 120-day study of loads of 200 MW or more and their paired generation, and says the filing responds to the E-8 show cause order.

Asks FERC to: Accept the LARS tariff revisions effective December 2, 2026, with a Commission order by that date so the first LARS study period can start in the first quarter of 2027.

Answers the order. MISO's section 205 answer to the E-8 show cause order on electrically proximate load, tying LARS to the order's resource adequacy request and deferring the cost recovery agreement to a November 16 filing.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-8 MISO: Cost-recovery agreement (P 6(b), p. 6) E-8 MISO: Proximate-generation service (P 6(e), p. 7)

Key points

  1. MISO asks for a December 2, 2026 effective date so it can start the first study period in Q1 2027.
    MISO requests that the Tariff revisions submitted herewith be made effective December 2, 2026 PDF p. 2
  2. MISO says LARS responds to the show cause order finding on study procedures for electrically proximate large loads.
    MISO's existing Tariff may be unjust and unreasonable because it does not provide generator interconnection study procedures and services that account for the operational dynamics of serving electrically proximate large loads. PDF p. 3
  3. MISO moved the load threshold from 250 MW to 200 MW after stakeholder input.
    Stakeholders proposed alternative thresholds, including 200 MW, which MISO proposes herein PDF p. 3
  4. Load and generation studies run together and finish in about 120 days.
    The generation and load studies will be completed at the same time and will finish within approximately 120 days. PDF p. 5
  5. Only the Associated Transmission Customer, often a load-serving entity, can initiate an application, and a $250,000 non-refundable fee applies.
    A non-refundable fee of $250,000 must accompany the LARS Application and be paid by the LARS Associated Transmission Customer. PDF p. 8
  6. MISO takes ten applications per study period and runs three periods a year.
    There will be three LARS study periods each year and each study period will be completed in approximately 120 days PDF p. 9
  7. The generator is capped at 150 percent of the load's peak demand, and the pair must sit in one Local Resource Zone.
    limiting the amount of requested Interconnection Service to no more than 150% of the peak demand of the LARS Associated Load PDF p. 10
  8. MISO says costs from load-side and common violations go to Baseline Reliability or Other projects, and the pro forma cost shifting agreement follows by November 16, 2026.
    a pro forma cost shifting agreement will need to be executed and any new terms in transmission services agreements will need to be met. PDF p. 14
  9. Pairs must stay together for 15 years or the generator's LLGIA ends after a transition of up to one year.
    MISO will terminate the LLGIA after a limited operations transition period PDF p. 16

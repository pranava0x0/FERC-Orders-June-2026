Request for Abeyance of Southern California Edison Company, et al. in response to the Order issued on 06/18/2026

Fifteen CAISO transmission owners (the Joint PTOs) ask FERC to hold the 60-day show cause response deadline in abeyance for 90 days, to November 16, 2026, so each can prepare section 205 TO Tariff filings coordinated with CAISO.

Asks FERC to: Hold the deadline to respond to the show cause order and briefing questions in abeyance until November 16, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Requests the up-to-90-day abeyance option in the E-10 CAISO show cause order and previews TO Tariff filings on the study process and the pro forma cost recovery agreement. FERC granted it August 14, 2026.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Cost-recovery agreement (§ III.B.3, p. 25)

Key points

  1. The Joint PTOs seek a 90-day full abeyance of the 60-day response deadline to prepare section 205 TO Tariff filings.
    The Joint PTOs seek a 90-day full abeyance of the current 60-day deadline to respond to all issues raised in the OSC PDF p. 2
  2. Tariff changes will vary by PTO because some serve load and others do not.
    some PTOs serve load while others do not, for example PDF p. 3
  3. Filing on August 17 would likely conflict with CAISO's design, so the Joint PTOs want a coordinated set of filings.
    Filing tariff revisions on August 17 would require the Joint PTOs to submit tariff provisions that would likely conflict with the CAISO’s design PDF p. 4
  4. The Joint PTOs read the co-location, flexible service and proximate generation findings as aimed at the CAISO Tariff.
    the third, fourth, and fifth issues – co-located load, flexible services, and proximate load – are primarily directed at the CAISO Tariff PDF p. 5
  5. CAISO's Board acts October 28, so CAISO Tariff revisions are unlikely to be final until near November 16.
    The CAISO does not currently plan to seek Board approval until October 28, just over two and a half weeks before a 90-day extended filing deadline PDF p. 6
  6. The expected filings cover study, application and operational procedures plus transparency, cost recovery, financial security and rate treatment.
    present revisions to the TO Tariffs to include study, application, and operational procedures for the interconnection of large loads PDF p. 7
  7. The Joint PTOs also ask that the briefing question deadline be tolled for 90 days.

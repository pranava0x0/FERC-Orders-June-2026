ER26-3515-000 · 2026-08-28 · Organization of PJM States, Inc.

Motion for Extension of Time of the Organization of PJM States, Inc. to submit comments in response to PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.'s 08/13/2026 proposal

The Organization of PJM States moves for an 11-day extension of the comment deadline on PJM's Interim Resource Adequacy Service and Large Load Registry filing. PJM does not oppose the motion.

Asks FERC to: Extend the comment deadline in ER26-3515 from September 3 to September 14, 2026, with action by September 1.

Changes the schedule. Moves the comment schedule in PJM's separate interim resource adequacy docket ER26-3515, PJM's proposal on how to serve load that outpaces capacity, the subject of the E-7 adequacy report.

E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8)

Key points

  1. OPSI asks to move comments from September 3 to September 14, 2026, and PJM does not oppose.
    OPSI requests that the Commission extend the time for comments in this docket from September 3, 2026 to September 14, 2026. PDF p. 1
  2. PJM's August 13 filing proposes a load reduction service for new large loads and a large load registry.
    a new supplemental capacity product through which New Large Loads [(“NLL”)] provide a load reduction service that is a tool available to PJM to manage resource adequacy shortfalls PDF p. 1
  3. PJM's compensation mechanism creates new obligations for Electric Distributors and touches retail ratemaking.
    PJM proposes a compensation mechanism for loads that provide this new service, which creates new obligations for Electric Distributors. PDF p. 2
  4. State regulators need time to assess the interaction of PJM, distributors and retail regulators in implementing the service.
    to allow the retail regulators in the PJM region additional time to consider the complex interaction between the rights and responsibilities of PJM, Electric Distributors, and retail regulators PDF p. 2

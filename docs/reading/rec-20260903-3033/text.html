UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION

Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc. 
Docket No.
ER26-3552-000


NOTICE DENYING EXTENSION OF TIME

(September 3, 2026)

On August 28, 2026, pursuant to Rules 212 and 2008 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure,[fn:2] Michigan Environmental Justice Coalition, Community Collaboration on Climate Change, Natural Resources Defense Council, and Sustainable FERC Project (collectively, Public Interest Organizations) filed a motion requesting an extension of time, from September 8, 2026, to September 15, 2026, to submit comments to the tariff filing submitted by Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc. in the above-captioned proceeding.  Public Interest Organizations state that, given the complexity of the proceeding, good cause exists to grant the extension of time because it would give parties additional time to review the filing, evaluate the issues presented, consult with relevant technical and legal personnel, and prepare meaningful comments for the Commission’s consideration.  On September 2, 2026, the Mississippi Public Service Commission, the Arkansas Public Service Commission, and the Louisiana Public Service Commission (collectively, Southern Regulators) filed an answer in support of Public Interest Organizations’ motion.  On September 3, 2026, the Council of the City of New Orleans joined the Southern Regulators’ answer in support of Public Interest Organizations’ motion.
Upon due consideration, notice is hereby given that Public Interest Organizations’ motion for an extension of time is denied.  The deadline to submit interventions, protests, and comments in the above-referenced proceeding remains 5pm ET September 8, 2026. 				



Carlos D. Clay,
Deputy Secretary.

Footnotes
[fn:2] 18 C.F.R. §§ 385.212, 385.2008 (2025).

EL26-67-000 · 2026-08-10 · NATURAL GAS SUPPLY ASSOCIATION (DC)

Comments of the Natural Gas Supply Association on the Informational Reports re Resource Adequacy Measures and Proposals, PJM Interconnection L.L.C., et al

The Natural Gas Supply Association writes Chairman Swett on the RTO informational reports, asking FERC to have RTOs and ISOs add an assessment of whether gas pipeline infrastructure and fuel supply can support projected demand growth.

Asks FERC to: Direct RTOs and ISOs to supplement their resource adequacy informational reports with a section on gas infrastructure sufficiency, regional shortfall initiatives and generator fuel adequacy efforts.

Answers the order. Comment on the 30-day adequacy reports all six show cause orders required, asking that those reports and future updates address gas pipeline and fuel assurance.

E-7 PJM: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 8) E-8 MISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7) E-9 SPP: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7) E-10 CAISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7) E-11 ISO-NE: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7) E-12 NYISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7)

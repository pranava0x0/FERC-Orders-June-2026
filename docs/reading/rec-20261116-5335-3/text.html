RECITALS
WHEREAS, Customer has submitted a Large Load Integration Request to interconnect or serve a Large Load Facility with an aggregate demand equal to or exceeding fifty megawatts (50 MW) at a transmission-system interconnection point with the Transmission Owner’s service territory or affecting the Affected Transmission Owner’s transmission facilities in New York State; WHEREAS, New York Independent System Operator, Inc. (“NYISO”) and/or Transmission Owner have performed [system reliability impact studies, facility studies, and/or engineering assessments] pursuant to the NYISO Open Access Transmission Tariff (“OATT”), identifying that the interconnection and commercial operation of Customer’s Large Load Facility will necessitate the construction and placement into service of certain Network Upgrades and/or TO Attachment Facilities on the Transmission Owner’s transmission system to maintain electric system reliability and system adequacy; and

WHEREAS, Customer has received the initial cost allocation estimate for Network Upgrades and/or TO Attachment Facilities identified by System Impact Studies and any applicable Facilities Studies under Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT or successor tariff provisions needed to interconnect Customer’s Large Load Facility;

WHEREAS, the Initial Project Security Amount is subject to adjustment in accordance with the Interconnection Cost Allocation Process as set forth in Section [__] of Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT (the “Project Security Amount”);

WHEREAS, the Project Security Amount may thereafter be adjusted pursuant to the Large Load Cost Recovery Agreement (“CRA”), into which Customer has entered or will enter and pursuant

1.0
Defined Terms. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the NYISO OATT, NYISO Market Services Tariff, or, if not defined therein, in the CRA or applicable Large Load Interconnection Agreement, if those agreements are in effect.

1.1
“Abandon” means Customer’s (i) failure to issue a notice to proceed for Transmission Owner to commence construction of the Project Facilities within [six (6)] months after the applicable construction commencement milestone set forth in Exhibit D of the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, or (ii) suspension of, or causing the suspension of, construction of the Project Facilities for a period of [three (3)] years, which, in either case, is not due to an event of Force Majeure; provided that if Force Majeure causes such failure or suspension, Customer shall nevertheless be deemed to have abandoned construction if it fails to commence or resume construction within [six (6)] months after the end of the Force Majeure event (unless Customer has elected to continue such suspension for a total suspension period not exceeding [three (3)] years in accordance with the Large Load Interconnection Agreement or CRA).

“Force Majeure” means any event or circumstance beyond a Party’s reasonable control that prevents the Party from performing an obligation, is not the result of the negligence or intentional wrongdoing of such Party, and could not have been prevented or mitigated by the exercise of Good Utility Practice (in the case of the Transmission Owner) or due diligence (in the case of Customer), including acts of God, fire, flood, storm, lightning, explosion, breakage of or accident to machinery, epidemic or pandemic, cyber-attack (except to the extent caused by or resulting from Customer’s failure to comply with its obligations under this Agreement), sabotage, terrorism, war, civil disturbance, strikes and labor disturbances, acts or orders of any Governmental Authority, and curtailment or interruption directed by NYISO for reliability reasons. Force Majeure shall not include, with respect to Customer: (a) economic hardship, changes in market conditions, or the cost or unavailability of financing; (b) delays in Customer’s procurement of equipment or failure of Customer’s suppliers or contractors, unless caused by an independent Force Majeure event; (c) labor disputes involving only Customer’s or its contractors’ workforce;

ARTICLE II: SECURITY
2.1
Posting and Maintenance of Security. On or before each Security Posting Date, Customer shall provide Transmission Owner Security for Customer’s Obligations in the then-applicable Security Amount, and Customer shall maintain Security in the Security Amount at all times until the Security Release Date. Cash shall be wired to Transmission Owner in accordance with Transmission Owner’s written instructions delivered at least five (5) Business Days before the applicable Security Posting Date; if Transmission Owner delivers its instructions later, Customer’s deadline shall be extended until two (2) Business Days after receipt of the instructions. Each Letter of Credit shall be in form and substance acceptable to Transmission Owner. Customer shall provide a draft of each Letter of Credit to Transmission Owner for review at least five (5) Business Days before the applicable Security Posting Date. Transmission Owner shall have the opportunity to verify that the issuing bank satisfies the requirements of this Agreement, and the original Letter of Credit shall be delivered to Transmission Owner by hand delivery or overnight mail.

(a) The Initial Project Security Amount shall be established by reference to the initial cost estimate for Network Upgrades and/or TO Attachment Facilities identified by System Impact Studies and Facilities Studies under Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT or successor tariff provisions. This amount shall be entered at Schedule 1.

(b) The Security Amount shall be adjusted as prescribed in Sections 2.5, 2.6 and 2.7 of this Agreement and Article 6 of the CRA.

2.2
Letter of Credit Renewals and Replacements; Downgrades.
(a) Renewal. If a Letter of Credit is scheduled to expire before the Security Release Date, Customer shall renew or replace it no later than ninety (90) days before its expiration. If the issuing bank gives a non-extension notice, Customer shall provide acceptable replacement Security within five (5) Business Days after receiving that notice and in all events before the Letter of Credit expires. The renewed or replacement Letter of Credit shall expire no earlier than the later of (i) one (1) year after its issuance or renewal and (ii) the Security Release Date. Customer shall send evidence of the renewed or replaced Letter of Credit from the issuing bank to Transmission Owner within five (5) Business Days after renewal or replacement.

(b) Issuer Downgrade. If an issuing bank fails to maintain the Minimum Issuer Rating (an “Issuer Downgrade”), Customer shall deliver a substitute Letter of Credit within

PDF page 9

five (5) Business Days after the Customer knew or should have known of the Issuer Downgrade.

(c) Return of Replaced Letter of Credit. Upon receipt and acceptance of a substitute or replacement Letter of Credit, Transmission Owner shall, within five (5) Business Days after Customer’s request, return the replaced Letter of Credit to the issuing bank and notify Customer, unless Transmission Owner has drawn on it or a Draw Event is continuing.

• 2.3
Substitution of Security. If Customer replaces Cash with a Letter of Credit, or replaces a Letter of Credit with Cash or a Letter of Credit, in each case in an amount at least equal to the then-applicable Security Amount, Transmission Owner shall, within thirty (30) calendar days after receipt of the replacement Security, refund the replaced Cash or return the replaced Letter of Credit, as applicable, unless a Draw Event has occurred and is continuing. During any period in which Transmission Owner holds Security exceeding the Security Amount, Transmission Owner shall not apply, set off or draw on Security in an amount greater than the Security Amount. Cash refunds shall be made by wire in accordance with Customer’s written instructions delivered at least two (2) Business Days before the wire.

2.4
Draw Events. Transmission Owner may apply, set off and/or draw on the Security, up to the outstanding Security Amount, upon the occurrence of any of the following (each, a “Draw Event”):

(a) Customer notifies Transmission Owner in writing that it is ceasing to negotiate, or fails, within a reasonable period of time, to enter into the CRA, the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, or other applicable agreements, and either NYISO or the Transmission Owner has informed Customer in writing that the Project Facilities must be completed because they are included in the NYISO base case, Transmission Owner’s local transmission plan, or the NYISO’s Reliability Needs Assessment or Short-Term Assessment of Reliability or another interconnection request relies on completion of the Network Upgrades or TO Attachment Facilities; [(b)

Customer notifies Transmission Owner in writing that it is ceasing to negotiate, or fails, within a reasonable period of time, to enter into the CRA, the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, or other applicable agreements, and NYISO or Transmission Owner has determined that the Project Facilities are not needed, to the extent of costs to restore the New York State Transmission System to a safe and reliable condition;]

(c) An Event of Default has occurred under any Related Agreement and any applicable Cure Period has expired;

(d) Customer Abandons the Large Load Facility or a material portion of it, and the abandonment continues for thirty (30) days after Transmission Owner’s written notice to Customer;

(e) Customer fails to pay any amount, or any amount finally determined to be due, when due, under the CRA, and the failure continues beyond any applicable Cure Period;

PDF page 10

(f) The CRA has terminated prior to the expiration of the Assessment Period thereunder;

(g) A Letter of Credit will expire in thirty (30) days or less, or an earlier period required by Section 2.2(a), and Customer has not renewed or replaced it as required by Section 2.2(a);

(h) Customer fails to deliver substitute or replacement Security within the time required by Section 2.2(b);

(i) Customer fails to comply with a material term of this Agreement, or defaults under a material term of any Related Agreements, and the failure continues beyond any applicable Cure Period after Transmission Owner’s written notice describing the default; or

(j) Transmission Owner is otherwise entitled to draw on Security under any Related Agreement.

Upon a Draw Event, Transmission Owner may draw on or make demand under any or all of the Security without further notice to Customer. If Transmission Owner draws before it incurs the related costs, it shall hold the proceeds as Cash Security and apply them only to Customer’s Obligations in accordance with Section 2.5. Customer shall remain liable for all Customer’s Obligations that remain unpaid after application of the Security.

2.5
Application and Release of Security. Transmission Owner may apply any or all of the Security to Customer’s Obligations, including amounts owed by Customer to complete the Project Facilities or restore the New York State Transmission System to a safe and reliable condition. Upon a Draw Event, Transmission Owner shall apply the drawn funds (the “Funds”) to then-outstanding Customer’s Obligations and shall retain any Funds exceeding those obligations (“Excess Proceeds”) as security for future Customer’s Obligations. Within thirty (30) calendar days after the latest of the following dates (the “Security Release Date”), Transmission Owner shall refund any unapplied Cash and Excess Proceeds to Customer, and/or return any unapplied Letter of Credit to the issuing bank:

(i) the date on which all of Customer’s obligations under the Related Agreements have been satisfied;

(ii) the date on which the portions of the New York State Transmission System affected by construction of the Project Facilities have been restored to a safe and reliable condition, as confirmed in writing by NYISO or Transmission Owner, and all associated costs have been paid, as applicable, and all of Customer’s obligations under the Related Agreements have been satisfied (the “Restoration Completion Date”); or

(iii) the date on which Transmission Owner or NYISO confirms in writing that the Project Facilities are no longer required, Customer has paid all outstanding obligations, neither Party has any current or future obligation to restore the New York State Transmission System or to install or complete any Project Facilities, and all of Customer’s obligations under the Related Agreements have been satisfied (the “Obligation Termination Date”).

Cash refunds shall be wired to Customer in accordance with Customer’s written instructions delivered at least two (2) Business Days before the wire. Any interest earned on Cash Security or Excess Proceeds shall be for the account of Transmission Owner. Notwithstanding Section 9-207 of the New York Uniform Commercial Code and to the extent permitted by applicable law, Transmission Owner may invest, use, commingle or otherwise dispose of Cash Security and Excess Proceeds, subject to its obligations to apply or return those amounts under this Agreement. 2.6

 Reduction or Increase of Security Amount as a Result of the Interconnection Cost Allocation Process and/or the CRA.

Customer must timely post Security in the Initial Project Security Amount, subject to adjustment based on the Interconnection Cost Allocation Process in effect at the time of the Facilities Study. The Interconnection Cost Allocation Process shall provide the procedures pursuant to which cost allocations are adjusted when projects fail timely to post security following the release of the Facilities Studies until all remaining projects in the Cluster have posted required security. Required Security may also be adjusted upon execution of the CRA and adjusted annually thereafter to provide security for Customer’s obligations under the CRA for the remaining term.

If Customer provided a Letter of Credit as Security, the annual adjustment may occur at the time of renewal of the Letter of Credit by amendment or replacement of the Letter of Credit. If Customer provided Cash as Security, the annual adjustment shall be effectuated through either an increase in the Cash provided by Customer or a refund of Cash held by Transmission Owner. Within ten (10) Business Days after Customer’s written request following a reduction of the Security Amount, Transmission Owner shall refund by wire any Cash exceeding the adjusted Security Amount or return any replaced Letter of Credit (provided Customer has delivered a substitute Letter of Credit or amendment in the adjusted Security Amount) to the issuing bank. Changes to Schedule 1 under this Section shall not constitute an amendment of this Agreement.

2.7
Adjustment of Security Amount as a result of Change in Scope or Extension Request. The Security Amount shall be adjusted for reasons including but not limited to the following occurrences, and in each case as may be reflected in an amendment to the Large Load Interconnection Agreement and/or CRA:

(i) Change in Scope. If the scope of the Project Facilities is modified, expanded or otherwise changed from the scope described in the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, CRA, or other applicable agreements, including as a result of a Material Modification or an Exceedance Event under the Large Load Interconnection Agreement or updated engineering or design requirements, the Security Amount shall be adjusted by the amount of the change in Customer’s allocated share of the costs of the Project Facilities attributable to that change. Transmission Owner shall provide Customer with a written calculation of the adjustment.

(ii) Extension Request; Suspension or Delay. If Customer requests an extension of any milestone date in the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, CRA, or other applicable agreements, or suspends or delays performance under Section 3.3 of the CRA, and the extension, suspension or delay results in additional costs to Transmission Owner under the Large Load Interconnection Agreement or CRA,

the Security Amount shall be increased by the amount of those additional costs, as reasonably documented by Transmission Owner. Transmission Owner shall not require the increased Security Amount earlier than five (5) Business Days after delivering that documentation to Customer.

On the applicable Security Posting Date for an adjustment, Customer shall post additional Security in a form permitted by Section 2.1 if the Security Amount increases, or Transmission Owner shall take the actions described in Section 2.6 if the Security Amount decreases. Any increased Security Amount remains subject to reduction under Section 2.6.

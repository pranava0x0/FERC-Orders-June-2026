EL25-49-000 · 2026-10-14 · PJM

Motion for Leave to Answer and Answer of PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. to the limited protests of its August 17, 2026 compliance filing (EL25-49 and ER26-1479)

PJM answers the September 8 limited protests of Constellation, AMP and industrial customers to its August 17 co-location compliance filing, defending the July 1, 2028 Interim NITS date and agreeing to two clarifications in its November filing.

Asks FERC to: Grant leave to answer and accept the answer and the Pilong affidavit, and consider deferring the contested tariff issues until PJM's November compliance filing builds a fuller record.

Carries out the order. Defends PJM's E-2 co-location compliance, including the Interim NITS timeline, and previews the unified large load study PJM will file by November 16 in response to the E-7 show cause order.

E-2 PJM: Interim NITS (P 2, p. 3) E-7 PJM: Application / study process (P 6(a), p. 7) E-2 PJM: Retail BTMG netting rewritten (P 4, p. 3)

Key points

  1. PJM suggests FERC may defer the contested issues until its November compliance filing.
    it may be appropriate for the Commission to defer consideration of these issues until it has the benefit of a complete record following next month’s compliance filing. PDF p. 2
  2. PJM expects systems to accept requests for the new services in September 2027, after the changes needed for Interim NITS.
    PJM anticipates modifying the systems needed to begin accepting requests for the new transmission services in September of 2027. PDF p. 3
  3. PJM is designing a unified cluster study of large loads and generation, to be filed on or before November 16, 2026 in response to E-7.
    PJM is developing a proposal for a new a study process that would evaluate large loads and generation interconnection requests together in a unified cluster study that would take roughly a year to complete. PDF p. 3
  4. PJM gives a timeline running from a 2027 application window to service in the first quarter of 2029.
    application process in September of 2027, conducting the study in 2028, and beginning to provide service in first quarter 2029. PDF p. 4
  5. PJM says it will not repeat Necessary Studies already done, though changed scope can trigger more study.
    While PJM will not repeat studies already performed, project scope or other technical changes may trigger the need for additional study. PDF p. 4
  6. PJM answers Constellation that Interim NITS will be interruptible and curtailed before capacity-backed services.
    Interim NITS will be interruptible and curtailed before firm services like Firm Contract Demand Service or NITS that are capacity-backed services. PDF p. 5
  7. PJM will clarify in November that load with behind-the-meter generation is excluded from the Co-Located Load definition.
    PJM intends to propose clarifications to the definition of BTMG in the November compliance filing to specify that load with BTMG is excluded from the definition of Co-Located Load. PDF p. 7
  8. PJM does not object to AMP's proposed fix to the Eligible Generating Facility definition.
    PJM, does not object to AMP’s proposed revision to Eligible Generating Facility definition set forth in its pleading. PDF p. 7

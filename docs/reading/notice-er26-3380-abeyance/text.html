NOTICE ADDRESSING MOTION TO HOLD PROCEEDING IN ABEYANCE

(October 23, 2026)

On October 13, 2026, pursuant to Rule 212 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure,[fn:2] PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (PJM) filed a motion to hold the paper hearing proceedings and the submission of briefs in the above-captioned proceeding in abeyance pending resolution of PJM’s anticipated forthcoming filing that PJM states it plans to submit under section 205 of the Federal Power Act addressing the issues raised in a September 29, 2026, Commission order.[fn:3]  On October 14, 2026, Northern Virginia Electric Cooperative, Inc., filed an answer supporting PJM’s motion.
 
Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that the deadline for submission of initial briefs in Docket No. EL26-108-000 is extended from November 13, 2026 to 5pm on January 11, 2027, and the deadline for reply briefs is extended from December 3, 2026 to 5pm on January 26, 2027. 
 
					







Debbie-Anne A. Reese,
Secretary.

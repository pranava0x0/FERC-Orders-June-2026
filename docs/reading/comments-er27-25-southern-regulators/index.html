ER27-25-000 · 2026-10-23 · Mississippi and Louisiana PSCs

Comments on Behalf of the Southern Regulators (Mississippi and Louisiana PSCs) in Support of MISO Application

The Mississippi and Louisiana Public Service Commissions, as the Southern Regulators, file comments supporting MISO's LARS filing as a 120-day parallel study of loads above 200 MW and paired generation that responds to the show cause order.

Asks FERC to: Accept MISO's LARS filing.

Answers the order. Supports MISO's LARS filing as a response to the E-8 show cause order finding that MISO lacks study procedures for electrically proximate large loads.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The regulators describe LARS as meant to address the show cause order finding on electrically proximate large loads.
    The LARS proposal also is intended to address the Commission's June 18, 2026 Show Cause Order finding that MISO's Tariff may be unjust and unreasonable PDF p. 1
  2. LARS coordinates and speeds study of loads above 200 MW and the generation identified for them.
    coordinate and expedite the analyses needed to interconnect large loads above 200 MW and the new generation resources identified to support that new load. PDF p. 1
  3. Joint study can cut upgrade needs and costs, and LARS studies pairs in 120 days.
    LARS provides for a 120-day parallel study of eligible large loads and their paired generation resources. PDF p. 2
  4. They say permanence supplies the certainty needed to site and finance pairings.
    Making LARS a permanent process is reasonable because it provides certainty needed to site and finance large load-and-generation pairings. PDF p. 2
  5. They list the caps and eligibility rules that deter speculative requests.
    including a 150% generation to load ceiling, a $250,000 deposit, a three-year commercial operation date, and a same-Local-Resource-Zone requirement. PDF p. 2

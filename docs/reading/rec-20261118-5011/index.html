ER27-526-000 · 2026-11-18 · Clean Energy Advocate, S

Motion of Community Organizations and Clean Energy Advocates for a 30-day extension of time to submit comments re Midcontinent Independent System Operator,…

Community Organizations and Clean Energy Advocates, represented by Earthjustice, move in a motion dated November 17, 2026, in ER27-526-000, for a 30-day extension to comment on MISO's large load filing. They cite pending federal legislation and the number of related MISO filings. They ask for a ruling by November 23, 2026.

Asks FERC to: Extend the time to comment on MISO's filing to January 15, 2027, and rule by November 23, 2026.

Asks for more time to comment on MISO's section 205 filing, the filing MISO made to answer the E-8 show cause order. The change would align MISO's comment date with the 60 days PJM requested.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The movants ask for a 30-day extension of the comment period on MISO's November 16 filing.
    move for a 30-day extension of time to submit comments on Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.’s (“MISO”) November 16, 2026 filing PDF p. 1
  2. First reason: Congress is debating a bill that could bear on MISO's filing.
    Congress is actively debating the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act (“BAAJA”) PDF p. 2
  3. They say a section of the bill on transmission cost allocation may conflict with MISO's filing.
    BAAJA Section 2107 contains directives regarding transmission cost allocation that may be inconsistent with MISO’s filing PDF p. 2
  4. Second reason: the ordinary comment period is too short for this proceeding.
    the ordinary comment period is insufficient to allow parties to address the many issues raised by this complex and lengthy proceeding PDF p. 3
  5. They say MISO has split its response across at least four other section 205 filings.
    MISO has filed at least four piecemeal 205 filings prior to, or simultaneous with, the instant matter PDF p. 4
  6. They ask for a comment date on the motion and a ruling within a week.
    request the Commission set a comment date of November 19, 2026, on this motion, and render a decision by November 23, 2026 PDF p. 2
  7. The requested new deadline is January 15, 2027.
    GRANTING the requested extension of time, until January 15, 2027, to comment on MISO’s filing PDF p. 5

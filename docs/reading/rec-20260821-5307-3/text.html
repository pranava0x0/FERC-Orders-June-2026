Q. 
CAN YOU EXPLAIN WHY THE RBP COLLATERAL REQUIREMENT IS A 
5 
BURDEN TO NOVEC? 
6 
A. 
Yes. To give an idea of the relative impact on NOVEC, the RBP collateral amount exceeds 
7 
the $1.8 billion of assets on NOVEC’s balance sheets. I am uncertain whether financial 
8 
institutions would be willing to provide NOVEC with a letter of credit in an amount that 
9 
exceeds NOVEC’s assets. Even if NOVEC could procure a letter of credit, being so over-
10 
leveraged would severely damage NOVEC’s credit rating. NOVEC may not be able to 
11 
enter into bilateral wholesale energy and capacity transactions, where the sellers would 
12 
begin requiring greater credit assurance even though today they do not require NOVEC to 
13 
post collateral due to NOVEC’s strong credit rating. The resulting credit-support costs 
14 
associated with the bilateral transactions will increase power supply costs, which will be 
15 
passed onto retail customers. 
16

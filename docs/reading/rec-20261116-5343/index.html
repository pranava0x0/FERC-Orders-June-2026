2026-11-16 Large Load Tariff Amendment: Resubmission under different filing code

CAISO files its large load tariff amendment a second time on November 16, 2026, in ER27-532-000, under a different filing code. The text is identical to its filing in ER27-506-000: a 50 MW large load definition, co-location disclosure by generators, and a Flexible Interim Load Interconnection service. Deposits, security and cost recovery sit in the owners' tariffs.

Asks FERC to: Accept the revisions effective February 16, 2027, with scheduling coordinator changes on November 1, 2027 and settlement changes on April 30, 2028.

Filed under section 205 to answer the E-10 show cause order for CAISO. This is a resubmission of the ER27-506 filing under a different filing code, with the same page text.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-10 CAISO: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Cost-recovery agreement (§ III.B.3, p. 25) E-10 CAISO: Service for flexible loads (§ III.D, p. 62) E-10 CAISO: BTMG netting (pp. 60-61, p. 60)

Key points

  1. CAISO says its revisions, with the transmission owners', answer the show cause order.
    Taken together, the CAISO and Participating Transmission Owner (PTO) tariff revisions supply the tariff-level clarity, customer protections, and operational tools the Order seeks. PDF p. 1
  2. Deposits, security and cost recovery are left to each transmission owner's tariff.
    the PTOs will implement just and reasonable deposit, security, reimbursement, and cost-recovery provisions in their own tariffs PDF p. 3
  3. Generator interconnection customers must disclose plans to serve co-located load.
    requires generator interconnection customers to disclose plans to serve co- located load PDF p. 3
  4. The interim service lets flexible loads energize before network upgrades are finished.
    The CAISO’s Flexible Interim Load Interconnection (FILI) can permit earlier energization while network upgrades are pending PDF p. 3
  5. A permanent flexible service is left for later work.
    with additional work planned on permanent flexibility and other emerging configurations PDF p. 3
  6. A large load is a single site of 50 MW or more.
    and that has a peak load of 50 MW or greater. PDF p. 11
  7. Loads already operating or with a complete request before February 16, 2027 are not treated as large loads.
    the CAISO will not consider any load a large load subject to the CAISO tariff where the load is operational or has a complete request with the PTO before the CAISO tariff revisions’ effective date of February 16, 2027 PDF p. 13
  8. CAISO asks for a February 16, 2027 effective date.
    the CAISO requests that the Commission accept the tariff revisions contained in this filing effective February 16, 2027 PDF p. 46

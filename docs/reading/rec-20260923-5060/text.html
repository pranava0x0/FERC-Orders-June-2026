Pursuant to Rules 212 and 213 of the Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (“FERC” or the “Commission”),1 FirstEnergy Service Company

(“FirstEnergy”) respectfully files this Limited Answer in response to the answer to comments and protests submitted by PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (“PJM”) on September 10, 2026 (the

“September 10 PJM Answer”), in this proceeding on PJM’s proposal to establish a Reliability Backstop Procurement (“RBP”) mechanism.2

I. Answer
Although the September 10 PJM Answer purports to respond to the points made in the
August 21 FirstEnergy Comments and Protest,3 PJM fails to advance facts or law that satisfy its burden of proof under section 205 of the Federal Power Act (the “FPA”) to demonstrate that the July 31 RBP Proposal is just and reasonable.4  As explained in the August 21 FirstEnergy

Comments and Protest, there are a number of ways in which PJM has not met its burden of proof.5 The September 10 PJM Answer does not repair those defects.  Among other deficiencies, PJM’s answer fails to explain or address how its proposal could possibly be just and reasonable if it does not actually allocate RBP-related costs to the new data centers driving the need for such capacity, in accordance with the NEDC/PJM Governors’ Statement of Principles that underlies the RBP proposal.6

EL26-71-001 · 2026-09-21 · FERC

Order dismissing Eolian's request for rehearing and clarification of the June 18, 2026 CAISO show-cause order

FERC dismisses Eolian's request for rehearing or clarification of the CAISO show cause order. It finds the show cause order is not a final order, and declines to give guidance while the proceeding is in abeyance until November 16, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Disposes of a rehearing request against the E-10 CAISO show cause order and confirms the August 14 abeyance, leaving responses due November 16, 2026.

E-10 CAISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. Eolian asked that electrically proximate generation be allowed permanent load-limited generator interconnection service.
    electrically proximate generation may access permanent load-limited generator interconnection service where it is equally capable of enabling the large load to limit its withdrawals Source text
  2. Eolian also asked that CAISO be required to show its reforms open a pathway for significant generating capacity to take load-limited interconnection.
    must be paired with an obligation for CAISO to show that the reforms will enable a pathway for significant generating capacity to provide load-limited interconnection. Source text
  3. FERC says the show cause order made no final determination, only a preliminary finding that the tariffs appear unjust and unreasonable.
    The Commission determined only that the tariffs appear to be unjust, unreasonable, and unduly discriminatory or preferential and instituted an FPA section 206 show cause proceeding Source text
  4. FERC dismisses the clarification request and gives no guidance during the abeyance.
    We do not intend to provide clarification or guidance with respect to the Show Cause Order while the show cause proceeding is held in abeyance. Source text
  5. FERC restates that responses are due November 16 and answers December 16, 2026.
    responses to the Show Cause Order will be due on November 16, 2026, and answers to responses will be due on December 16, 2026. Source text

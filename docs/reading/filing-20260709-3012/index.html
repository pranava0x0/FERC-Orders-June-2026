EL26-72-000 · 2026-07-09 · SECRETARY OF THE COMMISSION, FERCCOMMISSIONERS AND IMMEDIATE STAFF (THE COMMISSION)

Errata to the Commission issued Order Instituting Proceeding Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act; correcting the caption re ISO New England Inc. et al

FERC's Secretary issues an errata notice correcting the caption and footnote 10 of the E-11 ISO-NE show cause order so Vermont Electric Cooperative is listed among the non-jurisdictional Participating Transmission Owners.

Changes the schedule. Corrects who counts as a Participating Transmission Owner that must respond to the E-11 show cause order; ISO-NE itself must still respond about non-public utilities' Tariff provisions.

E-11 ISO-NE: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The caption wrongly included Vermont Electric Cooperative, and footnote 10 omitted it.
    The caption in the order inadvertently included “Vermont Electric Cooperative, Inc.” and footnote 10 inadvertently omitted “Vermont Electric Cooperative, Inc.” Source text
  2. The notice deletes VEC from the caption and adds it to footnote 10.
    This errata notice hereby corrects the caption, as shown above, by deleting “Vermont Electric Cooperative, Inc.” Source text
  3. FERC keeps authority to review non-public-utility formula rates as part of ISO-NE's Tariff.
    the Commission retains the authority to review each entity’s formula rates as a component of ISO-NE’s Tariff in an order to show cause directed towards ISO-NE Source text

EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-03 · Southwest Power Pool, Inc.

SPP requests more time to answer the June 18 show cause order

SPP and a group of transmission owners (Joint Movants) move for a 95-day abeyance of the EL26-68 show cause proceeding, to November 20, 2026, and describe the section 205 revisions SPP plans on alternative technologies, cost shifting, co-location and flexible-load service.

Asks FERC to: Hold the proceeding, including the briefing questions deadline, in abeyance for 95 days through November 20, 2026, with a five-day answer period and prompt action.

Answers the order. Invokes the E-9 abeyance option and previews SPP's section 205 package on the order's alternative-technology, cost-recovery, co-location and flexible-load directives.

E-9 SPP: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-9 SPP: Evaluate alt tech by default (P 46, p. 52) E-9 SPP: Cost-recovery + crediting (Ordering (B)(1)(b), p. 21) E-9 SPP: New services for flexible loads (Ordering (B)(1)(d), p. 22)

Key points

  1. Joint Movants ask for 95 days, five more than the order contemplates, to fit SPP's Board meeting.
    The additional five (5) days is necessary to accommodate SPP's full stakeholder process, which culminates in a scheduled Board of Directors PDF p. 3
  2. SPP will add alternative transmission technology evaluation to its HILL study process, which does not now require it.
    it does not currently require SPP to evaluate alternative transmission technologies when studying HILLs. PDF p. 9
  3. SPP will revise pro forma NITS and point-to-point service agreements to carry HILL operational requirements.
    to develop revisions to the Tariff's pro forma transmission service agreements PDF p. 10
  4. The CARE Team is weighing financial security, direct assignment of upgrade costs and minimum contributions from customers serving large loads.
    direct assignment, in whole or in part, of Network Upgrade costs associated with large load delivery point additions or modifications PDF p. 13
  5. On co-location and behind-the-meter generation, stakeholders must settle terminology, the study process and overlap among transmission, interconnection and market registration.
    the overlap between transmission service, interconnection, and market registration requirements. PDF p. 15
  6. Price Adaptive Load Service would create non-firm service with usage-based pricing and mandatory dispatchability.
    PALS would establish a new transmission service under which participating loads would receive transmission service subject to usage-based pricing, mandatory dispatchability requirements PDF p. 16
  7. Working group votes run September and October, MOPC in October, RSC in November, and the Board on November 17, 2026.
    culminating in working group votes during September and October, MOPC in October, the RSC where applicable in November, and final Board consideration on November 17, 2026. PDF p. 17

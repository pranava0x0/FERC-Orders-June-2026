EL26-72-000 · 2026-08-03 · ISO New England Inc.PTO AC

ISO-NE requests more time to answer the June 18 show cause order

ISO-NE and the Participating Transmission Owners Administrative Committee jointly ask FERC to hold the E-11 show cause proceeding in abeyance for 90 days from August 17, 2026 and commit to a single section 205 filing by November 16, 2026.

Asks FERC to: Hold the proceeding in abeyance for 90 days starting August 17, 2026, with a five-day comment period and a ruling by August 13, 2026.

Answers the order. Uses the abeyance option in the E-11 order to postpone the 60-day deadline and maps ISO-NE's planned section 205 filing onto the order's five reform categories.

E-11 ISO-NE: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-11 ISO-NE: Application / study process (P 5, p. 25) E-11 ISO-NE: Alternative transmission technologies (P 75, p. 75) E-11 ISO-NE: Cost-recovery agreement (P 6, p. 6)

Key points

  1. The movants commit to file by November 16, 2026.
    ISO-NE and the PTOs will submit the FPA Section 205 filing to the Commission on or before November 16, 2026. PDF p. 2
  2. They ask for a shortened comment period and expedited ruling because the response is due August 17.
    respectfully request that the Commission shorten the comment period for this motion to five days and grant expedited treatment PDF p. 2
  3. They say more time is needed to develop proposals and present them for stakeholder review.
    additional time is needed to develop those proposals and present them for stakeholder review. PDF p. 4
  4. The filing will cover all five reform categories: study processes, cost transparency, co-location rules, flexible load service and generation studies.
    efficient study processes, cost transparency, co-location rules, flexible load service, and generation studies PDF p. 5
  5. Study reform will include a large load definition with a size and interconnection voltage threshold, and evaluation of alternative transmission technologies.
    This definition will be based on the characteristics of ISO-NE’s transmission system and will include load size and an interconnection voltage threshold. PDF p. 5

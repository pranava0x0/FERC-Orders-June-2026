EL26-71-000 · 2026-07-20 · California Independent System Operator Corporation

CAISO reports on resource adequacy and large-load proposals

CAISO files the 30-day generation adequacy informational report required by the show cause order. It says California's integrated planning shows no systemic shortfall, and it lays out the stakeholder schedule for its large load reforms.

Answers the order. Answers the E-10 CAISO order's 30-day directive for an informational report on how CAISO will ensure adequate generation, and sets out the stakeholder timeline later used in its abeyance motion.

E-10 CAISO: 30-day report on serving large loads with adequate generation (p. 7)

Key points

  1. CAISO says California's planning, led by the CEC, CPUC and CAISO, differs from the capacity-short markets in other regions.
    California's integrated resource and transmission planning framework is fundamentally different from many other organized markets that are confronting capacity shortfalls PDF p. 2
  2. The CEC forecasts data center load in the CAISO grid of 1.8 GW by 2030 and 4.9 GW by 2040.
    the CEC forecasts data center load in the CAISO grid to increase by 1.8 gigawatts (GW) by 2030 and 4.9 GW by 2040 PDF p. 4
  3. State projections in the 2025-2026 Transmission Plan have load growing 15 GW by 2035 and 20 GW by 2040.
    California load will grow by 15 GW by 2035 and 20 GW by 2040 PDF p. 5
  4. CAISO reports no systemic generation adequacy shortfall comparable to other regions.
    Current planning assessments do not indicate a systemic generation adequacy shortfall comparable to those in other regions. PDF p. 9
  5. Nearly 36 GW of new generation and storage came online for CAISO load from January 2020 to March 2026, including more than 16 GW of storage.
    nearly 36 GW of new generation and storage capacity coming online to serve CAISO load between January 2020 and March 2026 PDF p. 10
  6. CAISO's 2026 summer assessment finds a surplus of over 2.5 GW against the 1-in-10 reliability standard.
    The CAISO’s 2026 analysis indicates that the current portfolio meets that target with a surplus of over 2.5 GW. PDF p. 11
  7. Peak load in the WEIM region fell 3.8 percent in 2025 from 135.3 GW to 130.1 GW.
    Peak market load in the Western Energy Imbalance Market (WEIM) region was 130.1 GW in 2025, down 3.8 percent from the 2024 peak of 135.3 GW. PDF p. 13
  8. The Large Load stakeholder initiative is the vehicle for the show cause response, with a FERC filing on November 16, 2026.
    The CAISO’s Large Load stakeholder initiative will become the primary vehicle for CAISO reforms to address the Order to Show Cause. PDF p. 14

How California sets its plans

The California Energy Commission forecasts demand, the utilities commission sets the resource portfolio, and CAISO tests whether transmission can deliver it.

planning process incorporates the CEC's demand forecast and the CPUC's resource portfolios PDF p. 6

Transmission capacity · PDF pp. 8–13

Can the planned generation reach customers?

CAISO reports that its 2025 to 2026 planning studies examined portfolios with another 40 GW over ten years. Building generation only helps if developers can connect it and deliver the power.

Generation that cannot reach load is generation in name only. PDF p. 12

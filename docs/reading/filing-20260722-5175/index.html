EL26-67-000 · 2026-07-22 · Allegheny Electric Cooperative, Inc.

Motion to Rescind Show Cause Order of Allegheny Electric Cooperative, Inc

Allegheny Electric Cooperative, a non-profit G&T cooperative and PJM transmission owner, moves FERC to rescind the E-7 show cause order as to it because it is not a public utility under FPA section 201(f).

Asks FERC to: Rescind the PJM show cause order against Allegheny as a non-public utility and waive any notice and comment periods.

Changes the schedule. Seeks removal of one non-jurisdictional transmission owner from the E-7 show cause order's list of respondents, the same issue FERC fixed in its August 13 errata.

E-7 PJM: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 7)

Key points

  1. Allegheny says it is owned by its members and is not a public utility subject to FERC jurisdiction.
    Allegheny is not a public utility subject to FERC jurisdiction, pursuant to Section 201(f) of the Federal Power Act. PDF p. 3
  2. FERC exempted other non-public-utility owners from the order while keeping authority over their tariff provisions through PJM.
    it retains the authority to review each of these entities’ tariff provisions as a component of PJM’s Tariff in an order to show cause directed towards PJM PDF p. 4
  3. Allegheny cites the CAISO order's offer to rescind at a non-public utility's request and notes its PJM tariff portions contain no interconnection processes.
    we will rescind the show cause order against them at their request PDF p. 4
  4. Allegheny asks for rescission and a waiver of notice and comment periods.
    respectfully requests that the Commission rescind the PJM Show Cause Order against it, as a non-public utility PDF p. 5

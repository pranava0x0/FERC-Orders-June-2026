PRO FORMA LARGE LOAD COST RECOVERY AGREEMENT 
This PRO FORMA LARGE LOAD COST RECOVERY AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) 
is entered into as of this [___] day of [___], 20[__], by and between: 
• 
[NEW YORK TRANSMISSION OWNER] (“Transmission Owner” or “Connecting 
Transmission Owner” or “Affected Transmission Owner”), a [corporation] organized and 
existing under the laws of the State of New York, having an office and place of business 
at [___________]; and 
• 
[CUSTOMER NAME] (“Customer”), a [corporation / limited liability company] 
organized under the laws of the State of [______], having an office and place of business 
at [______], and owning a Large Load Facility with an expected peak demand of [__] 
MW at the Point of Interconnection. 
Transmission Owner and Customer are collectively referred to as the “Parties,” and each 
individually as a “Party.” 
RECITALS 
WHEREAS, Customer has submitted a Large Load Integration Request to interconnect or serve 
a Large Load Facility with an aggregate demand equal to or exceeding fifty megawatts (50 MW) 
[at a transmission-system interconnection point within the Transmission Owner’s service 
territory] or [affecting the Affected Transmission Owner’s transmission facilities in New York 
State]; 
WHEREAS, New York Independent System Operator, Inc. (“NYISO”) and/or Transmission 
Owner have performed system reliability impact studies, facility studies, and engineering 
assessments pursuant to the NYISO Open Access Transmission Tariff (“OATT”), identifying 
that the interconnection and commercial operation of Customer’s Large Load Facility will 
necessitate the construction and placement into service of certain Network Upgrades and/or TO 
Attachment Facilities on the [Transmission Owner’s] [Affected Transmission Owner’s] 
transmission system to maintain electric system reliability and system adequacy; and 
WHEREAS, Customer has received the cost allocation for Network Upgrades and/or TO 
Attachment Facilities identified by System Impact Studies and Facilities Studies under 
Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT or successor tariff provisions and has entered into, and 
posted Financial Security for the benefit of [Transmission Owner] [Affected Transmission 
Owner] in accordance with, the Large Load Security Agreement; 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein 
contained, the Parties agree as follows: 
ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS 
Wherever used in this Agreement, capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meanings 
assigned to them in the NYISO OATT or NYISO Market Services Tariff.

1.1 “Affiliate”: means, with respect to a person or entity, any other person or entity directly or 
indirectly controlling, controlled by, or under common control with such person or entity. 
“Control” means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct the management or 
policies of a person or entity; a voting interest of ten percent (10%) or more creates a rebuttable 
presumption of control.  
1.2 “Assessment Period”: A term of 15 years commencing on the Commencement Date. 
1.3 “Commercial Operation Date” (COD): The date on which Customer’s Large Load Facility 
has achieved energization and begins taking firm electric service pursuant to [the applicable 
Tariff] or [the Large Load Interconnection Agreement] at or above its designated minimum 
capacity commitment, as confirmed by notice delivered under the Large Load Interconnection 
Agreement. 
1.4 “Commencement Date”: The date upon which the Network Upgrade and/or TO Attachment 
Facility is placed in service, as reflected in the In-Service Date under the Large Load 
Interconnection Agreement [or other applicable agreement]. 
1.5 “Cure Date”: The last day of the applicable cure period under Section 7.2. 
1.6 “FERC”: The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission or its regulatory successor. 
1.7 “Large Load Facility”: A newly interconnecting or expanded end-use electric load, co-
located arrangement, or data center facility with an anticipated peak electrical demand equal to 
or exceeding 50 MW connecting at a voltage of 69 kV or higher. 
1.8 “Large Load Upgrade Charge”: The monthly charge billed to Customer under Article 4 to 
recover the revenue requirement for the Network Upgrades and the TO Attachment Facilities 
specified in Appendix B. 
1.9 “Net Present Value Residual Charge”: A lump sum payment equal to the net present value 
of the Customer’s obligations under the remaining term of this Agreement at the time of 
termination that would have been collected over the remaining term of the Assessment Period, 
plus any cost of removal if applicable, calculated using the inputs set forth in Appendix B for 
each Identified Upgrade set forth in Appendix A. The discount rate shall be equal to the interest 
rate pursuant to Section 35.19a of FERC’s regulations, or successor regulation, effective at the 
time of termination. To the extent the calculation of the Net Present Value Residual Charge 
requires estimates of the Customer’s remaining obligations, such estimates shall be determined 
by the Transmission Owner in its reasonable discretion. 
1.10 “Network Upgrades”: Additions, modifications, enhancements, or replacements (other 
than TO Attachment Facilities) to the [Transmission Owner’s] [Affected Transmission Owner’s] 
electric transmission system required at or beyond the Point of Interconnection to accommodate 
the Customer’s Large Load Facility reliably under NERC, NPCC, NYSRC, and Transmission 
Owner [Affected Transmission Owner] reliability criteria and identified through the Large Load 
Integration Procedures contained at Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT and set forth in 
[Appendix A, the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, and/or other applicable agreement]. 
1.11 “NY PSC”: The New York State Public Service Commission or its regulatory successor. 
1.12 “Reimbursable Costs”: Costs and expenses incurred by the Transmission Owner in 
planning, designing, engineering, permitting, procuring, project managing, constructing, testing, 
and commissioning the Network Upgrades and the TO Attachment Facilities, including any

incremental costs resulting from a Customer-requested suspension or a Customer delay under 
Section 3.3, consistent with Good Utility Practice. 
1.13 “Related Agreements”: Collectively, this Agreement, the Large Load Interconnection 
Agreement, the Large Load Security Agreement, and any other applicable agreement referenced 
in Section 3.4. 
1.14 “TO Attachment Facilities”: All facilities and equipment owned or controlled by 
Transmission Owner between the Point of Interconnection and the Point of Change of 
Ownership that are constructed or modified for the purpose of interconnecting the Large Load 
Facility, including line taps, breakers, disconnect switches, bus work, metering instrument 
transformers (if owned by Transmission Owner), System Protection Facilities at a Transmission 
Owner substation, and associated civil and structural works, as described in [Appendix A, the 
Large Load Interconnection Agreement, and/or other applicable agreement]. 
ARTICLE 2: JURISDICTIONAL HARMONIZATION 
2.1 Jurisdictional Harmonization. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as: 
(a) An assertion by the Parties of FERC jurisdiction over bundled retail transmission and 
distribution rates established by the NY PSC; or 
(b) A waiver by Transmission Owner or Customer of any rights, claims, or defenses 
under the Federal Power Act (“FPA”) or the New York Public Service Law concerning 
the jurisdictional boundary between wholesale and retail ratemaking. 
ARTICLE 3: SCOPE AND CONSTRUCTION OF NETWORK 
UPGRADES AND TO ATTACHMENT FACILITIES  
3.1 Identification of Upgrades. Transmission Owner shall engineer, design, procure, construct, 
install, commission, own, operate, and maintain the Network Upgrades and the TO Attachment 
Facilities detailed in Appendix A and the Large Load Interconnection Agreement, or other 
applicable agreement, which were, in turn, identified through the process contained within 
Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT. 
3.2 Milestones and Construction Schedule. Transmission Owner shall use commercially 
reasonable efforts to engineer, design, procure, and construct the Network Upgrades and the TO 
Attachment Facilities in accordance with the milestone schedule set forth in [the Large Load 
Interconnection Agreement and/or other applicable agreement]. All such milestones, other than 
the In-Service Date of the Network Upgrades and TO Attachment Facilities, are good faith 
estimates, and a change to any such milestone shall not require an amendment of this Agreement. 
Customer acknowledges that environmental review, Article VII and Article VIII permitting, and 
all property acquisitions are subject to New York State regulatory approvals and external 
contingencies. 
3.3 Suspension and Customer Delays. If Customer requests a suspension of construction or 
delays its commercial in-service milestone, Customer shall remain liable for all carrying costs, 
re-sequencing and re-mobilization costs, and cancellation fees incurred by Transmission Owner 
under Good Utility Practice during such suspension or delay. Transmission Owner reserves the

PDF page 7

right to notify Customer if such suspension or delay is expected to increase the total estimated 
cost of the Network Upgrades or the TO Attachment Facilities and to require additional Financial 
Security in accordance with the Large Load Security Agreement. 
3.4 Related Agreements. This Agreement is entered into in connection with, and shall be read 
together with, [the Large Load Interconnection Agreement between Transmission Owner and 
Customer dated [●] (the “Large Load Interconnection Agreement”)] [and/or other applicable 
agreement] and the Large Load Security Agreement between Transmission Owner and Customer 
dated [●] (the “Large Load Security Agreement”). Customer shall be the same legal entity as the 
customer under the Large Load Interconnection Agreement (or other applicable agreement) and 
the Large Load Security Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and 
any Related Agreement regarding the costs of, or charges for, the Network Upgrades or the TO 
Attachment Facilities, this Agreement shall control; and in the event of any conflict between this 
Agreement and any Related Agreement regarding Financial Security, the Large Load Security 
Agreement shall control. 
ARTICLE 4: RECOVERY OF NETWORK UPGRADE AND TO 
ATTACHMENT FACILITY COSTS 
4.1 Customer Responsibility for Allocated Network Upgrade and TO Attachment Facilities 
Revenue Requirement. Customer shall be responsible for and shall pay to the Transmission 
Owner the revenue requirement associated with the cost allocation to Customer for required 
Network Upgrades and TO Attachment Facilities identified in the Facilities Study specified at 
Section 44.12.1.8 of Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT, or successor provisions as set forth in 
Appendix A. The revenue requirement shall include, without limitation, appropriate depreciation 
or amortization, return, income and property taxes, operation and maintenance expenses, 
metering installation, administrative costs, and other costs as specified in Appendix B. The 
revenue requirement breakdown is specified herein at Appendix B. 
4.2 Large Load Upgrade Charge. The revenue requirement specified at Appendix B shall be 
recovered through a Large Load Upgrade Charge. This Agreement shall be specific to a single 
Large Load Facility, as determined by the Transmission Owner in its reasonable discretion. 
Transmission Owner shall calculate a separate charge for each Identified Upgrade (as defined in 
Appendix B) for which Customer is responsible, as set forth in Section 3 of Appendix B, and the 
monthly Large Load Upgrade Charge shall be the sum of all such Identified Upgrade-specific 
monthly charges. The monthly Large Load Upgrade Charge is specified at Section 4 of 
Appendix B for each month of years one (1) through the final year of the Assessment Period. 
The Transmission Owner shall follow the true-up procedures set forth in Section 6 of Appendix 
B. 
4.3 Early Termination. If, prior to the expiration of the Assessment Period, this Agreement is 
terminated, or Customer is in default under this Agreement or the Large Load Security 
Agreement and fails to timely cure the default, Customer shall be liable to the Transmission 
Owner for the Net Present Value Residual Charge, and the Transmission Owner shall be entitled 
to draw upon the Financial Security and apply it toward the Net Present Value Residual Charge. 
If any other outstanding charges or payments are due, Customer shall pay them within thirty (30) 
days of the termination date or the Cure Date, as applicable.

4.4 TO Attachment Facilities. Customer shall be responsible for and shall pay to the 
Transmission Owner (a) all actual costs incurred by Transmission Owner for studies, 
engineering, design, procurement, construction, oversight, testing, and commissioning of the TO 
Attachment Facilities, including applicable overheads, taxes, and line outage costs, to the extent 
not covered by any study cost reimbursement agreement or allocated otherwise under the NYISO 
OATT; and (b) Transmission Owner’s reasonable and prudent costs, including, without 
limitation, overheads, of operating, maintaining, repairing, and replacing the TO Attachment 
Facilities, except to the extent such costs are paid by a third party using such facilities. Unless 
otherwise agreed to by Transmission Owner and Customer in Section 7 of Appendix B, such 
costs shall be [included in the revenue requirement specified at Appendix B and recovered 
through the Large Load Upgrade Charge] [billed as a separately stated component of the Large 
Load Upgrade Charge] [, at Transmission Owner’s election, paid by Customer as a contribution 
in aid of construction in advance of or as incurred during construction], and Sections 4.3 and 6.2 
and Appendix C shall apply to the TO Attachment Facilities in the same manner as to the 
Network Upgrades. Customer’s responsibility for, and the recovery of, the costs of the TO 
Attachment Facilities shall be governed exclusively by this Agreement. 
ARTICLE 5: CUSTOMER PAYMENTS 
5.1 Commencement Date. Customer’s responsibility to make monthly payments shall begin as 
of the Commencement Date. 
5.2 Monthly Billing and Payments. The Large Load Upgrade Charge shall be billed monthly by 
Transmission Owner with Customer payment due within thirty (30) days. Monthly bills and 
payments shall be transmitted electronically in accordance with the information contained at 
Appendix D, or through paper copies, as the Parties may specify, using the addresses contained 
at Appendix D. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to effectuate efficient billing and 
payment. 
5.3 Late Payments. Customer shall pay all invoices on or before their due date, regardless of 
whether Customer disputes the same in good faith. Any balances that remain unpaid when due 
shall accrue interest at the interest rate specified at 18 C.F.R. § 35.19a or successor regulation. 
Transmission Owner may include unpaid balances, with any accrued interest, on the next 
monthly bill. Any disputes shall be addressed in accordance with Section 9.11. 
ARTICLE 6: FINANCIAL SECURITY, CREDITWORTHINESS, 
AND EXIT PROTECTION 
6.1 Obligation to Post Financial Security. To protect other retail and wholesale customers 
against stranded asset risk in the event Customer fails to achieve the Commercial Operation 
Date, downscales its capacity requirements, or abandons installation of the Large Load Facility, 
Customer shall post and maintain security in favor of [Transmission Owner] [Affected 
Transmission Owner] in the amount and manner specified in the separate Large Load Security 
Agreement (“Financial Security”). Subject to the determination of obligations under Appendix B 
of this Agreement, nothing in this Agreement limits in any way the obligations of Customer 
under the Large Load Security Agreement.

PDF page 9

6.2 Stranded Cost Recovery Upon Abandonment (Exit Protection). If Transmission Owner 
or Customer terminates this Agreement prematurely or otherwise defaults on its financial 
obligations: 
(a) To the extent that Transmission Owner determines within its reasonable discretion 
that the Network Upgrade is no longer needed, including based on written confirmation 
from NYISO that completion of the Network Upgrade is not required and that no other 
interconnection request, NYISO interconnection or transmission planning study 
(including any Reliability Needs Assessment or Short-Term Assessment of Reliability), 
or base case relies on completion of the Network Upgrade, Transmission Owner shall 
promptly cease incurring uncommitted costs and make reasonable efforts to mitigate 
losses, except in the case of actions necessary to preserve system reliability, avoid injury 
or loss of human life, or prevent environmental degradation. If NYISO informs 
Transmission Owner in writing that the Network Upgrade must be completed, 
Transmission Owner may continue to engineer, procure, and construct the Network 
Upgrade, and Customer shall remain responsible for all Reimbursable Costs of 
completing the Network Upgrade in accordance with Section 6.2(b). 
(b) Transmission Owner shall be entitled to draw upon the Financial Security as specified 
in the Large Load Security Agreement to recover all Reimbursable Costs, including 
without limitation engineering costs, committed equipment cancellation fees, 
environmental remediation costs, the costs to restore the bulk electric system to safe and 
reliable condition, including necessary expediting and acceleration fees, any non-
salvageable construction expenditures, the costs of removal, relocation, retirement, or 
other disposition of any facilities already installed, and, where the Network Upgrade 
remains needed under Section 6.2(a), the costs of completing the Network Upgrade. 
Customer shall remain liable for any such costs remaining unpaid after application of the 
Financial Security. 
(c) Any excess security remaining after full recovery and true-up shall be promptly 
refunded to Customer. 
6.3 Reduction in Maximum Load Facility Peak Demand. Any reduction of the Maximum 
Load Facility Peak Demand under Section 3.6 of the Large Load Interconnection Agreement 
shall not reduce the Large Load Upgrade Charge, the Net Present Value Residual Charge, or the 
Financial Security, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties. 
ARTICLE 7: BREACH, DEFAULT, AND REMEDIES 
7.1 Events of Default. An Event of Default shall occur if Customer: 
(a) Fails to pay any amount when due; 
(b) Fails to provide, maintain, renew, replace, or increase the Financial Security as 
required under Article 6 and the Large Load Security Agreement; 
(c) Becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or undergoes dissolution; 
(d) Abandons or materially repudiates its development or interconnection obligations; or

PDF page 10

(e) Is in default under the Large Load Interconnection Agreement (or other applicable 
agreement) or the Large Load Security Agreement and any applicable cure period 
thereunder has expired.  
7.2 Notice and Opportunity to Cure. Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default, the non-
defaulting Party shall provide written notice. The defaulting Party shall have (i) five (5) Business 
Days after receipt of notice to cure any failure to pay any amount when due or to provide, 
maintain, renew, replace, or increase Financial Security, and (ii) thirty (30) days after receipt of 
notice to cure any other default that is capable of cure; provided that a cross-default under 
Section 7.1(e) shall arise only after expiration of the cure period in the applicable Related 
Agreement, and no additional cure period shall apply under this Agreement. 
7.3 Remedies. If an Event of Default is not remedied by the Cure Date, the non-defaulting Party 
may: 
(a) Terminate this Agreement upon written notice; 
(b) Draw upon the Financial Security to satisfy outstanding liabilities; 
(c) Suspend engineering, procurement, and construction of the Network Upgrades and the 
TO Attachment Facilities; and 
(d) Pursue any legal or equitable remedies available under New York State law or the 
FPA. 
ARTICLE 8: TERM AND TERMINATION 
8.1 Effective Date. This Agreement shall become effective upon execution by all Parties within 
the timeframe set forth in Attachment LL of the NYISO OATT[, subject to any filing with FERC 
that may be required under Section 205 of the FPA]. 
8.2 Term. This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect until all Large Load Upgrade 
Charge obligations and other payment obligations under Articles 4 and 5, including any Net 
Present Value Residual Charge, have been fully satisfied. 
8.3 Regulatory Modification. Without creating any additional regulatory filing obligations, if 
FERC or the NY PSC issues an order requiring a material modification, including termination or 
rejection, of this Agreement, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for sixty (60) days to 
conform the Agreement. If agreement cannot be reached, Transmission Owner may terminate 
this Agreement and draw upon the Financial Security for all incurred, unrecoverable 
Reimbursable Costs.  
8.4 Cross-Termination. Termination of the Large Load Interconnection Agreement (or other 
applicable agreement) for any reason other than Transmission Owner’s Default (including 
Customer’s termination for convenience or non-renewal) shall constitute early termination of this 
Agreement for purposes of Section 4.3, and the Net Present Value Residual Charge shall become 
due.

Customer shall release Customer or any existing Financial Security until the assignee’s

Majeure shall not excuse any obligation to make payments or to post and maintain Financial 
Security. 
9.10

9.11(b)(ii). Whether a termination is proper shall not be subject to arbitration unless the

3. Calculation of the Estimated Accelerated Annual Revenue Requirement 
For each Billing Year during the Assessment Period, Transmission Owner shall calculate the 
Estimated Accelerated Annual Revenue Requirement, which shall constitute the annual Large 
Load Upgrade Charge, by performing the following steps in sequence: 
(a) Step One – Estimated Network Upgrade Book Value. Transmission Owner shall 
determine the Estimated Network Upgrade Book Value for each Identified Upgrade, which 
shall equal the total estimated capital cost of such Identified Upgrade, inclusive of direct 
costs, applicable indirect costs (including overheads and allowance for funds used during 
construction (“AFUDC”)), as set forth in Section 1 of this Appendix B and as recorded in 
accordance with FERC’s Uniform System of Accounts, 18 C.F.R. Part 101. 
(b) Step Two – Annual On-going Charge Factor and Large Load Upgrade-Specific 
Capital Revenue Requirement. Transmission Owner shall determine the Annual On-
going Charge Factor in effect for the Billing Year in accordance with Sections 5 and 6 of 
this Appendix B. Transmission Owner shall also determine the LLCRR for the applicable 
Billing Year using the applicable approved inputs and Identified Upgrade-specific 
investment balances. 
(c) Step Three – Estimated Annual Revenue Requirement. Transmission Owner shall 
add the LLCRR to the product of the Large Load Upgrade Gross Plant in Service and the 
Annual On-going Charge Factor, and round the sum to the nearest [rounding increment to 
be specified by Transmission Owner], as follows: 
EARR = LLCRR + (LLGP × OCF) 
The Estimated Annual Revenue Requirement represents the estimated annual cost of 
owning, operating, and maintaining the Identified Upgrade. 
(d) Step Four – Average Service Life and Contract Term. Transmission Owner shall 
determine the Average Service Life of the Identified Upgrade and the Contract Term, each

PDF page 20

expressed in years, in accordance with the definitions set forth in Section 2 of this Appendix 
B. 
(e) Step Five – Estimated Accelerated Annual Revenue Requirement. Transmission 
Owner shall multiply the Estimated Annual Revenue Requirement by the Average Service 
Life and divide the product by the Contract Term, as follows: 
EAARR = (EARR × ASL) ÷ CT 
The resulting Estimated Accelerated Annual Revenue Requirement provides for the 
recovery within the Contract Term of the aggregate Estimated Annual Revenue 
Requirements that would otherwise be recovered over the Average Service Life of the 
Identified Upgrade. 
(f) Annual and Monthly Large Load Upgrade Charge. Transmission Owner shall 
perform the calculation set forth in this Section 3 separately for each Identified Upgrade. 
The annual Large Load Upgrade Charge for each Billing Year shall equal the sum of the 
Estimated Accelerated Annual Revenue Requirements for all Identified Upgrades for that 
Billing Year. The monthly Large Load Upgrade Charge shall equal one-twelfth of the 
annual Large Load Upgrade Charge, as set forth in Section 4 of this Appendix B.

6. Annual Update and True-Up 
(a) Annual Update. No later than August 1 of each calendar year during the Contract Term 
and the year immediately following the Contract Term, Transmission Owner shall 
recalculate the Large Load Upgrade-Specific Capital Revenue Requirement and Annual 
On-going Charge Factor using the Rate Data Sources then most recently available, and 
shall recalculate the Estimated Annual Revenue Requirement, the Estimated Accelerated 
Annual Revenue Requirement, the Annual Deferred Revenue, and the monthly Large Load 
Upgrade Charge for the succeeding Billing Year in accordance with Sections 3 and 5 of 
this Appendix B. Each annual update shall reflect, without limitation, changes during the 
Contract Term in Transmission Owner’s approved or accepted return on common equity 
and depreciation rates.  
(b) Annual True-Up. Because the Annual On-going Charge Factor applied in each Billing 
Year is derived from data for the most recently completed reporting year, the Large Load 
Upgrade Charge billed for each Billing Year shall be an estimate. In the Billing Year 
following each Billing Year, Transmission Owner shall determine the difference between 
(i) the Large Load Upgrade Charge billed for the prior Billing Year and (ii) the Large Load 
Upgrade Charge that would have been billed for that Billing Year using the Annual On-
going Charge Factor, the Large Load Upgrade-Specific Capital Revenue Requirement, and 
Large Load Upgrade Gross Plant in Service derived from actual data for the corresponding 
period. Any resulting over-recovery or under-recovery, together with interest calculated in 
accordance with 18 C.F.R. § 35.19a, shall be refunded to or surcharged to Customer, as 
applicable, in equal monthly installments over the succeeding Billing Year, in accordance 
with the accounting protocols set forth in Appendix C. 
(c) Replacement of Estimated Costs. Upon completion of the Final Cost Reconciliation 
Protocol set forth in Section 1 of Appendix C, the actual Reimbursable Costs of each 
Identified Upgrade shall replace the Estimated Network Upgrade Book Value for all 
purposes of this Appendix B. Transmission Owner shall recalculate the Large Load 
Upgrade Charge prospectively and shall true up all amounts billed from the 
Commencement Date, with interest calculated in accordance with 18 C.F.R. § 35.19a. 
(d) Informational Statement. Concurrently with each annual update, Transmission 
Owner shall provide Customer with a written statement setting forth the recalculated 
Annual On-going Charge Factor, Large Load Upgrade-Specific Capital Revenue 
Requirement, and Large Load Upgrade Gross Plant in Service, and each input used to 
determine those amounts, the specific Rate Data Source and account or line reference from

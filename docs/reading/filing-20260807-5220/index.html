EL26-68-000 · 2026-08-07 · Corporate Energy Buyers Association

Answer of Corporate Energy Buyers Association in Support of the Amended Motion for Abeyance filed on 08/03/2026 by Southwest Power Pool, Inc. et al

The Corporate Energy Buyers Association answers in support of the SPP Joint Movants' amended motion for a 95-day abeyance in EL26-68 and asks FERC to grant it on an expedited basis.

Asks FERC to: Grant the Joint Movants' amended motion for a 95-day abeyance in an expedited manner.

Answers the order. A buyer-side answer supporting the E-9 SPP abeyance motion, which moved the 60-day show cause response to November 20, 2026.

E-9 SPP: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. CEBA says abeyance lets SPP and stakeholders work collaboratively instead of moving the debate to the FERC docket.
    as opposed to a rushed proposal that would unnecessarily shift stakeholder debate to the Commission docket. PDF p. 1
  2. CEBA says the abeyance accommodates the Regional State Committee and SPP Board meetings.
    reasonable accommodations necessary to convene the Regional State Committee and the SPP Board of Directors PDF p. 1
  3. CEBA says the 95 days would not materially slow reform and would improve commercial viability and legal durability.
    extension would not materially impede the Commission's objectives for advancing reforms quickly yet offers significant benefits PDF p. 2

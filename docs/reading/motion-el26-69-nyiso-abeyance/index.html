EL26-69-000 · 2026-08-03 · New York Independent System Operator, Inc.

NYISO requests more time to answer the June 18 show cause order

NYISO moves for the maximum 90-day abeyance of the EL26-69 show cause response, to November 16, 2026, so it can build a section 205 filing through its stakeholder governance process and coordinate with the New York transmission owners.

Asks FERC to: Issue an order by August 13, 2026 holding the proceeding in abeyance for 90 days until November 16, 2026, with a five-day answer period.

Answers the order. Invokes the E-12 order's 90-day abeyance option and describes the section 205 package NYISO plans on study process, flexible service, co-location and proximate generation.

E-12 NYISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-12 NYISO: Timely study window (P 44, p. 31) E-12 NYISO: Proximate-generation service (P 121, p. 72)

Key points

  1. NYISO requests the full 90 days and action by August 13.
    hold this proceeding in abeyance for the maximum period permitted by the Show Cause Order, i.e., for 90 days, until November 16, 2026. PDF p. 1
  2. NYISO plans a new large load study process replacing its existing load interconnection rules, with expedited joint studies.
    the NYISO plans to create a new large load study process that would replace and improve upon its existing load interconnection rules. PDF p. 7
  3. NYISO would offer firm and non-firm variants of its financial reservation service, with non-firm withdrawal service for flexible large loads.
    make large loads eligible to take non-firm withdrawal service to the extent their ability to be flexible mitigates their impact on the system. PDF p. 7
  4. For co-location, NYISO intends to sunset its BTM:NG program and treat load and generation as separate market entities.
    the NYISO intends to propose sunsetting its BTM:NG program and replacing it with a new co-located resource participation model PDF p. 7
  5. NYISO ties the timing to New York's shared governance and the NYPSC's parallel large-load proceeding.
    a 90-day abeyance is reasonable in the NYISO's case because it will facilitate the NYISO's ability to obtain approval from its stakeholder Management Committee before making a section 205 filing. PDF p. 9
  6. Schedule: committee votes October 14 and 15, Management Committee October 28, filing November 16, 2026.
    Provided that Management Committee and Board approvals are secured, the NYISO will submit a section 205 proposal on November 16, 2026 PDF p. 11

ER26-3552-000 · 2026-10-09 · OFFICE OF ENERGY MARKET REGULATION

FERC requests information about MISO’s zero-injection agreement

FERC's Office of Energy Market Regulation issues a deficiency letter on October 9, 2026 asking MISO for more information on its ZGIA filing, including resource adequacy treatment, the Transient Tolerance Window and contingency studies, with a 45-day response period.

Answers the order. Staff questions the resource adequacy and reliability design of MISO's ZGIA, the first piece of MISO's E-8 response on co-located load, in the separate ER26-3552 proceeding.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. Staff finds MISO's August 18 ZGIA submittal deficient and requests more information.
    Please be advised that your submittal is deficient and that additional information is necessary to process the filing. Source text
  2. Staff asks why a Seasonal Accredited Capacity set by participation type is just and reasonable for a resource that cannot inject beyond its point of interconnection.
    Please explain why it is just and reasonable to assign a SAC for a ZG Resource, which cannot inject beyond its point of interconnection, using the currently effective method based on participation type Source text
  3. Staff questions treating a 100 MW ZG Interconnection Service resource as equally deliverable as a 100 MW Network Resource Interconnection Service resource.
    please explain why it is just and reasonable to consider these two resources as equally deliverable to load. Source text
  4. Staff asks how the Transient Tolerance Window will be set per resource, including when it would be shorter than 20 seconds.
    Please explain how MISO and the applicable transmission owner will establish the Transient Tolerance Window applicable to each ZG Resource, including the circumstances in which it would require a window shorter than 20 seconds. Source text
  5. Staff asks how the ZG Impact Assessment will evaluate injections during the window and the contingencies it will study.
    Please explain how MISO will evaluate, in the ZG Impact Assessment, the amount, in MW, of injections during the Transient Tolerance Window Source text
  6. MISO has 45 days to respond, and the response amends the filing and sets a new filing date.
    A response to this letter must be filed with the Secretary of the Commission within 45 days of the date of this letter Source text

ER26-3650-000 · 2026-09-18 · Mississippi Public Service CommissionArkansas Public Service Commission

Protest of Mississippi Public Service Commission et al. re Midcontinent Independent System Operator, Inc.'s 08/28/2026 filing

The Mississippi and Arkansas public service commissions protest MISO's large-load reliability filing as too general to design equipment against, and object to placing the requirements in Business Practice Manuals.

Asks FERC to: Condition acceptance of MISO's filing on MISO filing the specific interconnection requirements in the Tariff, not in the Business Practice Manuals.

Answers the order. Protest of MISO's first large-load filing after the E-8 order, arguing that operational requirements for large loads belong in the FERC-jurisdictional Tariff.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The Southern Regulators say the filing gives only a general overview that developers cannot build to.
    It lacks the specific detail needed to actually interconnect to the grid. PDF p. 1
  2. They warn that equipment may need costly replacement once MISO publishes the actual requirements.
    only to then replace that equipment at great expense after MISO later publishes the actual requirements. PDF p. 1
  3. They object to putting the requirements in BPMs, which MISO can change with little market participant input.
    when it finally develops the requirements, MISO unreasonably proposes to place them in Business Practice Manuals (BPMs). PDF p. 1
  4. They support Entergy's limited protest that the rule of reason puts such requirements in the Tariff.
    the Rule of Reason mandates that these requirements be placed in the Tariff where they are subject to FERC’s oversight to ensure they are just and reasonable. PDF p. 2

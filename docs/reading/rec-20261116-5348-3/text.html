COST RECOVERY AGREEMENT

This Cost Recovery Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into as of __________ (the “Execution Date”) between  Trans Bay Cable LLC (“Trans Bay” or “Participating Transmission Owner”) and ________________, a [corporation/limited liability company/other] organized and existing under the laws of the State/Commonwealth of _____________________ (the “Eligible Customer”).  The Eligible Customer is requesting Interconnection service to serve a Large Load, as those terms are defined in the Participating Transmission Owner’s tariff. The Participating Transmission Owner and Eligible Customer may be referred to herein individually as a “Party” or collectively as the “Parties.”

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Participating Transmission Owner is a Non-Load-Serving Entity Participating Transmission Owner within the California Independent System Operator, Inc. (“CAISO”) Balancing Authority Area that does not provide retail electric service;

WHEREAS, Participating Transmission Owner owns the [Facility Name], which is under CAISO’s Operational Control and within the CAISO Controlled Grid;

WHEREAS, Participating Transmission Owner’s Transmission Owner Tariff (the “Tariff”) controls the interconnection of load to [Facility Name], subject to compliance with the CAISO tariff;

WHEREAS, Eligible Customer is requesting interconnection to Participating Transmission Owner’s [Facility Name] for the purpose of serving [describe Large Load requesting service, physical location, and MW];

WHEREAS, the Parties have entered into this Agreement to establish the teams and conditions for the Interconnection in order to enable the Eligible Customer to serve the Large Load  and for the Participating Transmission Owner to fully recover from the Eligible Customer the costs associated with serving the Large Load.

NOW, THEREFORE, in exchange for good and valuable consideration of the premises and mutual covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

1.1
Definitions

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement that are not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning provided in the Participating Transmission Owner Tariff, as may be amended from time-

to-time.  In the event of a difference in a defined term between this Agreement and the definition in Module A of the Tariff, the defined term in this Agreement shall control.

1.2
Annual Required Revenue Commitment

Annual Required Revenue Commitment means the Eligible Customer’s Total Required Revenue Commitment divided by twenty (20) years.

1.3
Cost Recovery Payment

Cost Recovery Payment means the amount the Eligible Customer must pay in addition to the Large Load Annual Received Revenue to meet its Annual Required Revenue Commitment.

1.4
Large Load Annual Received Revenue

Large Load Annual Received Revenue means revenue that is received by the Participating Transmission Owner from CAISO through the Access Charges during a specific calendar year and that is directly attributable to the Eligible Customer’s Large Load.

1.5
Large Load Network Upgrade

Large Load Network Upgrade shall mean any Network Upgrade attributable to an Eligible Customer interconnecting on behalf of a Large Load. Large Load Network Upgrades shall be specified in the Eligible Customer’s applicable Interconnection Agreement.

1.6
Monthly Cost Recovery Payment

Monthly Cost Recovery Payment means the Cost Recovery Payment divided by twelve (12) months.

1.7
Total Required Revenue Commitment

Total Required Revenue Commitment means the incremental revenue requirement for any Large Load Network Upgrade(s) to serve the Eligible Customer’s Large Load, calculated in accordance with Section 3.2.

ARTICLE 3: ELIGIBLE CUSTOMER PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS

3.1
Performance Obligations

The Eligible Customer shall be responsible for the Total Required Revenue Commitment as stated in Exhibit A to this Agreement. The Eligible Customer shall be responsible for the timely payment of all applicable invoices assessed herein. This Agreement does not relieve Eligible Customer from paying any charges or amounts assessed by the CAISO or any other governmental or regulatory authority to the Eligible Customer.

3.2
Total Required Revenue Commitment

3.2.1 Calculation of Total Required Revenue Commitment

The Total Required Revenue Commitment shall be calculated consistent with FERC cost of service ratemaking principles and set forth in Exhibit A of this Agreement, and as further set forth in Section 3.2.2, and shall incorporate a full return on the capital cost, depreciation, operations and maintenance, and taxes of the Large Load Network Upgrade(s).

3.2.2 Initial and Adjusted Total Required Revenue Commitment

The initial Total Required Revenue Commitment will be based on the estimated costs of the Large Load Network Upgrade(s) and shall be stated in Exhibit A to this Agreement. The Total Required Revenue Commitment shall be subject to adjustment by the Participating Transmission Owner to reflect the actual costs of the Large Load Network Upgrade(s).  Adjustments to the Total Required Revenue Commitment to reflect actual costs shall be initially calculated within one (1) year of the in-service date of the Large Load Network Upgrades and each year thereafter, and shall be reflected

in Exhibit A of this Agreement with a true-up adjustment as a credit or charge and including interest. Interest associated with any true-up adjustment will be determined in accordance with 18 C.F.R § 35.19a.

3.3
Required Cost Recovery Payments

Each calendar year following the Large Load Network Upgrade’s in-service date and continuing throughout the term of this Agreement, the Participating Transmission Owner shall:

(a) determine whether the Large Load Annual Received Revenue for that calendar year meets the Annual Required Revenue Commitment; and, if not,

(b) calculate the Cost Recovery Payment, payable by the Eligible Customer, equal to the difference between (i) the Annual Required Revenue Commitment for the applicable calendar year and (ii) the Large Load Annual Received Revenue for that calendar year, plus any applicable interest calculated in accordance with 18 C.F.R § 35.19a.

3.4
Invoice for Monthly Cost Recovery Payments

Within thirty (30) days of the end of each month of any calendar year in which a Cost Recovery Payment is owed, the Participating Transmission Owner shall invoice the Eligible Customer for the Monthly Cost Recovery Payment as determined under Article 8. The Eligible Customer shall timely pay such invoice in accordance with Article 8.

3.5
Treatment of Cost Recovery Payments

For each calendar year during the Term of this Agreement, the Participating Transmission Owner shall treat any Cost Recovery Payments required to be paid by the Eligible Customer as a credit in the next calculation of the Participating Transmission Owner’s Transmission Revenue Requirement in  Appendix I of its Tariff by deducting the Cost Recovery Payments from the Transmission Revenue Requirement, and shall include a workpaper  detailing such crediting and rate treatment in the filing at FERC implementing such calculation.

3.6
Payment in Event of Termination, Cancellation, or Withdrawal

Upon termination, disconnection, suspension, or abandonment of the Large Load project, or should the Eligible Customer terminate, cancel, or otherwise withdraw or terminate this Agreement, the Interconnection Agreement, or its Interconnection service for any reason, the Eligible Customer shall remain obligated to pay to the Participating Transmission Owner all costs incurred or committed by the Participating Transmission Owner associated with serving the Large Load, including Network Upgrades, prior to the effective date of any such termination or change. In the event the Eligible Customer fails to make such payment, this Agreement shall not be Terminated until such payment is made and payments shall continue to be due under Article 3. The Participating Transmission Owner may draw upon the Required Security to satisfy the Eligible

ARTICLE 4: Security Requirements

4.1
Required Security

The Eligible Customer shall provide and maintain financial security in accordance with this Article 4 (the “Required Security”). The Required Security shall be in an amount equal to the Total Required Revenue Commitment and shall take one of the following forms as reasonably acceptable to the Participating Transmission Owner:

(a) cash;
(b) an irrevocable letter of credit issued by a commercial bank that has (i) an office, branch, or place of business in the United States where claims can be presented, (ii) assets of at least $10 billion, and (iii) a Credit Rating of either “A3” or higher by Moody’s, or (b) “A-” or higher by Standard & Poor’s (“S&P”). If a commercial bank is rated by each of Moody’s and S&P, the lower rating will be the applicable standard;

(c) an irrevocable guaranty, provided that the guarantor maintain a senior unsecured credit rating of BBB- equivalent by Moody’s, S&P, or Fitch. If the guarantor is rated by one or more rating agencies, the lowest rating will be the applicable standard; or

(d) such other form of security as the Eligible Customer may propose and the Participating Transmission Owner, in its sole discretion, determines to be acceptable.

When the amount of Total Required Revenue Commitment is adjusted per Section 3.2.2, the amount of Required Security shall be adjusted accordingly. The amount of Required Security may be further adjusted in accordance with Sections 4.3 and 4.4.

4.2
Timing to Provide Required Security

The Eligible Customer shall provide the Required Security within thirty (30) Calendar Days of the later of (i) the execution of this Agreement by all parties or (ii) acceptance of this Agreement by FERC. Failure to provide the Required Security within such timeframe will result in the Large Load Interconnection Application being deemed withdrawn.

4.3
Annual Reduction of Required Security

The Required Security shall remain with the Participating Transmission Owner for the Term of this Agreement. The Participating Transmission Owner will reduce the Required Security following each Annual Update during the Term of this Agreement to reflect the completed payments made by the Eligible Customer pursuant to Article 3 of this Agreement. Any such reduced Required Security must be provided in a revised form of irrevocable security otherwise meeting the requirements of Section 4.1.

ARTICLE 11: BREACH AND DEFAULT

11.1
General

A Party shall be considered in default of this Agreement (“Default”) if it fails to cure a Breach in accordance with the terms of this Section 11.1.  A breach (“Breach”) shall mean the failure of a Party to perform or observe any material term or condition of this Agreement; provided that no Breach shall exist where such failure to discharge an obligation (other than the payment of money) is the result of Force Majeure as defined in this Agreement or the result of an act or omission by the other Party. Upon a Breach, the non-breaching Party shall give written notice of such Breach to the breaching Party.  The breaching Party shall have thirty (30) Calendar Days from receipt of the Breach notice within which to cure such Breach; provided however, if such Breach is not capable of cure within thirty (30) Calendar Days, the breaching Party shall commence such cure within thirty (30) Calendar Days after notice and continuously and diligently complete such cure within ninety (90) Calendar Days from receipt of the Breach notice; and, if cured within such time, the Breach specified in such notice shall cease to exist.

11.2
Consequence of Default

In the event of Default, Eligible Customer shall promptly pay any remaining Total Required Revenue Commitment owed under this Agreement.  In the event that Eligible Customer does not promptly such amount, the Participating Transmission Owner may draw on the Required Security. This payment or draw on the Required Security does not limit any and all rights and remedies available to the non-defaulting Parties allowed by law with respect to such default or collecting all amounts owed under this Agreement. The Defaulting Party shall indemnify non-Defaulting Parties for reasonable costs, attorney fees and/or expenses incurred with respect to a default or collecting all amounts owed, including, as applicable, any tax gross-up obligations under this Agreement.

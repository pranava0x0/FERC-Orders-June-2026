Dear Governor Shapiro: 
 
  
Thank you for your October 2, 2024, letter relaying your views on large loads 
co-located with generation facilities, and the potential impacts on the Commonwealth 
of Pennsylvania, referencing PJM Interconnection, L.L.C., in Docket No. ER24-2172-
000 and PECO Energy Co., in Docket No. ER24-2893-000.     
 
In your letter, you highlight the importance of co-located load projects in 
development in Pennsylvania on economic and community development 
opportunities.  You further request the Commission avoid a patchwork of different 
rules for different utilities, as well as a significant departure from traditional rules that 
allocate costs based on the extent to which an off-taker is served by the grid.   
Again, I thank you for detailing your views on this critical topic. Because the 
proceedings referenced in your letter are contested, and the issues you raise are 
relevant to other contested pending proceedings as well, I cannot discuss the merits of 
these issues at this time.  I agree that co-located load arrangements will play an 
important role in the nation’s electric future.  As I emphasized in my statement issued 
February 21, 2025 after the Commission unanimously voted to launch a review of 
issues associated with the co-location of large loads such as AI-enabled data centers at 
generating facilities in PJM, including whether the PJM tariff needs to establish rules 
to create clarity while ensuring grid reliability and fair costs to customers, we want to 
act quickly on this issue.  While I cannot announce a specific date for final action, it is 
our intent to conduct and complete this important proceeding expeditiously, so we 
can provide clarity and certainty to all, from consumers to investors to load-serving 
utilities and independent generators.   
You have my commitment that we will continue to actively work on these 
issues.  I take seriously our obligation to ensure any action the Commission takes is in 
the public interest.  I assure you that Commission decisions are based on a thorough 
and careful review of the record and the parties’ pleadings.

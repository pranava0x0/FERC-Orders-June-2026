PJM incorrectly relies on assertions about jurisdiction and cooperative federalism to justify the obvious flaws in the IRAS Filing. PJM asserts (at 85–93) that jurisdictional rules are the reason that PJM’s IRAS Filing fails to create rules that actually impose curtailability on data center loads that interconnect with no new capacity.

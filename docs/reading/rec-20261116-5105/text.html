On June 18, 2026 the Commission issued its Order Instituting 
Proceeding Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act (“Show Cause 
Order”),1 requiring each Participating Transmission Owner (“PTO”) within 
the California Independent System Operator’s (“CAISO”) system to address 
issues associated with serving large load and co-location arrangements.  In 
this filing, pursuant to Section 205 of the Federal Power Act, Citizens 
Sunrise Transmission LLC (“Citizens Sunrise”) files proposed revisions to its 
Transmission Owner Tariff (“TO Tariff”) to address the issues raised in the 
Show Cause Order.  Citizens Sunrise respectfully requests that the 
Commission accept these TO Tariff revisions to become effective January 15, 
2027, which is sixty (60) days from the date of this filing. 
 
 
I. 
BACKGROUND ON CITIZENS SUNRISE 
 
Citizens Sunrise is a wholly owned subsidiary of Citizens Energy 
Corporation, a non-profit organization dedicated to making life’s basic needs

Imperial Valley an average of $350 a year on their electric bills.  Citizens 
Sunrise estimates its involvement in the Sunrise Project will generate 
approximately $45 million in charitable assistance over the term of the lease.

of the CAISO PTOs, including Citizens Sunrise, “lack provisions that 
address, with sufficient clarity and consistency” how each PTO “will timely 
study…the provision of transmission service to Eligible Customers on behalf 
of large loads.”4  The Commission directed each PTO to either justify why its

As discussed in more detail below, Citizens Sunrise has a limited, 
leasehold interest in only a portion of the overall Sunrise Project

responsibility for operating and maintaining the line, remains with SDG&E.  
For these reasons, Citizens Sunrise proposes revisions to its TO Tariff to 
make clear that any study, planning, interconnection or expansion requests 
related to Citizens Sunrise’s interest in the Sunrise Project will be governed 
by the relevant provisions of the SDG&E TO Tariff. 
 
A. 
Relevant Existing TO Tariff Provisions 
 
Citizens Sunrise’s current TO Tariff recognizes the limited nature of 
its interest in the Sunrise Project.  Section 16.12 of Citizens Sunrise’ current 
TO Tariff states that for so long as the Transfer Capability Lease between 
Citizens Sunrise and SDG&E is in effect, Sections 8 (“Obligation to 
Interconnect or Construct Transmission Expansions and Facility Upgrades”), 
9 (“Expansion Process”), and 10 (“Interconnection Process”) of the Citizens 
Sunrise Tariff will be “superseded by the corresponding sections of the TO 
Tariff of San Diego Gas & Electric.”5  Article III of the Transfer Capability 
Lease, which is included at Appendix II to the Sunrise TO Tariff, outlines the 
division of responsibility between SDG&E and Citizens Sunrise, with 
SDG&E retaining all responsibility for operation and maintenance6 and to 
act as the interconnection agent for the Sunrise Project.7   
 
These existing provisions illustrate that any responsibility for studying 
or serving large load customers or addressing the needs of co-located 
arrangements is beyond the scope of Citizens Sunrise’s limited authority as a 
non-load serving PTO with no end-use customers and a limited interest in a 
portion of a single transmission line segment within SDG&E’s system.

B. 
Proposed TO Tariff Provision Revisions 
 
In response to the Show Cause Order, Citizens Sunrise is proposing a 
series of revisions to its TO Tariff to ensure it is clear that when it comes to 
all interconnection matters, including interconnection and service of large 
load customers or co-located arrangements, the appropriate TO Tariff 
provisions are those of SDG&E.  These revisions include the following: 
 
• Revisions to the definitions of Expedited Interconnection Agreement, 
Facilities Study Agreement, Facility or Facilities Study, 
Interconnection, Interconnection Agreement, Interconnection 
Application, Reliability Upgrade, Requests for Expedited 
Interconnection Procedures, System Impact Study, and System Impact 
Study Agreement, each to clarify that SDG&E’s TO Tariff provisions 
will govern. 
 
• Added new language to Section 8, Obligations to Interconnect or 
Construct Transmission and Expansions and Facility Upgrades, to 
confirm that all conduct that falls under Section 8 will be governed by 
SDG&E’s TO Tariff. 
 
• Added new language to Section 9, Expansion Process, to confirm that 
all conduct that falls under Section 9 will be governed by SDG&E’s TO 
Tariff. 
 
• Added new language to Section 10, Interconnection Process, to confirm 
that all conduct that falls under Section 10 will be governed by 
SDG&E’s TO Tariff. 
 
• Citizens Sunrise additionally makes a series of administrative 
revisions to address typos, update addresses, and similar type changes 
with this filing. 
 
Each of these revisions is shown in redline in Attachment B to this filing, 
which is a redline of the Citizens Sunrise TO Tariff.  A clean version of the 
revised TO Tariff is included as Attachment A. 
 
Citizens Sunrise respectfully submits that these revisions address the 
concerns raised in the Show Cause Order within the bounds of Citizens 
Sunrise’s limited interest in, and control over, the Sunrise Project.  Put 
simply, Citizens Sunrise is bound by the terms of its Transfer Capability

I. BACKGROUND
A. The PJM Co-Located Load Proceeding
On December 18, 2025, the Commission issued an order in Docket Nos. EL25-49-000, et al., pursuant to section 206 of the FPA, finding the PJM Tariff unjust and unreasonable because it does not contain provisions addressing with sufficient clarity or consistency the rates, terms, and conditions of service that apply to generators serving Co-Located Load and Eligible Customers taking transmission service on behalf of Co-Located Load.7

The Commission directed PJM, inter alia, to submit revisions to its Tariff to require that the Eligible Customer taking transmission service on behalf of the Co-Located Load takes one of three transmission services:  (1) Network Integration Transmission Service (“NITS”), including a new interim, non-firm transmission service available until all Network Upgrades necessary to provide the requested NITS are complete (“Interim NITS”), (2) a new Firm Contract Demand (“Firm Contract Demand”) transmission service, or (3) a new Non-Firm Contract Demand (“Non- Firm Contract Demand”) transmission service.8  The Commission also established a paper hearing to determine the just and reasonable rates, terms, and conditions for these new transmission services.9  On February 23, 2026, in Docket Nos. EL25-49-000, et al., PJM and the PJM Transmission Owners each submitted an initial brief to address the Commission’s paper hearing questions with respect to the new transmission services.  On March 25, 2026, and on April 24, 2026, various parties submitted response briefs and reply briefs, respectively. 7

B. The PJM Large Load Show Cause Proceeding
On June 18, 2026, the Commission also issued a show cause order under section 206 of the FPA to PJM, together with the PJM Transmission Owners, directing PJM to justify or reform the Tariff provisions governing how large loads connect to and receive service from the transmission system.11  The PJM Show Cause Order preliminarily found that PJM’s Tariff appeared to be unjust, unreasonable, or unduly discriminatory or preferential because of a lack of sufficiently clear and consistent provisions to address service for large loads and related configurations.12  The Commission therefore directed PJM and the PJM Transmission Owners, within 60 days, to show cause why the Tariff remains just and reasonable without provisions addressing:  (1) the application process, study procedures, and ongoing operational requirements for Eligible Customers seeking transmission service on behalf of large loads; (2) additional transparency concerning Network Upgrade costs for such service, together with the absence of a pro forma cost recovery agreement and a mechanism to ensure related payments are properly credited to transmission owners’ revenue requirements; (3) transmission service options tailored to flexible large loads willing and able to limit their use of the transmission system under certain conditions; 10

The issues the PJM Transmission Owners are addressing in response to the PJM Show Cause Order substantially overlap with those that the Commission directed the PJM Transmission Owners to address in the June 18 Order.  Many of the issues addressed in the PJM Show Cause Order incorporate or otherwise build off of the requirements imposed by the Commission in the PJM Co-Located Load proceeding.  For instance, the PJM Show Cause Order preliminarily found that new transmission services should be available to “flexible large loads” willing and able to limit their withdrawals, specifically the three new services it required in the PJM Co-Located Load proceeding:  (1) Interim NITS, (2) Firm Contract Demand, and (3) Non-Firm Contract Demand.18 In effect, the PJM Show Cause Order contemplates expanding the scope of these new transmission services to a broader set of customers.  Therefore, the PJM Transmission Owners anticipate that the underlying rate design approaches that the Commission directed be adopted in compliance with the June 18 Order would serve as the basis for these new services as applied to both Co-Located Load customers and flexible large load customers.  However, the PJM Transmission Owners will still need to carefully consider, in coordination with PJM and other interested stakeholders, what additions and modifications are necessary to adapt those rate proposals to new services available under the PJM Show Cause Order.

Given the substantial overlap between the requirements in the two orders, the best approach is for PJM and the PJM Transmission Owners to develop the Co-Located Load compliance filing 17

III. REQUEST FOR EXPEDITED CONSIDERATION AND SHORTENED
COMMENT PERIOD
The PJM Transmission Owners request expedited consideration of this motion for extension and request that the Commission issue an order granting the motion no later than August 10, 2026.  Expedited consideration and the issuance of an order by that date will provide the PJM Transmission Owners sufficient assurance that they will have adequate time to develop and submit both the June 18 Order compliance filing, as well as one or more FPA section 205 filings necessary to implement the PJM Show Cause Order.  Expedited consideration will also allow the PJM Transmission Owners to focus their efforts on developing these filings in a holistic consistent fashion, rather than spending critical time preparing a separate Co-Located Load compliance filing that likely would require substantial subsequent amendments.  Consistent with this request, the PJM Transmission Owners also seek a shortened comment period on this motion for an extension of time.  Specifically, the PJM Transmission Owners request that the Commission establish a comment period of five (5) days.  Good cause exists to grant a shortened comment period for the reasons set forth above.  Moreover, no party will be prejudiced as a result of granting a five (5) day comment period, as the additional time and certainty afforded by an expedited order will accrue to the benefit of stakeholders by providing the PJM Transmission Owners sufficient time to solicit and consider stakeholder feedback on their proposals.

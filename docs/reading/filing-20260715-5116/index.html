EL26-68-000 · 2026-07-15 · Southwest Power Pool Transmission Owner Group

Answer of Southwest Power Pool Transmission Owner Group to L.M. Marlowe's 07/08/2026 Motion for Settlement Procedures

The SPP Transmission Owner Group answers L.M. Marlowe's July 8 motion for a nationwide settlement conference or settlement judge in EL26-68 and asks FERC to deny it as premature and disruptive to the regional show cause approach.

Asks FERC to: Deny the Marlowe motion for settlement procedures and a settlement conference or settlement judge.

Answers the order. Defends the E-9 SPP show cause order's regional design and its abeyance option against a request to replace regional proceedings with a settlement conference.

E-9 SPP: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5)

Key points

  1. The TO Group calls the motion premature and disruptive to FERC's choice of regional show cause proceedings.
    The Commission should deny the Motion because it is premature and would profoundly disrupt the Commission's well-reasoned decision to address the challenge of large load additions on a regional basis PDF p. 1
  2. A nationwide settlement would reject FERC's choice of regional solutions developed through stakeholder processes.
    it is a rejection of the Commission's pointed choice to address large load issues on a regional basis, rather than impose a rigid nationwide rule PDF p. 2
  3. Hundreds of ANOPR commenters would face cost and logistics in a Commission-run settlement.
    Hundreds of parties have submitted comments in response to the ANOPR, the overwhelming majority of whom are located outside of Washington, D.C. PDF p. 3
  4. The 90-day abeyance option in the show cause orders already provides for stakeholder cooperation.
    Orders already provide for the exact cooperation Movant claims to seek, without the extra administrative burden. PDF p. 3
  5. The movant's interest rests on a claimed framework, and she alleges no concrete injury from the interlocutory orders.
    The Movant alleges no cognizable, concrete, or particularized injury from the Commission's interlocutory Show Cause Orders PDF p. 4

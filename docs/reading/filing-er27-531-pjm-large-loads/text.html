INTRODUCTION 
As it is elsewhere in the country, data center construction and operation is causing 
unprecedented load growth in the PJM Region.26  PJM’s 2025 long-term load forecast 
shows a peak load growth of 32 gigawatts (“GW”) from 2024 to 2030, with 30 GW of that 
growth coming from data centers.27  PJM’s 2026 long-term load forecast projects a 3.6% 
annual increase in its summer peak load over the next 10 years;28 in 2023, PJM projected 
just a 0.8% annual increase over a 10-15 year horizon.29  The extraordinary growth of data 
centers and other Large Loads presents unique reliability and resource adequacy challenges 
as compared to traditional loads.  Data centers have significant energy and capacity 
demands and can be built faster (12-18 months) than the generation and transmission 
facilities necessary to meet those demands.30  The North American Electric Reliability 
Corporation (“NERC”) recognizes this as a resource adequacy issue, explaining that Large 
Loads are “magnitudes larger than most loads historically seen on the system and

Consistent with the Commission’s directives to establish a study process for Large

A. 
The Proposed Fast Energization Process Is Just and Reasonable and 
Advances the Commission’s Categories for Reform in the June 2026 
Orders 
The Fast Energization Process is just and reasonable for four principal reasons.  
First, it is designed to address and promote meaningful improvement in response to real 
challenges facing the grid: insufficiently fast interconnection processes for load and 
generation, parallel and duplicative study processes, speculative requests, limited visibility 
into the drivers of transmission upgrades, and an ongoing resource adequacy shortfall.  
Second, it is built on a unifying design that studies generation and load together, shortens 
the process by half, and moves readiness and financial commitments to the front of the 
process to promote readiness to build.  Third, it preserves the first-ready, first-served 
principle from recent interconnection study process reforms (consistent with Order No. 
2023)304 and adds greater transparency and cost certainty.  Fourth, its core features track 
the directives of the Large Load Show Cause Order.305  The Commission need not find that 
PJM’s proposal is the only reasonable approach in order to accept it, only that PJM’s 
proposal is a just and reasonable one.306

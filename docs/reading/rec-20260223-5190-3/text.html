

Manages all phases of assigned projects with teams of senior staff and supporting analysts across multiple offices.

Mr. Blazunas primarily provides regulatory strategy and rate case expertise as it pertains to regulated electric and gas utility rate proceedings. Mr. Blazunas joined Concentric in October 2021 after spending nine years in positions of progressively greater responsibility in the regulatory departments of large investor-owned electric utilities. Prior to joining Concentric, Mr. Blazunas managed a team at Exelon Corp. responsible for the cost of service, revenue allocation, rate design, revenue decoupling, and rate administration functions for Exelon’s Pepco Maryland and District of Columbia jurisdictions. Mr. Blazunas has experience testifying in both traditional base distribution rate case and multiyear rate plan proceedings, as well as in developing, supporting, and implementing utility of the future initiatives, including transportation electrification programs. Mr. Blazunas has testified as an expert witness before the Rhode Island Public Utilities Commission, the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio, the Maryland Public Service Commission, the Public Service Commission of the District of Columbia, and the Delaware Public Service Commission. Mr. Blazunas holds a B.A. in Economics from the University of Dayton and an M.A. in Economics from the University of Akron. He has taught both graduate and undergraduate Economics at the University of Akron and undergraduate Economics at Cuyahoga Community College.

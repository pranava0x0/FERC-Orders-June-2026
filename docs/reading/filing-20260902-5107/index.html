Answer of Southern Regulators in Support of Motion for Extension of Time filed by the Public Interest Organizations filed on 08/28/2026

The Southern Regulators (Mississippi, Arkansas and Louisiana commissions) answer in support of a motion by public interest organizations to move the comment deadline on MISO's Zero-Injection Generator Interconnection Agreement proposal from September 8 to September 15, 2026.

Asks FERC to: Grant the public interest organizations' request to extend the ER26-3552 comment deadline from September 8 to September 15, 2026.

Changes the schedule. Seeks more time to comment on MISO's ZGIA proposal, which the filers describe as part of MISO's response to the E-8 show cause order covering co-located loads.

E-8 MISO: Show cause or propose tariff changes in 60 days (p. 5) E-8 MISO: Services for flexible / co-located loads (P 6(d), p. 7)

Key points

  1. The Southern Regulators back a seven-day extension of the comment deadline, from September 8 to September 15, 2026.
    The PIOs request that the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) extend the comment deadline in this proceeding by seven days from September 8, 2026, to September 15, 2026. PDF p. 1
  2. They describe the ZGIA as part of MISO's response to the June 18 show cause order, which offered a choice between explaining the tariff and filing amendments.
    The ZGIA proposal is part of MISO’s response to a recent Show Cause Order, PDF p. 1
  3. The ZGIA is a novel framework for studying generators that are co-located with and exclusively serve large loads.
    The ZGIA framework is MISO’s novel proposal to study the interconnection of generating facilities that are co-located with PDF p. 1
  4. As retail regulators, they cite their interest in the ZGIA's reliability effect in MISO South and its fit with MISO's overall response.
    the Southern Regulators have a substantial interest in understanding how the ZGIA proposal will impact reliability in MISO South and fit into MISO’s holistic Show Cause Order Response. PDF p. 2

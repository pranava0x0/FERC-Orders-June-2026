I.
DESCRIPTION OF THE SIX CITIES
Each of the Six Cities operates a municipal electric system that is located within the
CAISO Balancing Authority Area.  The Cities are each electrically surrounded by Southern
California Edison Company (“SCE”), which is an Original Participating TO3 in the CAISO.
Although each of the Cities has made arrangements to obtain energy and capacity from a variety
of resources, the Cities are dependent upon the CAISO and SCE for the delivery of the majority
of these resources.
The Cities of Anaheim, Azusa, Banning, and Riverside became Participating TOs in the
CAISO and signed the Transmission Control Agreement (“TCA”) among and between the
CAISO and its Participating TOs effective January 1, 2003.  The City of Pasadena executed the
TCA and became a Participating TO as of January 1, 2005, and the City of Colton executed the
TCA and became a Participating TO as of January 1, 2013.  As Participating TOs in the CAISO,
each of the Cities has transferred to the CAISO’s Operational Control certain transmission
facilities and Entitlements, and they each have a TO Tariff on file with the Commission that
establishes rates, terms, and conditions of service on their facilities and Entitlements consistent
with the CAISO Tariff and the TCA.  In general, the Six Cities’ facilities and Entitlements over
which the CAISO has assumed Operational Control represent either shares of jointly-owned

II.
RELEVANT BACKGROUND
On June 18, 2026, the Commission issued the Show Cause Order, thereby initiating a
proceeding under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act.  The Show Cause Order found that the
CAISO Tariff and/or the TO Tariffs of each of the CAISO Participating TOs appear to be unjust,
unreasonable, or unduly discriminatory or preferential to the extent that they do not include
provisions establishing the rates, terms, and conditions for the interconnection of and service to
certain large and co-located loads.  The Show Cause Order therefore directed the CAISO and the
CAISO Participating TOs to either explain how their respective tariffs remain just and
reasonable without specific provisions for the interconnection of large and co-located loads or to
develop such provisions.4
III.
JUSTIFICATION FOR RESCISSION
Each of the Six Cities is a non-public utility that is exempt from the Commission’s
jurisdiction pursuant to Section 201(f) of the Federal Power Act.5  The Commission
acknowledged that the Show Cause Order included Participating TOs that are not subject to the
Commission’s jurisdiction and explained that it would “rescind the show cause order” against
such entities at their request, provided that the CAISO “respond[s] to this order regarding the

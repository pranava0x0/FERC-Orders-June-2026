ANSWER OF CONSTELLATION ENERGY GENERATION, LLC
Constellation submits this answer1 to respond to the Transmission Owners’ Request for
Clarification2 of the Large Load Show Cause Order.3 The Transmission Owners also request
rehearing in the above-captioned proceeding, and although Constellation disagrees with the
Transmission Owners’ rehearing arguments, including those in Exelon and FirstEnergy’s joint
rehearing request,4 Constellation does not respond here because Exelon and FirstEnergy’s
arguments are premature and barred by Rule 713 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and
Procedure.5
The Transmission Owners request that with respect to the obligation to define “rates, terms,
and conditions applicable to interconnection customers serving ‘electrically proximate large load’

or co-located load”6 the Commission clarify that they will be permitted to propose and support a

respect to Co-Located Loads, as it has already ruled twice that the Point of Interconnection and

thinly-veiled collateral attack on the Commission’s previous rulings in Docket Numbers ER26-
1088-000 and ER26-1479-000.
In addition, the Commission should clarify that any eligibility requirements PJM and the
Transmission Owners propose in their initial filings must not be so prescriptive as to improperly
disqualify large loads, foreclosing any benefits that help avoid unnecessary transmission upgrades
and reduce strain on the grid. The Commission should also clarify that Transmission Owners may
not use the Point of Change in Ownership, or any other point of reference, to define “electrically
proximate large load” in a manner that likewise undermines speedy interconnection or avoidance
of unnecessary transmission upgrades. As the Co-Located Load proceeding demonstrates,9 clear
direction from the Commission is necessary to ensure that PJM and Transmission Owners propose
terms and conditions that facilitate, rather than frustrate, large load interconnections.

CONCLUSION
For the reasons stated above, Constellation respectfully requests that the Commission deny
the Transmission Owners’ Request for Clarification, but if the Commission nonetheless grants the
request, it should make clear that PJM and the Transmission Owners should propose terms that
encourage the use of new transmission services and the speedy interconnection of large loads.

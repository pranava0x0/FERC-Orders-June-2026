Dear Secretary Reese,
The Southwest Power Pool Regional State Committee (RSC) respectfully submits this letter in
support of Southwest Power Pool, Inc.’s (SPP) request for an extension of time, until November
20, 2026, to respond to the Commission’s June 18, 2026, Order to Show Cause in the above-
referenced proceeding.
The Commission’s order raises significant and complex issues concerning the integration of large
loads, including how to mitigate the risk that transmission costs attributable to large-load
development may be shifted to other transmission customers. These issues directly implicate the
RSC’s responsibility within the SPP regional governance structure for determining regional
proposals concerning the allocation of transmission costs.
The RSC, working through SPP’s Cost Control and Allocation Review and Evaluation (CARE)
Team, is currently considering potential measures to mitigate cost-shift risks associated with large
load customers. Among other matters, the CARE Team is evaluating approaches for assigning or
securing responsibility for transmission costs driven by new large loads, particularly when
projected load does not materialize as anticipated or does not remain on the system for the period
used to justify transmission investment.
The CARE Team expects to develop a recommendation for consideration by the RSC and the SPP
Board of Directors during their quarterly business meetings on November 16 and 17, 2026. That
recommendation will reflect substantial analysis and regional stakeholder and state-regulatory
input regarding matters that are related to the cost-shifting concerns identified in the Commission’s
order.
Granting the requested extension would allow the RSC and SPP to complete this established
regional process and would enable SPP’s response to more fully reflect the resulting
recommendations. It would also promote the development of a thoughtful, durable, and regionally

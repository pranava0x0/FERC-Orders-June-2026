MOTION FOR EXTENSION OF TIME
OF THE ORGANIZATION OF PJM STATES, INC.

Pursuant to Rules 212 and 2008 of the Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Federal
Energy Regulatory Commission (“FERC” or the “Commission”),1 the Organization of PJM States,
Inc. (“OPSI”) submits this Motion for an Extension of Time to submit comments in response to
PJM’s August 13, 2026, filing proposing revisions to the Open Access Transmission Tariff
(“Tariff”) and the Reliability Assurance Agreement Among Load Serving Entities (“RAA”) to
establish Interim Resource Adequacy Service (“IRAS”), among other things.2 OPSI requests that
the Commission extend the time for comments in this docket from September 3, 2026 to September
14, 2026. OPSI requests that the Commission act on this motion by September 1, 2026.3 PJM has
authorized OPSI to represent that PJM does not oppose this Motion for Extension of Time.
I.
BACKGROUND
On August 13, 2026, PJM filed a proposal to create “a new supplemental capacity product
through which New Large Loads [(“NLL”)] provide a load reduction service that is a tool available
to PJM to manage resource adequacy shortfalls that would otherwise drive shedding of native
loads.”4 PJM also proposed to create a new large load registry and meaningful changes to the
position of the region’s Variable Resource Requirement (“VRR”) curve, depending on the amount

II.
MOTION FOR EXTENSION OF TIME
Pursuant to the combined notice of filings issued on August 13, 2026, comments on PJM's
proposal are due on September 3, 2026.6 Good cause exists to extend the comment deadline in this
proceeding from September 3, 2026 to September 14, 2026 to allow the retail regulators in the
PJM region additional time to consider the complex interaction between the rights and
responsibilities of PJM, Electric Distributors, and retail regulators related to the implementation
of IRAS and the associated compensation mechanism. OPSI asserts that no party will be prejudiced
should FERC grant this motion, nor is this motion made solely for the purpose of delay.
PJM proposes meaningful state-federal interactions in the implementation of the IRAS
product itself and the compensation rate for the service. In order to ensure that the retail electric
regulators in the PJM region have the opportunity to provide substantive comments, OPSI
respectfully requests a limited extension of time to file comments in this docket. Failing to grant
the motion would result in rushed reactions to PJM’s proposal, which has materially evolved since
PJM presented it to stakeholders at the end of June. Granting the extension would, therefore,
support the Commission in its decision-making. The issues in this docket are not insignificant. The
proposal raises significant questions related to the interaction between wholesale and retail
ratemaking.

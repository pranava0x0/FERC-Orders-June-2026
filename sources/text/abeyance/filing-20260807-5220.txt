ANSWER OF CORPORATE ENERGY BUYERS ASSOCIATION
IN SUPPORT OF THE MOTION FOR ABEYANCE

Pursuant to Rule 213 of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s (Commission)
Rules of Practice and Procedure,1 Corporate Energy Buyers Association (CEBA) respectfully
submits this Answer in support of the amended motion for abeyance submitted jointly by
Southwest Power Pool, Inc. (SPP) and SPP transmission owners (collectively, Joint Movants).2
Joint Movants ask the Commission to hold this proceeding in abeyance by 95 days and, in so
doing, extend certain deadlines for responding to the June 18, 2026 Order to Show Cause (Show
Cause Order).3
The issues raised in the Show Cause Order raise complex and substantial issues that
require due consideration to craft solutions that are appropriately tailored to the region. Granting
the requested abeyance will promote administrative efficiencies by allowing time for SPP and
stakeholders—including CEBA and its members—to work collaboratively, as opposed to a
rushed proposal that would unnecessarily shift stakeholder debate to the Commission docket.
The requested abeyance also reflects reasonable accommodations necessary to convene the
Regional State Committee and the SPP Board of Directors, both of which are essential steps in
SPP’s stakeholder process. Lastly, the requested abeyance strikes an appropriate balance: a 95-

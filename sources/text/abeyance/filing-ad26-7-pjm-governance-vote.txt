Re: PJM Governance and Stakeholder Reforms, Docket No. AD26-7-000

Dear Secretary Reese:
In accordance with the Commission’s notice issued August 10th in the above-referenced matter,
on September 1st, representatives of PJM, the states in the region, Members, and stakeholders
commenced confidential and privileged discussions facilitated by FERC’s Dispute Resolution
Service. This is an informational update on the proceedings.
Three term sheets of proposed governance and stakeholder reforms were considered at a
Special Members Committee meeting on September 30, 2026. The Members term sheet,
sponsored by a group of PJM Members, received the requisite sector-weighted two-thirds
majority vote of the PJM Members Committee, and advanced over a PJM term sheet and over
a Consumer Advocates term sheet.
Accordingly, the Members term sheet represents an agreement in principle among PJM’s
Members. PJM notes that the Organization of PJM States, Inc., the PJM Governors’
Collaborative and other state representatives do not view the approved term sheet as satisfactory.
Neither PJM’s nor the Consumer Advocates’ term sheet received the requisite two-thirds or even
a majority vote.
Thank you for your attention to this matter.

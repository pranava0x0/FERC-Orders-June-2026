support of the ISO-NE’s request for a short extension of the comment period on the

I. ANSWER
As noted by the ISO, given the breadth of this filing with Tariff Revisions, as well
as the upcoming holiday, an extension of the comment period in the instant docket to 24
days, or until December 7, 2026, would help to ensure that interested parties have
ample time to review and develop comments on the filing, particularly multiple member
organizations like trade associations and NGOs (“Non-Governmental Organizations”).4
EPSA therefore agrees with ISO-NE and respectfully requests that the Commission
grant this request. The short extension would have the added benefit of aligning the
comment period with the other expected large loads filings that will be filed with the
Commission on November 16, which would allow stakeholders to take a holistic look at
these critical proceedings, benefiting the respective records in these multiple
proceedings.
II. CONCLUSION
WHEREFORE, EPSA respectfully requests that the Commission grant ISO-NE’s
request to extend the comment period in this proceeding, so that comments would be
due on December 7, 2026.

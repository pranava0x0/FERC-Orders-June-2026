ANSWER OF SOUTHERN REGULATORS IN SUPPORT OF MOTION FOR
EXTENSION OF TIME

The Mississippi Public Service Commission, the Arkansas Public Service Commission,
Louisiana Public Service Commission, and the Council of the City of New Orleans (the Southern
Regulators) submit this answer1 in support of the Public Interest Organizations’ (PIOs)2 motion
for an extension of time3 to submit comments on the Midcontinent Independent System Operator,
Inc.’s (MISO) Zero-Injection Generator Interconnection Agreement (ZGIA) proposal in the above
captioned docket. The PIOs request that the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission
(Commission) extend the comment deadline in this proceeding by seven days from September 8,
2026, to September 15, 2026. Good cause exists for the Commission to grant the PIOs’ request.4
The ZGIA proposal is part of MISO’s response to a recent Show Cause Order,5 which
required MISO to either (i) explain why its tariff remains just and reasonable and not unduly
discriminatory or preferential without provisions addressing, among other things, transmission
service and co-location arrangements for large load customers or (ii) file amendments to make its
tariff just and reasonable as applied to large and co-located loads.6 The ZGIA framework is
MISO’s novel proposal to study the interconnection of generating facilities that are co-located with

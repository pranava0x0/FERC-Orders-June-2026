Relentless Energy Systems LLC respectfully submits the enclosed technical evidence
and Xyce netlists and results to assist regional transmission organizations and the
Commission in mitigating the infrastructure impacts of large loads in support of the
Commission’s ongoing Show Cause orders. This filing provides comprehensive
analytical data, solver-verified models, and engineering specifications for a novel
Grid Enhancing Technology (GET) directly applicable to the large load interconnection
constraints and transmission congestion issues driven by the unprecedented national
growth of high-density loads.
This submission details Geometric Phase Isolation (GPI), a passive five‑dimensional
LCFVR parallel resonant manifold engineered to physically offload magnetizing VARs
at the Point of Load. The attached evidence demonstrates that GPI provides Xyce

C.
The Commission’s June Order
On June 18, 2026, the Commission issued the June Order, addressing the Sixty-
Day Compliance Filing, the paper hearing submissions, and requests for rehearing and
clarification of the December Order.35
The June Order found that PJM’s Sixty-Day Compliance Filing partially complies

just and reasonable and directed PJM to make a compliance filing to revise its Tariff.37

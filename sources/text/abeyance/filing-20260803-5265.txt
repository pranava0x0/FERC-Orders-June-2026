II.
GOOD CAUSE EXISTS TO HOLD THIS PROCEEDING IN ABEYANCE
Good cause exists to grant the Joint Movants' abeyance request. Granting the
requested abeyance will allow SPP to utilize its stakeholder process to develop
comprehensive Tariff revisions responsive to the Show Cause Order. The issues identified
by the Commission involve numerous, interrelated aspects of the Tariff, and developing
proposed Tariff revisions addressing these issues requires careful technical analysis,
coordination among numerous stakeholder groups and membership sectors, and
consideration through SPP's governance process before any proposed revisions can be
presented to the Board for approval and filed with the Commission pursuant to Section 205
of the FPA.
The requested abeyance also will promote administrative efficiency. Absent an
abeyance, SPP, Transmission Owners, stakeholders, and the Commission would be
required to simultaneously litigate the issues raised in this Section 206 proceeding while
SPP conducts the stakeholder process necessary to develop potential Tariff revisions
addressing those same issues. By contrast, the requested abeyance will allow SPP to focus
its efforts on developing a comprehensive Section 205 filing or a package of Section 205
filings that responds to the Commission's concerns through its stakeholder process, thereby
potentially narrowing or resolving issues that otherwise would be litigated in this
proceeding. In addition, feedback received through the stakeholder process has the

Motion.

A.
Transmission Service to Eligible Customers on Behalf of Large Loads
In the Show Cause Order, the Commission finds that the Tariff is preliminarily
unjust and unreasonable because it does not require (1) the evaluation of alternative
transmission technologies in transmission service request studies, in all instances, without
the need for a request from the Eligible Customer seeking transmission service on behalf
of a large load; and (2) if traditional Network Upgrades are selected instead of alternative
transmission technologies, inclusion in the study report to the Eligible Customer seeking
transmission service on behalf of a large load of a sufficiently clear demonstration of why
alternative transmission technologies are not feasible or would not result in lower costs or
a faster timeline for accommodating the transmission service request.14

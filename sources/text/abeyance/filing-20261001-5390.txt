Re: Docket No. EL26-70-000 - Public Comment of a Pulaski County, Arkansas Resident
To the Secretary of the Commission:
Please accept the attached public comment in Docket No. EL26-70-000. I am filing as an individual resident
and electric customer in Pulaski County, Arkansas.
My comment asks the Commission to keep transparency, cost responsibility, reliable planning, and
protection of existing customers at the center of its consideration of rapidly growing large loads such as data
centers.
I appreciate the Commission providing members of the public an opportunity to participate in this
proceeding. My identifying and contact information are provided through FERC eFiling.
Respectfully submitted,
Individual resident and electric customer
Pulaski County, Arkansas

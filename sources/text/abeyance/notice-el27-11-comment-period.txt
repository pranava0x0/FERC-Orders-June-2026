UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY COMMISSION

Exelon Corporation

v.

PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.

  Docket No.
EL27-11-000

NOTICE ESTABLISHING COMMENT PERIOD

(November 13, 2026)

	On November 4, 2026, PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (PJM) filed a preliminary answer opposing fast-track processing and a motion requesting a four-week extension of time, until December 21, 2026, to answer the complaint filed by Exelon Corporation (Exelon) in the above-captioned proceeding.  PJM argues that there is good cause to grant the extension because the additional time will permit PJM to incorporate into its response the outcome of the deliberations by the PJM Board, which will meet on November 17, 2026.  PJM also argues that Exelon has failed to demonstrate any compelling reason for Commission action by the requested January 31, 2027, date and therefore a four-week extension of the answer deadline will not impair the Commission’s ability to act.

On November 9, 2026, Exelon filed a motion for leave to answer and answer.  Exelon supports an extension of the comment period to December 7, 2026, but argues that the scale of PJM’s proposed extension is excessive for the narrow scope of the allegations it must answer.  Exelon also urges the Commission to act sufficiently in advance of the May 2027 Base Residual Auction.

	Upon consideration, notice is hereby given that PJM’s motion for extension of time is granted and the deadline to file the answer to Exelon’s complaint, comments, and interventions is December 21, 2026.

Carlos D. Clay,
Deputy Secretary.

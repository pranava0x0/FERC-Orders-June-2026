Pursuant to Section 205 of the Federal Power Act (“FPA”), 16 U.S.C. § 824d,
and Section 35.13 of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission’s (“Commission”)
Regulations, 18 C.F.R. § 35.13, Southwest Power Pool, Inc. ("SPP") submits revisions
to Schedule 15 and Attachment T of its Open Access Transmission Tariff (“Tariff”)1
to add provisions for Conditional High Impact Large Load Service (“CHILLS”) for
pricing Zones in the SPP West Region, and to make other clarifying edits.

SPP respectfully requests that the Commission accept the proposed revisions,
find the revisions just and reasonable, and set an effective date of February 1, 2027.

II.
DESCRIPTION OF AND JUSTIFICATION FOR PROPOSED TARIFF
REVISIONS

A.
Schedule 15

In the CHILLS Filing, SPP proposed a new Schedule 15, to establish charges
for CHILLS.8 SPP proposes to add pricing Zones 100, 101, 102, 103, and 104 under
Section 1 of Schedule 15:

Zone 100:
Colorado Springs Utilities

Zone 101:
Platte River Power Authority

Zone 102:
Tri-State G&T Association – West

Zone 103:
Colorado River Storage Project Zone

Zone 104:
Loveland Area Projects Zone

These revisions are just and reasonable because they effectuate the intent of the
CHILLS Filing already accepted by the Commission and will allow those entities in
the SPP West Region that take CHILLS to also pay for the use of the transmission
facilities located in those newly formed Zones in the SPP West Region.

for all applicable Transmission Owners under the Tariff. SPP proposes to revise the

First, SPP adds a reference to Schedule 14 for the DC Tie Access Charge. The
DC Tie Access Charge is defined as “[a] charge on a transmission reservation utilizing
a West DC Tie that sinks outside of the Zone where the West DC Tie is located.”11 The
Transmission Customer is responsible for the DC Tie Access Charge determined in
Schedule 14 of the Tariff. SPP adds this reference to the rate sheets where the DC Tie
Access Charge is applicable.

Second, SPP adds a reference to Schedule 15 to appropriately adjust the Point-
To-Point Transmission Service Rates for any revenues received for Schedule 15
charges. The proposed changes to add a reference to Schedule 15 revenues are just and
reasonable because they will appropriately adjust the Point-To-Point Transmission
Service Rates for any revenues received for Schedule 15 charges. The changes are
consistent with the revisions to other rate sheets accepted by the Commission in the
CHILLS Filing.

Third, SPP adds a reference to Incremental Market Efficiency Use revenues.
The Incremental Market Efficiency Use Charge is “[a] market recovery mechanism to
compensate West DC Tie Transmission Owners for the expected loss of life of certain
West DC Tie facilities due to increased utilization of the West DC Ties by the
Integrated Marketplace.”12 SPP adds this reference to the rate sheets where Incremental
Market Efficiency Use revenues are applicable.

Fourth, SPP removes the reference to Attachment AU revenues. Due to an
administrative error, SPP mistakenly included references to Attachment AU revenues
rate sheets for Loveland Area Projects, SPP West Region Average, and Tri-State G&T
Association-West in the SPP West Region. As explained in the SPP Expansion Filing,
Attachment AU revenues are allocated only to Transmission Owners in the SPP East
Region.13

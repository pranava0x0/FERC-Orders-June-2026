# PJM’s two filings: what changed on November 9

PJM filed the registry provisions left standing by FERC’s October 9 order. Separately, it refiled its proposed reliability backstop under the correct section 205 filing code. FERC’s [November 10 notice](https://elibrary.ferc.gov/eLibrary/filelist?accession_number=20261110-3030) set **November 30 at 5 p.m. Eastern** for comments on both. It did not approve either filing. The June show-cause deadlines remain separate: **November 16**, or **November 20 for SPP**.

| Question | What the original says |
|---|---|
| What counts as one large load? | The threshold is **50 MW of cumulative peak demand** at a single electrical site. Affiliated facilities within a one-mile radius can count together under the configurations in the agreement. The registry is not the curtailment service FERC rejected. [Clean agreement, p. 109](../../docs/reading/compliance-er26-3515-pjm/text.html#p-109) |
| Who can see the data? | PJM would publish aggregate totals by distributor area where possible; officials and market participants receiving confidential data remain subject to safeguards. The Ohio Consumers’ Counsel sought comparable confidential access for consumer advocates in a separate rehearing filing. [Registry, p. 124](../../docs/reading/compliance-er26-3515-pjm/text.html#p-124) · [Ohio’s filing](../../docs/reading/rehearing-er26-3515-ohio/index.html) |
| When must a load be reported? | The proposal sets March 1, 2027 for loads entering service before June 1, 2027. Later loads must be reported before entering service; suppliers must promptly report changes. [Schedule 11, p. 125](../../docs/reading/compliance-er26-3515-pjm/text.html#p-125) |
| How much security would suppliers post? | PJM proposes **40% of the discounted payment obligation**. At an assumed clearing price of $555/MW-day, its example yields about **$686,000 per MW** of collateral. Neither the auction price nor the bill has been set. [Transmittal, p. 23](../../docs/reading/refiling-er27-454-pjm-backstop/text.html#p-23) |
| Can a supplier leave the obligation behind? | PJM proposes continuing liability after a load-serving entity leaves. A subsequent rate filing would determine the amount. PJM asks for a March 1, 2027 procurement start and a four-week offer window. [Departure, p. 36](../../docs/reading/refiling-er27-454-pjm-backstop/text.html#p-36) · [Timing, p. 62](../../docs/reading/refiling-er27-454-pjm-backstop/text.html#p-62) |

**The states did not form one bloc.** Thirteen commissions backed [OPSI’s August backstop comments](https://opsi.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/OPSI-ER26-3380-000-RBP-Comments.pdf#page=1); West Virginia did not. Eleven, including West Virginia, backed [OPSI’s September interim-service comments](https://opsi.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ER26-3515-000-IRAS-Comments.pdf#page=1); Kentucky, Ohio and Virginia abstained. Those were votes on OPSI’s comments about earlier versions. Ohio’s consumer advocate is a separate office from the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio.

**The commissioners left a record of what they would scrutinize.** In the September backstop order, Swett called for a record FERC could test, and See focused on whether the forecast justified shifting costs to existing customers. At [Senate oversight](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119shrg64322/pdf/CHRG-119shrg64322.pdf), Rosner linked investment signals to state confidence (physical pp. 69–70), Chang connected upgrade costs to the customers triggering them (p. 75), and LaCerte discussed beneficiary payment and state oversight (pp. 79–80). None of those statements is a vote on the November filings. [Attributed positions and sources](perspective-coverage.md).

**Beyond PJM:** MISO proposed studying some new loads and nearby generation together; state regulators asked what happens to existing queue customers. SPP’s November 10 Upper Missouri amendment restores two omitted revenue references and requests a February effective date. In California, Morongo Transmission had already answered on August 12 as a transmission owner with no end-use customers. Its answer does not settle CAISO’s proceeding.

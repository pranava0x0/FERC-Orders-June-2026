Pursuant to Rules 211 and 212 of the Rules of Practice and Procedure of Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (“FERC” or the “Commission”), in an abundance of caution, and after 
completing the instructions for refiling provided by the FERC Online team, Equinix Inc. 
(“Equinix”) is submitting this Motion for Leave to Protest, Seek Clarification on and Comment 
Out-Of-Time to the filing by PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (“PJM”) entitled “Proposal of PJM 
Interconnection, L.L.C. to Establish Interim Resource Adequacy Service and a Large Load 
Registry to Address System Reliability” (the “IRAS Filing”), which PJM submitted on August 
13, 2026 in the above-captioned docket.1   
On August 13, FERC publicly noticed the PJM filing and established September 3 as the 
deadline for interventions and the submittal of comments and protests. Equinix filed a timely 
doc-less motion to intervene in this proceeding on Aug. 21, 2026. At 4:20 pm on Sept. 3, 2026, 
prior to the 5:00 p.m. deadline, Equinix submitted what it believed to be its comments, a request 
for clarification, and a protest of the PJM proposal.  However, as detailed in the attached, it later 
that day received notice from FERC Online that FERC watermarks were on the document as

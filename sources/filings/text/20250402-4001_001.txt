Dear Senators Van Hollen, Booker, Kelly, and Kaine: 
 
  
Thank you for your February 14, 2025, letter alerting the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (Commission) to your request that the Commission convene 
a technical conference addressing increasing electricity demand from data centers and 
other large new loads.  Your concerns are essential to ensuring the Commission’s 
consideration of this matter is both responsive and accurate.   
 
In your letter, you highlight the importance of ensuring that sufficient new 
generation becomes available to meet growing electricity demand while maintaining 
just and reasonable rates.  You state that, as part of a technical conference, the 
Commission should consider the range of policy options available to address resource 
adequacy challenges.  I am pleased to confirm that the Commission has announced a 
Commissioner-led technical conference on June 4 and 5, 2025, to explore the 
challenges to resource adequacy in regional transmission organization and 
independent system operator regions, including issues pertaining to load growth.  I 
invite you and your staff to attend the technical conference.  In addition, I wish to 
highlight that the Commission voted unanimously on February 20, 2025, to institute a 
show cause proceeding reviewing issues associated with the co-location of large loads 
in the PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. region in Docket No. EL25-49 et al. 
You have my commitment that we will continue to monitor this situation.  I 
take seriously the Commission’s obligation to ensure our actions are in the public 
interest and can assure you that any decisions the Commission makes will be based 
upon a thorough and careful review of the records.

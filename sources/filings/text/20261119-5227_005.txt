3.
I am submitting this affidavit to support the accuracy of the information
included in Attachment A to this affidavit. The gross and net transmission plant per 
megawatt (“MW”) estimated values for the SPP Region that are included in Attachment A 
were used as support for the Load Safe Harbor Limit proposed in the above referenced
--- PAGE 3 ---
docket. The difference between the gross and net plant values shown in Attachment A is 
the amount of accumulated depreciation expense associated with the transmission assets. 
The transmission plant per MW values were derived from: (1) gross and net plant values 
and annual transmission revenue requirement (“ATRR”) values in individual Transmission 
Owner formula rate template updates (effective as of the August 2026); and (2) ATRR and 
zonal coincident peak (“CP”) load information sourced from the August 2026 Revenue 
Requirements and Rates (“RRR”) file.1 The loads represent the sum, across all Zones in 
the SPP Region in the first column and across all Zones in the SPP East Region in the 
second column, of the highest monthly value for each Zone during calendar year 2025. 
Values for gross and net plant were sourced primarily from the Transmission Owners’ 
formula rates. However, approximately 4% of the value was derived through estimation, 
based on the ratios between the gross and net plant amounts and the ATRR amounts of 
those Transmission Owners for which formula rate information was used, multiplied by 
the ATRR amounts of those Transmission Owners for which formula rate information was 
not used.     
4.
To the best of my knowledge, the information included in Attachment A is
true and correct.

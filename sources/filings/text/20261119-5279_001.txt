EXPEDITED MOTION FOR A NINE-DAY EXTENSION OF TIME OF  
CONSTELLATION ENERGY GENERATION, LLC 
 
Constellation Energy Generation, LLC (“Constellation”) respectfully requests a short 
nine-day extension of the December 7, 2026 comment deadline in the above-captioned dockets. 
On November 16, 2026, Pacific Gas and Electric Company (“PG&E”), Southern 
California Edison Company (“SCE”), and San Diego Gas & Electric Company (“SDG&E”) 
submitted filings under section 205 of the Federal Power Act in the above-captioned dockets in 
response to the Commission’s June 18, 2026 order instituting a section 206 proceeding (“Show 
Cause Proceeding”) requiring a response from the California Independent System Operator Corp. 
(“CAISO”) and numerous transmission-owning utilities in the CAISO region.1  CAISO also 
made a section 205 filing on November 16, 2026 in response to the same Commission order in 
Docket No. ER27-508-000.  The Show Cause Proceeding addresses RTO/ISO and transmission 
utility tariff provisions relating to large load integration.   
The PG&E, SCE, and SDG&E filings currently have been assigned a comment date of 
December 7, 2026, while the CAISO filing has a comment date of December 15, 2026.  All of 
these filings involve important issues that are highly interrelated.  As such, Constellation

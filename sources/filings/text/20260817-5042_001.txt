MOTION TO INTERVENE OF PERSISTENCE ANALYTICS GROUP LLC
Pursuant to Rule 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure, 18 C.F.R. § 385.214, 
Persistence Analytics Group LLC ("PAG") respectfully moves to intervene in the above-
captioned proceeding.
I. INTEREST
PAG is a New Jersey-based small business focused on infrastructure risk analytics, including 
infrastructure assumption verification, decision assurance, demand durability, grid stress, load 
integrity, and large-load development. This proceeding concerns PJM's proposed Reliability 
Backstop Procurement and presents issues directly relevant to PAG's work, including the 
evidentiary basis for modeled resource-adequacy needs, the maturity and durability of forecast 
large-load demand, and the relationship between projected need and long-duration procurement 
commitments. PAG's participation may assist the Commission, and no other party can 
adequately represent PAG's particular interests and perspective.
II. POSITION
PAG does not take a position on the ultimate disposition of PJM's filing through this motion. 
PAG seeks intervention so that it may address whether material assumptions supporting the 
proposed procurement are sufficiently evidenced and subject to appropriate revalidation before 
long-duration contractual obligations are created.

I. 
MOTION FOR LEAVE TO ANSWER 
NOVEC respectfully requests leave to submit this Answer. The Commission regularly 
allows answers for good cause shown, such as when the answers help ensure a more accurate and 
complete record, clarify the issues, or provide useful and relevant information that will assist the 
Commission in its deliberative process.5 This Answer will correct Vistra’s mischaracterizations of 
NOVEC’s pleading and provide other useful and relevant information that will assist the 
Commission in its deliberative process. As such, the Commission has good cause to accept 
NOVEC’s Answer. 
II. 
ANSWER 
 
 
Vistra wrongly asserts that NOVEC’s July 29 Motion for Clarification is an untimely 
request for rehearing because “the substance of the filing directly contests the merits” of the Order.6 
Vistra may disagree with NOVEC as to the clarity of the Order and may disagree with NOVEC’s 
opinion on aspects of the Order, but it should not inaccurately characterize the nature of NOVEC’s 
pleading. NOVEC’s Motion for Clarification did not ask the Commission to set aside, in whole or 
in part, its Order. NOVEC asked the Commission to provide additional detail on cost support 
information and to clarify whether the Order permits an earlier section 205 proposal on a Grid 
Reliance Charge than when Co-Located Load begins operating, which the Commission may or 
may not answer.  
NOVEC is quite aware of the difference between a request for rehearing and a motion for 
clarification. It is precisely because NOVEC is seeking clarification and not rehearing that

NOVEC did not conform its pleading to any of the requirements set forth by Rule 713. NOVEC 
also understands that, unlike a request for rehearing, its motion for clarification does not satisfy 
the statutory precondition for seeking judicial review of the Order. NOVEC’s Motion for 
Clarification simply is not a request for rehearing. Because the foundational premise of Vistra’s 
Answer is simply wrong and belied by NOVEC’s pleading, the Commission should reject Vistra’s 
Answer. Whether or not the Commission elects to provide any clarification of the Order, there is 
nothing procedurally improper about NOVEC’s request.  
III. 
CONCLUSION 
WHEREFORE, for the foregoing reasons, NOVEC respectfully requests that the 
Commission reject Vistra’s August 18 Answer and issue an order clarifying the June 18, 2026, 
Order as requested in NOVEC’s July 29 Motion for Clarification.

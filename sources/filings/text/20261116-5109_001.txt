I. 
BACKGROUND ON CITIZENS SYCAMORE 
 
Citizens Sycamore is a wholly owned subsidiary of Citizens Energy 
Corporation, a non-profit organization dedicated to making life’s basic needs 
more affordable through successful business ventures.  To date, Citizens 
Energy Corporation has donated more than $600 million to programs 
supporting the most vulnerable in our communities.   
 
In 2019, Citizens Sycamore invested approximately $27 million with 
San Diego Gas & Electric Company (“SDG&E”) in the Sycamore-Penasquitos 
Project (“Sycamore Project”) in exchange for a 30-year leasehold interest in 
approximately 12.92% interest in the underground segment of the Sycamore 
Project.2  Citizens Sycamore does not own any of the actual facilities.  
SDG&E retains ownership and operation and maintenance responsibilities.  
Citizens Sycamore became a CAISO PTO, turned its interest in the Sycamore  
Project over to the CAISO’s operational control and recovers its costs through 
its TO Tariff.  As reflected in its TO Tariff, Citizens Sycamore is a “Non-Load-
Serving Participating TO and has no End-Use Customers.”3 
 
As part of Citizens Energy Corporation’s charitable mission, Citizens 
Sycamore is contractually committed to dedicate 50% of its net after-tax cash 
flow from the Sycamore Project to programs assisting low-income families in 
SDG&E’s service area.  To date, the Sycamore Project has generated $3.7 
million in charitable donations, which are currently being used to purchase 
electric vehicles for community aid organizations, including Meals on Wheels 
and the Indian Health Council, among others.  Citizens Sycamore estimates 
its involvement in the Sycamore Project will generate approximately $14 
million in charitable assistance over the term of the lease.

study…the provision of transmission service to Eligible Customers on behalf

As discussed in more detail below, Citizens Sycamore has a limited,

governed by the relevant provisions of the SDG&E TO Tariff. 
 
A. 
Relevant Existing TO Tariff Provisions 
 
Citizens Sycamore’s current TO Tariff recognizes the limited nature of 
its interest in the Sycamore Project.  Section 16.12 of Citizens Sycamore’s 
current TO Tariff states that for so long as the Transfer Capability Lease 
between Citizens Sycamore and SDG&E is in effect, Sections 8 (“Obligation 
to Interconnect or Construct Transmission Expansions and Facility 
Upgrades”), 9 (“Expansion Process”), and 10 (“Interconnection Process”) of the 
Citizens Sunrise Tariff will be “superseded by the corresponding sections of 
the TO Tariff of San Diego Gas & Electric.”5  Article III of the Transfer 
Capability Lease, which is included at Appendix II to the Sycamore TO 
Tariff, outlines the division of responsibility between SDG&E and Citizens 
Sycamore, with SDG&E retaining all responsibility for operation and 
maintenance6 and to act as the interconnection agent for the Sycamore 
Project.7

B. 
Proposed TO Tariff Provision Revisions 
 
 
In response to the Show Cause Order, Citizens Sycamore is proposing a 
series of revisions to its TO Tariff to ensure it is clear that when it comes to 
all interconnection matters, including interconnection and service of large 
load customers or co-located arrangements, the appropriate TO Tariff 
provisions are those of SDG&E.  These revisions include the following: 
 
• Revisions to the definitions of Expedited Interconnection Agreement, 
Facilities Study Agreement, Facility or Facilities Study, 
Interconnection, Interconnection Agreement, Interconnection 
Application, Reliability Upgrade, Requests for Expedited 
Interconnection Procedures, System Impact Study, and System Impact 
Study Agreement, each to clarify that SDG&E’s TO Tariff provisions 
will govern. 
 
• Added new language to Section 8, Obligations to Interconnect or 
Construct Transmission and Expansions and Facility Upgrades, to 
confirm that all conduct that falls under Section 8 will be governed by 
SDG&E’s TO Tariff. 
 
• Added new language to Section 9, Expansion Process, to confirm that 
all conduct that falls under Section 9 will be governed by SDG&E’s TO 
Tariff. 
 
• Added new language to Section 10, Interconnection Process, to confirm 
that all conduct that falls under Section 10 will be governed by 
SDG&E’s TO Tariff. 
 
• Citizens Sycamore additionally makes a series of administrative 
revisions to address typos, update addresses, and similar type changes 
with this filing.

Citizens Sycamore respectfully submits that these revisions address the 
concerns raised in the Show Cause Order within the bounds of Citizens 
Sycamore’s limited interest in, and control over, the Sycamore Project.  Put 
simply, Citizens Sycamore is bound by the terms of its Transfer Capability 
Lease with SDG&E, and that agreement makes it clear that SDG&E, as the 
owner and operator of the Sycamore Project, has the authority and 
responsibility for all operational and interconnection related matters. 
 
 
To the extent that the CAISO or SDG&E propose TO Tariff revisions 
that impact the revisions Citizens Sycamore proposes in this filing, Citizens 
Sycamore commits to making a compliance filing to ensure its TO Tariff 
remains consistent.

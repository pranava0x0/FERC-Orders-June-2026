I. 
MOTION FOR EXTENSION OF TIME 
The Commission may extend filing deadlines for good cause.4  Here, good cause exists 
for the Commission to grant Public Interest Organizations’ request.  MISO’s Zero-Injection 
Generator Interconnection Agreement (“ZGIA”) filing responds to a lengthy Show Cause Order 
that itself followed a multi-stage rulemaking process5 in response to action by the United States 
Department of Energy.  The Commission’s Show Cause Order charged MISO with showing why 
its existing tariff should be found just and reasonable on certain issues concerning the integration 
of large electric loads into the bulk power system, or else with filing tariff amendments to render 
the tariff just and reasonable.  MISO and the MISO Transmission Owners then sought a 90-day 
abeyance of MISO’s response deadline (August 17, 2026) under the Show Cause Order, arguing 
that several new policy reforms, including ZGIA, to be codified through Section 205 tariff 
proposals during the abeyance period will adequately respond to the Show Cause Order.6   MISO 
expressly presents its Zero-Injection Generator Interconnection proposal in the instant filing as 
part of its response to the Show Cause Order.7

Public Interest Organizations, as well as other parties, would benefit from the additional 
time to review the ZGIA filing, evaluate the issues presented, consult with relevant technical and 
legal personnel, and prepare meaningful comments for the Commission’s consideration.  The 
complexity of the rulemaking process, Show Cause Order, and the instant ZGIA filing warrant 
providing commenters this additional time.  Furthermore, during the comment period set by the 
Commission, MISO also invited seven separate comment pieces from stakeholders at the August 
27, 2026 Large Load Working Group meeting,8 and five separate comment pieces from 
stakeholders at the August 19, 2026 Resource Adequacy Subcommittee meeting,9 further 
constraining the availability of interested parties to craft responsive comments for submission to 
the Commission.  Finally, MISO has proposed an effective date of October 18, 2026, so an 
extension of seven days to September 15, 2026 would still give MISO and other parties adequate 
opportunity to respond to the first round of comments.     
 
II. 
REQUEST FOR ACTION BY SEPTEMBER 4, 2026 
Public Interest Organizations respectfully request that the Commission act on this request 
for extension of time by September 4, 2026.  Action by that date is important so that Public 
Interest Organizations can best structure their comments to MISO’s filing and provide as detailed 
and thorough a comment filing as possible.

On June 18, 2026 the Commission issued its Order Instituting 
Proceeding Under Section 206 of the Federal Power Act (“Show Cause 
Order”),1 requiring each Participating Transmission Owner (“PTO”) within 
the California Independent System Operator’s (“CAISO”) system to address 
issues associated with serving large load and co-location arrangements.  In 
this filing, pursuant to Section 205 of the Federal Power Act, Citizens S-Line 
Transmission LLC (“Citizens S-Line”) files proposed revisions to its 
Transmission Owner Tariff (“TO Tariff”) to address the issues raised in the 
Show Cause Order.  Citizens S-Line respectfully requests that the 
Commission accept these TO Tariff revisions to become effective January 15, 
2027, which is sixty (60) days from the date of this filing. 
 
 
I. 
BACKGROUND ON CITIZENS S-LINE 
 
Citizens S-Line is a wholly owned subsidiary of Citizens Energy 
Corporation, a non-profit organization dedicated to making life’s basic needs 
more affordable through successful business ventures.  To date, Citizens

In 2024 Citizens S-Line invested approximately $40 million with the

its involvement in the S-Line Project will generate approximately $18 million 
in charitable assistance over the term of the lease. 
 
 
II. 
RESPONSE TO SHOW CAUSE ORDER 
 
 
The Commission’s Show Cause Order determined that the TO Tariffs 
of the CAISO PTOs, including Citizens S-Line, “lack provisions that address, 
with sufficient clarity and consistency” how each PTO “will timely study…the 
provision of transmission service to Eligible Customers on behalf of large 
loads.”4  The Commission directed each PTO to either justify why its TO 
Tariff remained just and reasonable, or to provide TO Tariff revisions 
necessary to remedy the Commission’s concerns.

As discussed in more detail below, Citizens S-Line has a limited 
leasehold interest in an Entitlement to incremental transfer capacity 
associated with a single upgrade project on the S-Line.   Ownership of the 
transmission line, as well as responsibility for operating and maintaining the 
line, remains with IID.  For these reasons, Citizens S-Line proposes revisions 
to its TO Tariff to make clear that any study, planning, interconnection or 
expansion requests related to Citizens S-Line’s interest in the S-Line Project 
will be governed by the relevant provisions and policies of IID, including its 
Open Access Transmission Tariff. 
 
Citizens S-Line additionally proposes revisions to its TO Tariff to make 
it consistent with the TO Tariffs of Citizens Sunrise Transmission LLC and 
Citizens Sycamore-Penasquitos Transmission LLC, two other CAISO PTOs 
that are also wholly owned subsidiaries of Citizens Energy Corporation.  The 
S-Line transmission line is located within IID’s Balancing Authority Area 
and is therefore not within the footprint of the CAISO’s Balancing Authority 
Area, nor is IID a CAISO PTO.  As a result, Citizens S-Line’s interest in the 
Project is limited to an Entitlements interest in certain transmission 
capacity, and it’s initial TO Tariff included more limited provisions than 
those of Citizens Sunrise Transmission LLC or Citizens Sycamore-
Penasquitos Transmission LLC, two other CAISO PTOs that are also wholly 
owned subsidiaries of Citizens Energy Corporation.  In particular, Citizens S-
Line’s TO Tariff contained a more limited Section 3 (TO Definitions), Section 
8 (Obligations to Interconnect or Construct Transmission Expansions and 
Facility Upgrades), Section 9 (Expansion Process), and Section 10 
(Interconnection Process). 
 
In response to the Show Cause Order, Citizens S-Line is proposing 
revisions that would expand these sections so that they mirror those of the 
other Citizens Energy Corporation PTOs, which Citizens S-Line believes will 
allow for more clarity on the provision of service to any large load or co-
located arrangement. These revisions include the following: 
 
• Revisions to Section 3, (TO Definitions) to expand the definition 
section, including, but not limited to adding definitions for Expedited 
Interconnection Agreement, Facilities Study Agreement, Facility or 
Facilities Study, Interconnection, Interconnection Agreement, 
Interconnection Application, Reliability Upgrade, Requests for 
Expedited Interconnection Procedures, System Impact Study, and

• Citizens S-Line additionally makes a series of administrative revisions

filing. 
 
Each of these revisions is shown in redline in Attachment B to this filing, 
which is a redline of the Citizens S-Line TO Tariff.  A clean version of the 
revised TO Tariff is included as Attachment A. 
 
Citizens S-Line respectfully submits that these revisions address the 
concerns raised in the Show Cause Order within the bounds of Citizens S-
Line’s limited interest in, and control over, the S-Line Project.  Put simply, 
Citizens S-Line is bound by the terms of its Incremental Transfer Capability 
Lease with IID, and that agreement makes it clear that IID, as the owner 
and operator of the S-Line Project, has the authority and responsibility for all 
operational and interconnection related matters. 
 
 
To the extent that the CAISO proposes TO Tariff revisions that impact 
the revisions Citizens S-Line proposes in this filing, Citizens S-Line commits 
to making a compliance filing to ensure its TO Tariff remains consistent.

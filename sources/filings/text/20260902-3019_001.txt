NOTICE DENYING EXTENSION OF TIME

(September 2, 2026)

On August 28, 2026, pursuant to Rules 212 and 2008 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure,[fn:2] the Organization of PJM States, Inc. (OPSI) filed a motion requesting an extension of time, from September 3, 2026, to September 14, 2026, to submit comments in response to the tariff filing submitted by PJM Interconnection, L.L.C. (PJM) in the above-referenced proceeding.  OPSI asserts that the extension of time will allow the retail regulators in the PJM region additional time to consider the interaction between the rights and responsibilities of PJM, Electric Distributors, and retail regulators related to the implementation of Interim Resource Adequacy Service and the associated compensation mechanism.  On August 31, 2026, Google LLC, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission, filed answers in support of OPSI’s motion.  On September 1, 2026 East Kentucky Power Cooperative, Inc. also filed an answer in support of OPSI’s motion.

Upon due consideration, notice is hereby given that OPSI’s motion for an extension of time is denied.  The deadline to submit interventions, protests, and comments in the above-referenced proceeding remains 5pm ET September 3, 2026.






Carlos D. Clay,
Deputy Secretary.

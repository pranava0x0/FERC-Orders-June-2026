ANSWER OF GOOGLE LLC 
 
Pursuant to Rule 213 of the Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (“Commission”),1 Google LLC (Google) respectfully submits answer in 
support of the Organization of PJM States, Inc.’s motion for an extension of time in which to 
respond to PJM Interconnection L.L.C.’s (“PJM”) filing in the above-captioned proceeding.  
Simply put, this is a proceeding motivated by and largely about retail customers and how they are 
regulated—matters over which the Federal Power Act reserves exclusive jurisdiction to the States. 2  
For that reason, it is absolutely vital that the States have the time they need to formulate a fulsome 
response to PJM’s proposal.  The Commission cannot issue a well-reasoned decision without that 
input.  Accordingly, Google urges the Commission to grant the request for an extension.

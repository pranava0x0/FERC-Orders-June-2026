ANSWER OF NORTHERN VIRGINIA ELECTRIC COOPERATIVE, INC.  
IN SUPPORT OF PJM INTERCONNECTION, L.L.C.’S MOTION FOR ABEYANCE 
Pursuant to Rule 213 of the Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Federal Energy 
Regulatory Commission (“Commission”),1 Northern Virginia Electric Cooperative, Inc. 
(“NOVEC”) respectfully submits this answer in support of PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.’s 
(“PJM”) motion for abeyance of the Reliability Backstop Procurement (“RBP”) paper hearing 
procedures to allow PJM to revise its RBP Proposal2 (the “Abeyance Motion”).3 For the reasons 
set forth herein and in the NOVEC Protest,4 the Commission should accept PJM’s Abeyance 
Motion. 
I. 
THE COMMISSION SHOULD GRANT PJM’S ABEYANCE MOTION 
 
PJM advised the Commission that it plans “to submit a new FPA section 205 filing that 
addresses, inter alia, cost allocation issues, Transmission Owner exit responsibilities, and LSE 
collateral requirements as they relate to RBP by October 29, 2026.”5 The Commission invited 
such a filing in its September 29, 2026, Order Accepting Reliability Backstop Procurement and 
Establishing Further Procedures and indicated that it would be open to holding the paper

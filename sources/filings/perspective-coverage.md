A state’s legal office, utilities commission, legislative delegation and electric cooperatives are not interchangeable.

**Verification.** Check each named speaker and the physical PDF page, order paragraph, or recording timestamp in the record’s locator. DOCX originals are not paginated; section names identify the passage. Sources include signed filings, orders summarizing party comments, first-hand interviews and original meeting records. A URL that returns an eLibrary application shell establishes reachability, not retrieval of the cited PDF. In a November 10 HTTP check of the 75 sources, 70 returned 200/206, four FERC media landing pages blocked automated access (403), and the CAISO CEBA file-list URL returned 500. The corresponding published June orders and CEBA’s August 7 answer are archived locally. Separate NJBPU and PUCO PDFs could not be confirmed from those offices in this pass; entries citing FERC’s account say so. OPSI’s signed copies came from its [filing directory](https://opsi.us/resolutions-and-filings/).
